  1. What makes an effective citizen? Discuss the sense of responsibility, empathy and patriotism.
    Topic: Effective Citizenship and the Constitution

    Three things make effective citizenship. The first is a sense of responsibility, which is an internal feeling about fulfilling one's responsibility. The second is empathy, which means feeling the pains of others and assessing how they are facing the challenges in their life. The third is patriotism, which means loving your country and trying to make it better in every matter.

  2. Write four duties of a citizen as per the Constitution of Pakistan.
    Topic: Effective Citizenship and the Constitution

    Four duties of a citizen are: (1) Loyalty to the state and obedience to the Constitution — Article 5 makes it binding on every citizen to be loyal to the state and to follow the Constitution in every aspect of life. (2) Being law-abiding, because laws are made for the safety of the citizens and ensure equal protection for all. (3) Respecting the rights of fellow citizens, such as their life, liberty, property and religious freedom. (4) Fulfilling trust and commitments, because the entire life of a society revolves around trust, and trust is the result of fulfilling commitments.

  3. Write two points about the Constitution of Pakistan.
    Topic: Effective Citizenship and the Constitution

    First, the Constitution is binding on every citizen: Article 5 makes loyalty to the state and obedience to the Constitution obligatory, and the state institutions are also legally bound to follow it. Second, the Constitution guarantees the fundamental rights of citizens, such as the right to life and liberty (Article 9), freedom of movement (Article 15), religious freedom (Articles 20, 21, 22) and protection of language and culture (Article 28). Territories, objectives and laws can be changed only within the limits of the Constitution.

  4. How does the Constitution of Pakistan protect the non-Muslims living and studying in Pakistan?
    Topic: Effective Citizenship and the Constitution

    The Constitution protects non-Muslims in several ways. Articles 20, 21 and 22 ensure religious freedom: every citizen has the right to choose his religion, preach it and establish religious institutions, and nobody is bound to follow any religion other than his own. Admission cannot be denied in educational institutions on the basis of religious background, so non-Muslim students have equal educational opportunities. Article 26 protects all citizens from discrimination based on religion, language, sex or birthplace, and Article 28 protects the right of every group to promote its distinct language and culture.

  5. Explain the rights of a citizen regarding a fair trial and freedom of movement.
    Topic: Effective Citizenship and the Constitution

    Fair trial: Every person is entitled to a fair trial in which judges make the decision and no institution interferes in the process. If a person cannot afford a fair trial, the Constitution ensures that he will be compensated so that he can have one, and there cannot be a media trial before the final judgment. Freedom of movement: According to Article 15, all citizens have the right to move freely and settle in any area of Pakistan unless it is a prohibited area. All areas of all cities are open to citizens, except private houses and institutions where permission is required to enter. The idea is to bring the Pakistanis together.

  6. Poverty is a major challenge before an effective citizen. Explain it and suggest how it can be dealt with.
    Topic: Challenges and Attributes of an Effective Citizen

    Poverty can be the biggest problem before an effective citizen. It creates continuous tension and depression for the man suffering from it, and it affects his behavior and attitude. To deal with it, we should look for the people suffering from poverty among our relatives and take practical steps to help them, making them feel that it is a temporary issue and that they can come out of it. When we alleviate poverty, it brings benefits like happiness, less tension and creativity.

  7. Discuss injustice as a challenge before an effective citizen. What does Surah An-Nisa (verse 135) command regarding justice?
    Topic: Challenges and Attributes of an Effective Citizen

    Injustice exists in our society at many levels. Judicial injustice means it takes years for the courts to decide cases. Economic injustice means there is a great difference between the salaries of the private and public sectors and people are unable to afford their basic needs. Social injustice means that in a family some people are preferred over others and are not treated equally. Society cannot flourish unless injustice is removed from it. Surah An-Nisa (135) commands the believers to stand firm for justice and bear true testimony, even if it goes against themselves or their relatives.

  8. Why is truthfulness an important trait of an effective citizen? Give two logical reasons.
    Topic: Challenges and Attributes of an Effective Citizen

    First, when a person is truthful, people trust him; trust is the basis of social interaction, and a society whose citizens are truthful progresses. Second, if the state institutions do not have truthfulness, the citizens lose their trust in them because they know these institutions cannot provide them with justice and rights. Therefore, we must first be truthful ourselves and then make this virtue common among the people around us.

  9. Write two points about flexibility as an attribute of an effective citizen.
    Topic: Challenges and Attributes of an Effective Citizen

    First, flexibility means adaptability in behavior, which attracts others towards you and leads to strong interpersonal relationships, ultimately creating an environment in which everybody is willing to help each other out; in contrast, strictness and reluctance to compromise cultivate a narrow one-man world. Second, being flexible means accepting and realizing that there is a possibility of making a mistake, which drives self-correction and also helps in correcting others, and as a result progress is made in society.

  10. How can an individual avoid self-praise and focus on genuine development?
    Topic: Challenges and Attributes of an Effective Citizen

    Self-praise is a weakness: beyond a limit it is a deceit, because it leads to dishonesty and a false sense of superiority, creates arrogance, makes a person vulnerable to manipulation, and stops improvement because he only listens to praise and not to his flaws. An individual can avoid it by practicing self-criticism — finding the shortcomings and negative habits of his own personality and improving them — which creates real confidence and genuine development. He should welcome real feedback instead of praise, especially leaders, whose wrong decisions based on praise can harm society's progress.

  11. Write a short note on egotism and on the educated (learning) citizen.
    Topic: Challenges and Attributes of an Effective Citizen

    Egotism means focusing solely on oneself. An egotist is not realistic; he seeks validation through self-praise, cannot see beyond his nose, cannot see good in others, and lives a deceitful life thinking the world revolves around him. This distances him from genuine connections and feedback, and he cannot become an effective citizen. In contrast, learning is a habit of the educated citizen: he is trained to enjoy learning, new learning creates joy and improves his personality, and when he learns he tries to teach others, which generates self-motivation, new ideas and opportunities for growth in society. Without learning, we cannot develop the country or become effective citizens.

  12. Discuss humility and its role in building social relationships.
    Topic: Challenges and Attributes of an Effective Citizen

    Humility is a vital character of an effective citizen and is the opposite of pride and arrogance, the traits that prevent us from developing ourselves by reflecting on and accepting our own mistakes. Humility helps in learning, and it provides the essential basis of building social relationships: practicing respect, kindness and compassion makes our relationships with others stronger. Recognizing our own limitations is very important for developing humility, as well as recognizing the different qualities and strengths that others have.

  13. Why is honoring commitments essential for nation-building?
    Topic: Challenges and Attributes of an Effective Citizen

    Without confidence in commitments getting honored, no nation can be built. Making and keeping promises is the fundamental thing in nation-building; it is impossible to lead a happy life, accomplish something or progress if there is no guarantee that commitments will be upheld. Countries where a high proportion of citizens fail to fulfill their commitments create an atmosphere of mistrust, which slows down progress and paves the way for negativity to grow. Every commitment is important, and if a commitment is betrayed, one should confess the failure, show sincere remorse and decide firmly to honor future commitments.

  14. Which strategies can individuals implement to promote honesty in organizational communication?
    Topic: Traits to Avoid and Saying No

    Individuals can promote honesty in organizational communication in several ways. First, they should be truthful themselves, because communication based on truth cultivates respect and trust between individuals. Second, they should avoid exaggeration, since exaggeration is a lie that eliminates honesty and hinders genuine communication; in a culture of exaggeration, a genuine message never creates impact. Third, they should speak clearly and simply, avoiding jargon and offensive language, and set aside personal preferences so that other views are heard honestly. Finally, they should discourage exaggerated or false information so that integrity, fairness and the importance of truth are preserved in the organization.

  15. Explain corruption and its two forms. How can corruption be overcome?
    Topic: Traits to Avoid and Saying No

    Corruption has two forms. Financial corruption involves monetary gains, while non-financial corruption involves rewarding individuals without merit. Corruption can be overcome when individuals hold themselves accountable for their actions. Good company also plays a role in combating corruption, and by having faith in religion and believing in accountability to a higher power (Allah), we can maintain our moral compass and avoid engaging in corrupt practices.

  16. What is favoritism? Discuss its consequences and the Quranic guidance about justice.
    Topic: Traits to Avoid and Saying No

    Favoritism means prioritizing personal relationships over others' needs. It is a form of corruption and injustice because it undermines justice and equal opportunity. It can be discouraged by being aware of it and seeking guidance from Allah. The Quran repeatedly emphasizes justice: in Surah An-Nisa (135), Allah says to be just even if it goes against yourself or your relatives, and in Surah Al-Maidah (8), Allah says to bear true testimony and be just, as it is closer to righteousness.

  17. Why should an effective citizen say no to bribery? How can bribery be reduced?
    Topic: Traits to Avoid and Saying No

    An effective citizen should not take bribes because bribery is a form of favoritism that compromises justice. Bribery is an addictive and contagious disease which leads to suffering and spreads throughout society like a plague. Bribery can be reduced when people understand its negative consequences, when public officials who make important decisions act with impartiality and justice, and when individuals are held accountable for bribery, which deters corruption.

  18. Write the benefits of traffic laws.
    Topic: Traffic Laws and Road Ethics

    Traffic laws serve as a fundamental framework for creating safer, more efficient and more orderly roadways. Their benefits include: (1) they play a crucial role in accident prevention by establishing rules and standards that promote safe driving behavior; (2) they protect lives and property; (3) they create a framework for orderly and efficient movement of vehicles, making traffic safer and more predictable for all road users; (4) they reduce congestion and promote orderly driving, which enhances the overall efficiency of travel and of the transportation system.

  19. Explain the importance of seat belts, indicators and traffic lights for road safety.
    Topic: Traffic Laws and Road Ethics

    Seat belts: Fastening seat belts properly is a simple yet highly effective way to protect oneself and others; seat belts save lives by reducing the risk and severity of injuries in the event of a crash. Indicators: Using indicators judiciously is essential for effective communication on the road; their consistent and deliberate use enhances road awareness and contributes to smoother, safer and more courteous interactions among road users. Traffic lights: Abiding by traffic lights is fundamental to maintaining order, promoting safety and ensuring efficient traffic management; they control traffic movements, reduce conflicts at intersections, and reduce the risk of accidents, creating a more harmonious and predictable road environment.

  20. What is the right attitude in case of a minor accident and a major accident?
    Topic: Traffic Laws and Road Ethics

    Minor accident: Stay calm, ensure everyone's safety and be respectful; either ignore it or park the car on the side of the road, speak the truth calmly, turn on hazard lights, communicate politely with the other driver, exchange contact numbers and follow up to ensure the situation is resolved properly. Major accident: Taking care of the injured is the top priority and truth should be spoken; prioritize safety by checking for injuries, move to a safe location if possible, and call emergency services immediately, providing accurate information about the location and extent of the accident.

  21. Why is using a mobile phone while driving dangerous? Also state the proper use of the horn.
    Topic: Traffic Laws and Road Ethics

    Using a mobile phone while driving is dangerous because it distracts the driver, reducing his attention and reaction time. It increases the likelihood of accidents by diverting focus from the road; handling a phone requires taking hands off the wheel, which compromises vehicle control, and looking at the screen causes visual distraction. Overall, mobile phone use significantly impairs driving performance and endangers everyone on the road. As for the horn, it is a tool for safety, not for communicating anger or annoyance; it should be used sparingly and responsibly, only where necessary for safety, because using it to intimidate can escalate road rage and startle others, potentially leading to accidents.

  22. Discuss the responsibilities of an effective citizen.
    Topic: The Effective Citizen in Daily Life

    An effective citizen has responsibilities at many levels. He is loyal to the state and obedient to the Constitution (Article 5), and he is law-abiding because laws ensure the safety and peace of the country and equal protection for all. He respects the rights of other citizens and fulfills the trust and commitments placed in him. He shows concern for others, which is the foundation of most good behavior, for example by following traffic rules, making queues and holding doors open for others. He also takes care of the environment by keeping it clean and not throwing garbage in public places, manages his time well, encourages virtue and discourages evil, and remains truthful, humble and helpful in his dealings.

  23. How can an effective citizen take care of the environment?
    Topic: The Effective Citizen in Daily Life

    We are responsible for keeping our environment clean and healthy for present and future generations. An effective citizen reduces waste, recycles materials and conserves natural resources, and adopts sustainable practices like reducing energy consumption and minimizing pollution. He does not throw garbage outside his home, car or in public spaces, because littering harms wildlife, pollutes ecosystems and degrades the beauty of the surroundings. He also picks up filth from floors and roads, disposes of waste in designated bins, and participates in community clean-up efforts, contributing to a cleaner and more sustainable planet.

  24. Discuss the importance of managing time for an effective citizen.
    Topic: The Effective Citizen in Daily Life

    Time is a limited and precious resource, so managing it effectively is crucial. Proper time management enables us to prioritize tasks, set goals and achieve them efficiently, leading to increased productivity and reduced stress. By allocating time wisely, we can balance work, personal life and leisure, meet deadlines, seize opportunities and avoid procrastination. We should not waste time in unnecessary gossip, so that we can focus on meaningful tasks. An effective citizen also shows time commitment in reaching a place on time and in starting and finishing programs on time, which shows professionalism, reliability and respect for others.

  25. What is true patriotism? How does patriotism help a country progress?
    Topic: The Effective Citizen in Daily Life

    Patriotism means loving your country and having the urge to make it progress. It is not blindly supporting the country's leadership regardless of their actions; true patriotism means holding the country and its leaders accountable, and speaking out against injustice, corruption or wrongdoing. A citizen makes his country progress in four ways: by doing well and striving for excellence in his own work, by helping others through volunteer work and community service, by following the laws, which maintains social order, stability and justice, and by speaking the truth, which fosters transparency, accountability and trust in institutions.

  26. Why does evil spread in society, and how should it be discouraged?
    Topic: The Effective Citizen in Daily Life

    Evil spreads if it is not discouraged, because silence or inaction can be interpreted as acceptance or approval, allowing wrongdoing to proliferate and take root. It spreads in society when individuals condone or engage in harmful actions, and ignorance, apathy and indifference allow it to flourish unchecked. Evil also finds a sympathetic place in hearts when fear, insecurity or resentment cloud judgment and morality. It should be discouraged appropriately: the response should be proportionate, with action and tone matching the severity of the wrongdoing — firm but respectful — and delivered at the appropriate time, so that accountability is promoted and further wrongdoing is deterred.

  27. Discuss the rules of proper listening and proper speaking in communication.
    Topic: Communication, Sharing and Honesty

    Listening properly: Communication is twofold, and listening is as important as talking — listening is half of communicating. We should listen like we want to be heard. Active listening requires listening attentively, both verbally and non-verbally, and absorbing what the other person is saying rather than thinking of our response while listening; this helps us decode the message effectively, avoid misinterpretation and respond appropriately. Speaking properly: We should use simple words and avoid complex phrases, jargon and technical terminology, and speak with pauses. We should speak for understanding, not display, use language appropriate to the audience, and cultivate polite language, because communication ceases the moment one starts using offensive language.

  28. Explain honesty and its different forms discussed in the handouts.
    Topic: Communication, Sharing and Honesty

    Honesty has several forms. (1) Taking interest in what you do: genuinely engaging with your tasks is honesty towards yourself and others; it demonstrates integrity, increases the quality of work and fosters a sense of purpose and fulfillment. (2) Choosing to do what interests you: aligning your actions with your passions and values, rather than conforming to external expectations, is a subtle form of honesty that requires introspection and self-awareness and leads to authenticity and success. (3) Seriousness in what you do: approaching tasks with diligence, focus and dedication reflects a commitment to excellence and integrity; it shows accountability and respect for the impact of your actions, and builds trust and credibility among peers and community.
