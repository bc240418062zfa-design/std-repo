﻿Assignment no.1 BT612p

1. How many classes are there of media based on consistency and also write down the composition of YEPD medium. (10)
Media Based Classes on Consistency;
There are three classes of media which are based on consistency as under:
1. Solid Media
2.Liquid Media
3.Semi-Solid Media
*Solid Media;
It contains a specific solidifying agent.For example agar at 1.5 to 2% in concentration. It is used in isolate the colonies and study its morphology.For example, Nutrient agar.
*Liquid Media;
This media does not contain any of solidifying agent.It is normally used in the growing of huge amounts of fungi or bacteria.For example as nutrient broth.
*Semi-solid Media;
It is contain of a lower concentration of agar which is 0.4 to 0.7%.It is used to test the motility or the anaerobic growth of bacteria.Its example is Sulfide-Indole-Motility medium.
Composition of YEPD Medium;

The  abbreviation of YEPD is Yeast Extract Peptone Dextrose,It is that medium which is mostly used to culture the fungi and Yeast.Its composition is as under:
Component
Amount per liter
Yeast Extract
10 g
Peptone
20 g
Dextrose (Glucose)
20 g
Agar (for solid medium)
15-20 g (optional)
Distilled Water
1 liter•
 For liquid YEPD, omit the agar.
Hence this medium is clearly sterilized through an autoclaving at 121°C for 15 to 20 minutes only.
Usage of YEPD Medium;
It is a best ideal for supporting extreme good growth in the yeast species such as Saccharomyces cerevisiae and mainly fungi.

2. What factors are important for the Development of Conidial Inoculum for Fungal Fermentation. (5)
These are most Important factors for the Development of Conidial Inoculum for fermentation of fungi as under;

1.Environmental Conditions;
In this environmental condition the fungi mainly require exact temperatures for sporulation which is from 25–30°C.Hence it maintain an maximum pH range which is usually from 5 to 7.It also bother the effective conidial production.Furthermore the best moisture and oxygen available which are critical for formation of conidia.
2.Fungal Strain Selection;
The fungal strain should be high productive and best to the desirable fermentation process.So the selection of strain which has an good effect on yield,growth and the metabolites production.
3.Growth Medium Nutrients;
This growth medium must provide all necessary and essential nutrients which include nitrogen,carbon and minerals to help the development of extreme and viable conidia.For example the use of Czapek-Dox agar and PDA for growth of conidial.
4.Storage plus harvesting of Conidia;
Here conidia should be harvested at their upto mark maturation for optimal visibility.So the exact storage conditions mainly are the freezing or desiccation,which are required to maintain high quality of the inoculum for successful fermentation process of fungi.
5.Sterilization;
The contamination free procedure during the preparation of  inoculum are necessary  to prevent unbearable growth of microbes.In this process whole of the equipment, inoculum and media should be sterilized.

4. What are the basic constitutes of a media for microbial growth? (5)
Basic Constituents of a Media for Microbial Growth
1.Nitrogen;
It is an essential for proteins synthesis,nucleic acid and other components of cells.For examples such as yeast,peptone,yeast extract,nitrate or the ammonium salts.
2.Minerals;
This constitute is more essential for the functions of enzymes and regulate cells integrity.So the main minerals are magnesium,calcium,potassium and trace elements like iron etc.
3.Carbon;
The carbon provides structural components for structure as well as nutrients for the growth of microbes.Its main examples are sucrose, glucose or other carbohydrate.

4.Growth;
The main organic compounds are the amino acids or vitamins which are essential for the growth of the fastidious organisms.Its best examples are thiamine,biotin or hemin as well.
5.Water;
It also acts as a good solvent for the nutrients and which help the reaction of  biochemicals.The pure,deionized or the distilled water is mainly used because it is a best for solutions.
Other Components;
Some other components also provide best environment or media for the growth of microbes are as under;
Solidifying agents,pH buffers and selective agents.
So these components provide the best and exact nutrients and environment required for the growth of microbes.
