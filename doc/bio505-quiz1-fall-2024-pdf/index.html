1. Variation is a difference that can be seen in individuals of the same species.
  Which one of the following statements is correct?
  It can be due to influence of genetic makeup, environment factors or by
  combination of both
2. The nucleosome is composed of a core of __________ histone proteins and
  DNA __________ bp.
  Eight, 147
3. The DNA between nucleosomes is called linker DNA. Its length is
  __________.
  20-80 bp
4. In life forms with the ZZ-ZW chromosomal sex determination, the males have
  __________ chromosomes.
  ZZ chromosomes
5. In life forms with the ZZ-ZW chromosomal sex determination, the males are
  __________.
  Heterogametic
6. The diameter of chromatin is __________.
  30 nm

7. Offspring of the same variety is termed as __________.
  Line breed
8. The type of inheritance that appears in the AB blood group phenotype is
  __________.
  Co-dominance
9. The molecular weight of the histone protein H4 is __________.
  13,960
10.     How many types of chromosomal banding are present?
  4
11.     In the parental generation, when Mendel crossed a true-breeding pea
  strain that produced smooth seeds with one that produced wrinkled seeds, the
  result was __________.
  All the F1 progeny seeds were smooth
12.     Offspring of the same variety is termed as __________.
  True breed
13.     Variations that are entirely genetically controlled are __________.
  Discontinuous
14.     __________ is an individual's observable traits, such as height, eye
  color, and blood type.
  Phenotype
15.     Band that indicates constitutive heterochromatin is __________.
  C band
16.     __________ is an example of inheritance that does not follow the
  principles of heredity formulated by Gregor Mendel.
  Epigenetics

17.    Variations that result from __________ are not acted upon by natural
  selection and thus do not affect evolution.
  Acquired characteristics
