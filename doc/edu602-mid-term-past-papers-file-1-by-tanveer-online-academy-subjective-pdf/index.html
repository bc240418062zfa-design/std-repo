Question: What is the difference between task-oriented and people-oriented leadership?
Answer: Task-oriented leadership focuses on achieving goals and completing tasks
efficiently, while people-oriented leadership emphasizes building relationships and
promoting the well-being of team members.

Question: What is the difference between reversible and irreversible?
Answer: Reversible refers to a situation or decision that can be undone or changed, while
irreversible refers to an action or decision that cannot be undone.

Question: What is the difference between trait and behavioral theory?
Answer: Trait theory focuses on identifying specific qualities or traits that make a leader,
whereas behavioral theory emphasizes actions and behaviors that define effective leadership.

Question: What are the 4 Is in transformational leadership?
Answer: The 4 Is in transformational leadership are Idealized Influence, Inspirational
Motivation, Intellectual Stimulation, and Individualized Consideration.

Question: What is a contingency plan?
Answer: A contingency plan is a predefined course of action to be taken in response to
unexpected events or emergencies, ensuring an organization’s ability to adapt to changes.

Question: What is transparent leadership?
Answer: Transparent leadership is characterized by openness, honesty, and clear
communication, ensuring trust and clarity in decision-making processes.

Question: What are the management components?
Answer: The key components of management include planning, organizing, leading, and
controlling to achieve organizational goals.

Question: What are the characteristics of a modern school?
Answer: Modern schools focus on collaborative learning, use of technology, student-
centered teaching, a diverse curriculum, and holistic development of students.

Question: What is a strategic plan?
Answer: A strategic plan is a long-term blueprint that outlines an organization’s goals,
strategies, and resources to achieve success over a defined period.

Question: What is the disadvantage of autocratic leadership?
Answer: Autocratic leadership can lead to low morale, limited creativity, and reduced
employee engagement as decisions are made unilaterally.

Question: What are the types of organizational structure?
Answer: The main types of organizational structure include functional, divisional, matrix,
flat, and hierarchical structures.

Question: What is the society and nature of man concept?
Answer: The concept explores how society shapes human behavior, emphasizing that human
nature is influenced by societal norms, values, and systems.

Question: What are Adler’s objectives?
Answer: Adler’s objectives focus on individual psychology, emphasizing personal growth
and self-awareness in social contexts.

Question: What are disagreements and how can they be overcome?
Answer: Disagreements can be overcome by fostering open communication, active listening,
collaboration, and finding mutually beneficial solutions.

Question: What are the differences between a leader and a manager?
Answer: A leader focuses on vision, motivation, and change, while a manager focuses on
processes, organization, and efficiency.

Question: What is the focus of transformational leadership?
Answer: Transformational leadership focuses on inspiring and motivating followers to
exceed their own self-interest for the benefit of the group.

Question: What are the three leadership styles according to Lewin’s studies?
Answer: Lewin identified three leadership styles: autocratic, democratic, and laissez-faire.

Question: What are the challenges of the contingency approach?
Answer: The contingency approach is challenging due to the complexity of accurately
assessing different situations and determining the appropriate leadership style.

Question: What are the main components of transactional leadership?
Answer: The main components of transactional leadership include contingent rewards,
management by exception, and laissez-faire leadership.

Question: Why is frequent monitoring of students' progress important in school?
Answer: Frequent monitoring helps identify academic issues early, allowing for timely
interventions and ensuring students meet learning objectives.

Question: What are the characteristics of a strategic plan?
Answer: A strategic plan is long-term, has clear objectives, and is flexible to adapt to
changes in the environment.

Question: What are the characteristics of transformational leadership?
Answer: Transformational leadership is characterized by inspiring and motivating others,
fostering innovation, and promoting a positive vision for the future.

Question: What is the Neo-classical theory?
Answer: Neo-classical theory emphasizes human relations and social factors, focusing on
employee satisfaction and motivation as key elements in organizational productivity.

Question: What are the types of organizational structure?
Answer: Organizational structures include functional, divisional, matrix, team-based, and flat
structures.
