                                                           Lecture: 01

Business Communication - The communication which takes place at the workplace, in the educational setting,
corporate office, or in a government job.
Business English - an umbrella term for a mixture of general everyday English, general Business English, and ESP.

Two components of a Business English course

    •        Language knowledge - reflect the formal aspects of grammar, vocabulary and sound system
    •        Communication skills - in which we activate this knowledge to transmit messages through different channels

General and Business English

    •        Objectives of both are the same
    •        Context is different
             ▪ In general English, one learns the basics, the mechanics of grammar, composition and vocabulary.
             ▪ In business English you build upon what you learned in general English

Impact of Revolution in Linguistics
This led to the awareness of the following:

         •     The differences between written and spoken language.
         •     The difference between the language of commerce and the language of engineering.
         •     The language needs of different groups are different.
         •     Also that if language varies from one situation of use to another, it should be possible to determine the
               features of specific situations and then make these the basis of learner's courses.

Areas of management

    1.       Planning
    2.       Leading
    3.       Organizing
    4.       Controlling

Communication has been influenced by a variety of disciplines

    •        Linguistics
    •        General language
    •        E - Learning
    •        Teaching and management training

ESP Phases of development

    1) The Concept of Special Language: Register Analysis. In 1960s and early 1970s, the basic principle was that
             each field has its own particular register.

    2) Rhetorical or Discourse Analysis. In this phase, attention was given to language above the sentence level,
        how sentences were combined in discourse to produce meaning.
    3) Target Situation Analysis/Needs Analysis. In this phase the aim was to take existing knowledge by
        analyzing the target situation and then identifying the linguistic features of that target situation.
    4) Skills and Strategies. In this phase, the attempt was to look below the surface to consider the thinking
        process that underlies the language use.
    5) Learner-centered Approaches: emphasized the leaner and his needs at the center of a course design.

ESP Tree
The tree represents some of the common divisions made in ELT. The top most branches show the level at which
individual ESP courses lie. So, we have:

    •   English for Medical Studies
    •   English for Psychology
    •   English for Technicians
    •   English for Economics
    •   English for teaching

The lower level shows the two common branches of ELT

    •   Whether the learner wants English for Academic Purposes (EAP)
    •   Or for Vocational Purposes (EVP) also called EOP or VESL

The next level down shows the three common branches of ELT

    •   EST (English for Science and Technology)
    •   EBE (English for Business and Economics)
    •   ESS (English for Social Sciences)

As we go down the tree, we see that ESP is just one branch of ESL and EFL, which are themselves the two main
branches of English Teaching in general.

Distinguishing between Business Communication and other forms of language teaching
The analogy of the tree has helped to show us what BC teaching is not:

    1) Business communication teaching is not a matter of teaching a ‘specialized variety’ of language.
    2) Business communication is not just a matter of business words for business students.
    3) BC teaching is not different in kind from any other form of language teaching.

BC; An approach or product

BC has to be seen as an approach not a product since business communication is not the teaching of a particular
language nor does it consist of a particular type of methodology or material. It should be taken as an approach to
language learning which is based on the learner's need.
