Physical and chemical method of fixing the smear
Physical method of fixing a smear involves attaching the specimen to a slide and preserving
structures with heat. Chemical methods employ substances like ethanol or formaldehyde to fix
the smear, maintaining internal and external features.

Classes of microbe’s media base on their function:
(– General purpose – Selective – Enrichment – Differential) short ans.
Microbial media are classified by function into General purpose, enrichment media, supporting
the growth of specific microbes; selective media, favoring certain microbes; and differential
media, highlighting differences in microbial metabolism. These classes aid in cultivating and
identifying diverse microorganisms.

Names the bacteria which required oxygen:
Obligate aerobes: 1= Mycobacterium tuberculosis, 2= Nocardia species, 3= Pseudomonas
aeruginosa.

Write three characteristic of bacterial cell wall?
   1. Peptidoglycan Structure: Bacterial cell walls contain peptidoglycan, a polymer with
      alternating sugar chains, providing rigidity.
   2. Protection: The cell wall protects against osmotic stress.
   3. Shape Determination: It contributes to bacterial shape and maintains structural integrity.

Write mechanism of oxidative phosphorylation and photophosphrylation?
Oxidative phosphorylation occurs in the mitochondria, utilizing the electron transport chain to
pump protons across the inner membrane. The resulting proton gradient drives ATP synthesis via
ATP synthase. Photophosphorylation in photosynthetic organisms uses light energy to generate a
proton gradient, ultimately leading to ATP synthesis through photophosphorylation.

Write the name of chemical which prevent food spoilage?
Chemicals such as preservatives, including benzoic acid, sorbic acid, calcium propionate, and
sodium nitrite, are commonly used to prevent food spoilage by inhibiting the growth of spoilage-
causing microorganisms and extending the shelf life of products.

Difference between conjugation carried out in HFS cells and F*cells.
Conjugation in Hfr (high-frequency recombination) cells involves the integration of the entire
donor bacterial chromosome into the recipient, allowing for the transfer of chromosomal genes.
In contrast, F* cells result from incomplete chromosomal transfer in F+ cells, leading to partial
genetic exchange during conjugation.

Different layers in endospores:
   1. Exosporium: Outermost layer, composed of proteins and sometimes lipids.
   2. Spore Coat: Beneath the exosporium, a thick layer of proteins that provides structural
      support.
   3. Cortex: Composed of peptidoglycan, located beneath the spore coat.
   4. Core Wall: Another layer of peptidoglycan.
   5. Inner Membrane: Surrounds the spore's protoplast, which contains the genetic material.

Functions of ethylene oxide:
Ethylene oxide is used as a sterilizing agent in various industries, especially healthcare. Its
function is to eliminate bacteria, viruses, and other microorganisms from equipment and medical
devices, ensuring aseptic conditions for critical applications.

Define osmotic pressure, osmotic lysis, plasmolysis:
Osmotic pressure is the pressure exerted by a solvent when it moves across a semipermeable
membrane to equalize solute concentrations on both sides. Osmotic lysis occurs when a cell
swells and bursts due to water influx caused by lower external solute concentration. Plasmolysis
is the shrinkage of a cell's protoplast away from the cell wall due to water loss, usually resulting
from exposure to a hypertonic environment.

Name of body parts of microscope:
   1. Eyepiece (ocular): The lens closest to the eye.
   2. Objective lenses: Magnifying lenses closest to the specimen.
   3. Nosepiece (turret or revolver): Rotating device holding multiple objective lenses.
   4. Stage: Platform holding the specimen.
   5. Condenser: Focuses light onto the specimen.
   6. Illuminator: Light source.
   7. Diaphragm: Controls the amount of light.
   8. Coarse and fine focus knobs: Adjust focus.
   9. Arm: Supports the upper parts.
   10. Base: Provides stability.

Names three chemical disrupt bacterial wall:
1= Lysozyme, 2= Penicillin, 3= Tetracycline.

Specialized and generalized transduction:
Specialized transduction is a bacterial genetic transfer process where specific genes are
mistakenly packaged into a phage during lysogeny, leading to their transfer to another bacterium.
Generalized transduction, in contrast, occurs when any bacterial DNA is randomly packaged into
a phage, facilitating the transfer of diverse genetic material between bacteria.

Who small pox coined:
Edward Jenner coined the term "vaccination" in 1796 while developing the smallpox vaccine,
using cowpox to protect against smallpox.

Difference between oxygenic and non-oxygenic photoautotrphs.
Oxygenic photoautotrophs, like plants and cyanobacteria, use water as an electron donor during
photosynthesis, producing oxygen. Non-oxygenic photoautotrophs, such as purple and green

bacteria, employ alternative electron donors like hydrogen sulfide, without generating oxygen.
Both groups harness light energy to synthesize organic compounds.

Write characteristics of an ideal antibacterial Chemical:
An ideal antibacterial chemical is potent against a broad spectrum of bacteria, exhibits low
toxicity to host cells, has a high therapeutic index, minimal side effects, and prevents resistance
development. It should be stable, easily administered, and possess good pharmacokinetics to
ensure effective treatment while minimizing adverse impacts on the host.
