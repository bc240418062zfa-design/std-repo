Short Questions (3 Marks)
1. What is meant by counseling and why is Community Language Learning
based on counseling technique?

Counseling is a process in which a counselor helps clients solve problems and reduce anxiety. In
Community Language Learning (CLL), the teacher acts as a counselor and learners act as
clients. The method is based on counseling because it creates a supportive environment where
learners feel secure and confident in learning a new language.

2. Write any 3 problems students face while writing in English.

      Lack of vocabulary
      Grammatical errors
      Difficulty organizing ideas coherently

3. What do NLP language teachers do differently from other language teachers?

NLP teachers focus on learners' emotions, motivation, confidence, learning styles, and positive
mental states. They build rapport, encourage self-belief, and use communication techniques to
improve learning.

4. Third dimension of language ideology / Kroskrity's dimensions of language
ideology.

The third dimension states that members of a society show different levels of awareness about
language ideologies depending on their social roles and experiences.

5. Principles of Neuro-Linguistic Programming – any 3.

      Rapport
      Anchoring
      Modeling
       (Other acceptable answers: Outcome orientation, flexibility, sensory acuity)

6. Gouin's graduation process: complexity, regularity and productivity.

      Complexity: Language structures become more complex gradually.
      Regularity: Language follows systematic patterns.
      Productivity: Learners can create new sentences using learned patterns.

7. Explain authenticity in the Whole Language domain.

Authenticity means using real-life language materials such as newspapers, stories, letters,
magazines, and conversations rather than artificial textbook exercises.

8. Ivan Pavlov's conditioned response with reference to behaviorism.

Pavlov showed that dogs learned to salivate at the sound of a bell when it was repeatedly paired
with food. This learned reaction is called a conditioned response and became a foundation of
behaviorist learning theory.

9. Types of syllables.

      Open syllable
      Closed syllable
      Vowel-consonant-e syllable
      R-controlled syllable
      Consonant-le syllable
      Vowel team syllable

10. It is impossible to imagine human life without language.

Language is essential for communication, expression of thoughts, social interaction, learning,
and cultural transmission. Human life and society cannot function effectively without language.

Long Questions (5 Marks)
1. Write about ELT which is not according to need-based theory in Pakistan.

English Language Teaching (ELT) in Pakistan often ignores learners' actual needs. Teaching is
generally examination-oriented and focuses heavily on grammar and memorization. Students
need communicative competence for education and employment, but classroom practices rarely
address these practical requirements. Therefore, ELT does not fully follow need-based theory.

2. Write about Neuro-Linguistic Programming (NLP).

Neuro-Linguistic Programming (NLP) is a humanistic approach that studies how people think,
communicate, and learn. It emphasizes rapport building, positive attitudes, motivation, and
effective communication. NLP teachers help learners develop confidence and use successful
learning strategies. It focuses on the relationship between neurological processes, language, and
behavior.

3. Define technique with example.

A technique is a specific classroom activity used to implement a teaching method. It is the
practical application of a method.

Example: Role-play activity in Communicative Language Teaching (CLT) is a technique used
to improve speaking skills.

4. According to Natural Order Hypothesis, what language-centered pedagogical
criteria for order did researchers show?

The Natural Order Hypothesis states that grammatical structures are acquired in a predictable
sequence. Some language features are learned earlier while others are acquired later, regardless
of the teaching order. Therefore, instruction should follow learners' natural developmental stages.

5. Difference between Silent Way and Repetition (Audio-Lingual Method).

              Silent Way                Repetition / Audio-Lingual
Teacher remains mostly silent         Teacher talks frequently
Learner-centered                      Teacher-centered
Discovery learning                    Habit formation
Students solve problems independently Students repeat drills
Errors are learning opportunities     Errors are corrected immediately
