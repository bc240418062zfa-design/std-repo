Sources of DNA
Purified DNA is required for a variety of molecular biology applications. DNA can be purified from any
living organism and its living parts

Origin of Samples:
1. Human tissues i.e. histological samples, prenatal samples, postmortem harvesting
2. Blood, (EDTA)
3. Hair, (follicle part of the hair to be specific
4. Rodent tissues, as rats are the most common lab mammals used in labs
5. Leaf
6. Bacteria, Bacterial cultures
7. Yeast, yeast cultures
8. Fungi
9. Insect, i.e. Drosophila melanogaster
10. Stool
11. Body fluids, i.e. semen
12. Spores
13. Soil
14. Clinical samples (e.g. biopsy samples, fine needle aspirates)
15. Forensic samples (e.g. dried blood spots, buccal swabs finger prints)
