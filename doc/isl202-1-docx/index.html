﻿Isl202 assignment 1
Spring 2022
Our lives are a continuous succession of God’s gifts. Allah has made it clear that we will never be able to count all of His blessings upon us, since they are innumerable.
ALLAH Almighty says: “And if you were to count Allah’s favors, you would not be able to number them; most surely Allah is Forgiving, Merciful.
But here we’re going to discuss any four of them.
Milk:
We give you to drink of that which is in their (cattle’s) bellies, between the dung and the blood: pure milk, a pleasant beverage for those who drink it.(16:66)
Milk is great blessing from Allah for all of us. It is a pure liquid which is very nutritious and beneficial for our health. It gives us good bone health, strong immune system etc. According to Holy Prophet (SAW) Milk of cow can treat any type of pain and ache.
Parents:
 And lower to them the wing of humility out of mercy and say, “My Lord, have mercy upon them as they brought me up (when I was) small.
Parents are the most precious blessing of Allah. They are the luckiest people in this world who have their parents alive. Parents are like a pillar, a shade, the biggest support of our lives.  Our parents are like a shield which protects you not just in this world but also hereafter by the grace and will of Allah.
Waking up today and being alive:
And among His signs is your sleep by night and by day and your seeking of His bounty, verily in that are signs for those who hearken”
Today is a blessing that allows us to live in the present while enabling us to review our past and plan for a better tomorrow. Waking up to see another day is a blessing. Don’t take it for granted. Make it count and be happy that you are alive.
Water:
“And We have sent down blessed rain from the sky and made grow thereby gardens and grain from the harvest
In the Qur’an, Allah uses the analogy of life and death for Muslims to recognize the value of water. We have taps that fill our basins with gold, but we cannot afford overflows while people wait for the rains. Third-world countries in desert climates are counting on us to give the cost of our water to them.  rain is a blessing. We need it to fill lakes and streams, which provide sources of drinking water and crop irrigation
Weather patterns:
It is Allah who sends the winds, and they stir the clouds and spread them in the sky however He wills, and He makes them fragments so you see the rain emerge from within them. And when He causes it to fall upon whom He wills of His servants, immediately they rejoice.
Weather affects us in a huge number of ways. Climate influences the growth of crops, thus affecting the availability and kind of food we eat. Fluctuations in weather (e.g. dry spells, wet spells) also affect crops. Weather affects what clothes we wear, and soon.

Juma:
