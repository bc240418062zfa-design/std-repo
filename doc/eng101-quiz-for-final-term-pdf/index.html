                               Quiz for Final-Term

1. Queen is said ______ Australia next year. (to be visiting)
2. I hurt myself ______ I was playing tennis. (when)
3. The injured boy must receive medical attention at once ______ he will die. (or)
4. I shall be back in the twinkle of ______. (an eye)
5. Which of the following is an example of a fused sentence? (Tom read the novel
   his friend watched the movie.)
6. The children at the deaf and mute center can ______. (neither hear nor speak)
7. The man with all the birds on my street ______ there. (lives)
8. A sentence showing a condition-prediction relationship is preceded by ______.
   (if)
9. There is an exception ______ every rule. (to)
10. Amber could not see the board, ______ she got glasses. (so)
11. The closest meaning of the word ABANDON is ______. (give up
   voluntarily)
12. A sentence that has unity is considered a ______ sentence. (good)
13. "The wall is covered with ivy. A stone path leads to the wall." The above
   construction is a ______. (fragment)
14. What are the major qualities of a well-written paragraph? (All of the above)
15. Aimlessly shifting from one point of view to another destroys sentence
   ______. (coherence)
16. Which shift makes the following construction faulty? "The only words that
   we were able to distinguish are horse and cart." (Past to Present shift)
17. The closest meaning of "put off" is ______. (postpone)
18. Octopuses have not only large brains ______ have a well-developed nervous
   system. (but also)
19. Flowchart symbols are connected together by means of ______. (flow lines)
20. One way to correct fused sentences is to add ______. (a period and a capital
   letter)
21. We use the phrase ______ when we want to express 100% certainty. (without
   a doubt)
22. "Watching the movie, the hero's love seemed to be long-haired, ample-height,
   and beautiful." This is an example of a ______. (dangling modifier)
23. Misplacing modifiers may cause ______. (reader's misunderstanding)
24. Which of the following is an example of a comma splice? (She opened the
   window, I fell out.)
25. "I have to go. My mom says it's time for dinner." is the correct form of a
   ______. (run-on sentence)

26. No sooner did I enter the classroom ______ the boys stood up. (than)
27. I bought a book from a book fair that cost Rs. 400. The word "that" refers to
  the ______. (book)
28. "Tobias sat by the telephone for hours hoping it would call." begins with an
  ING phrase called ______. (ING fragment)
29. In the sentence "B. J. Novak, author of the best-written novel...", the value
  word is ______. (best)
30. "Hopefully John will give in to despair and abandon his hopeless project."
  "Hopefully" is a ______. (misplaced adverb)
31. My fingers were injured, so my sister had to write the note ______ me. (for)
32. Pakistani ______ met his counterpart in London. (consul)
33. It's been snowing ______ Christmas morning. (since)
34. "The wall is covered with ivy, a stone path leads to the wall." is a ______.
  (comma splice)
35. There are ______ elements that make sentences effective. (three)
36. "Ali worked hard for his exams." is a ______. (complete sentence)
37. Truth may perhaps come to the price of a pearl that shines best by day. "That"
  refers to ______. (price of a pearl)
38. Identify the grammatical error: "There has been surprisingly little research on
  anorexia nervosa. Only 15 comparative trials published in the last 20 years."
  (Sentence fragment)
39. The type of conjunction used in compound sentences is ______.
  (coordinating conjunction)
40. I don't agree with you, but your idea certainly gives me food for ______.
  (thought)
41. They love English weather, ______? (don't they)
42. The people whose names are on the list will go to the camp. This is a ______
  clause. (noun clause)
43. Due to ______ in foreign investment, the leather goods business is declining
  day by day. (reduction)
44. The service at this restaurant is excellent; ______, the food is delicious.
  (moreover)
45. A perpetual state of indolence will ______ you miserable. (make)
46. "We ate the lunch that they had brought slowly." contains a ______.
  (misplaced adverb)
47. What order is necessary while attempting to persuade a reader? (order of
  importance)
