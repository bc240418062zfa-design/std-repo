B) Write your observation of frog skin that you have notices during dissection of frog?

Observation of Frog Skin During Dissection

During the dissection of a frog, several key characteristics of its skin can be observed, which highlight its vital
role in respiration, protection, and osmoregulation:

   1. Texture and Appearance
         o The skin appears smooth, moist, and slightly slimy due to the secretion of mucus from
             specialized glands.
         o It has a thin, delicate structure that allows for gas exchange, an essential function for amphibians.
   2. Coloration
         o The dorsal side often shows a dark, greenish, or brownish color with spots or patterns for
             camouflage.
         o The ventral side is usually lighter, aiding in thermal regulation and predator avoidance.
   3. Presence of Glands
         o Numerous mucus glands are visible, which keep the skin moist and facilitate cutaneous
             respiration.
         o Poison glands (in some frogs) can be identified as small, granular structures, which serve as a
             defense mechanism.
