Answer: (Greek: mikron = small and skopein = to look)

3) The first Microscope is developing in which year? Answer: 1590

4) .........coined the name microscope for the compound microscope
Galileo submitted to the Accademia dei Lincei?

Answer: Giovanni Faber in 1625

Answer: 14 parts

7) A……………………. is that part of an optical system, (in a microscope)
which is directed to the viewer?

Answer: An eyepiece

•8) An eyepiece converts the real- enlarged-intermediate- image from
the objective into……………?

Answer: an enlarged- virtual-image

9) The main task of Lens tube in microscope is……..?

Answer: to hold eyepiece

10) The diopter adjustment is a …...on your binocular.

Answer: Control knob

11) An …………is that part of an optical system, which is directed to the
object. It´s task is to collect the light rays,that are reflected from
the observed item.

Answer: Objective lens

12) The………. serves as a holder for the object plate.

Answer: Clip

13) Cross table is a ……………. term?

Answer: Technical term.

14) There are………, which can be used to move the table very precisely.

Answer: Adjusting screws

15) The screws are provided with a ………? Answer: Measuring scale,

16) A ………… is an optical lens which renders a divergent beam from a
point source into a parallel or converging beam to illuminate an object.

Answer: Condenser

17) How many lenses are present in condenser? Answer: One or two
lenses.

18) An ……………lens avoids chromatic aberration?

Answer: Aspheric lens

19) With the …………..one can regulate the distance between object and
objective?

Answer: Fine focus

20) The fine focus moves the stage only…………..? Answer: Minimally

21) Like the fine focus, the ………………also moves the stage to regulate
the difference between object and objective?

Answer: Coarse focus

22) The early microscopes used …………mirrors to refrect light on the
objects.

Answer: Concave

24) The base of microscope guarantees the necessary………to the
microscope.

Answer: Stability

25) Luminous-field diaphragm can be used to …………?

Answer: Reduce the illumination and adapt it to the size of the object

26) How many types of microscope?

Answer: Two

27) ………..microscopes use a single lens to magnify an object and cannot
reach high magnification……….?

Answer: Simple light microscopes

28) Compound light microscope has a higher………………power?

Answer: Magnification

29) The simplest microscope of all is a ……………?

Answer: Magnifying glass

30) Microscopes used in homes, schools, and professional laboratories
are actually compound microscopes and use at least two lenses to
produce a magnified image.       True

31) Most compound microscopes can magnify by………, or ……… times.

Answer 10, 20, 40, or 100.

32) MAGNIFICATION is defined as…………? Answer: Degree of
enlargement

33) Magnification is depends upon how many factors?

Answer: 3 (Optical tube length 2. Focal length of objective 3.
Magnifying power of eye piece)

34) the …………………..which is a measure of how great a substance slows
the speed of light. Answer: Refractive index.

35) …………is the change in direction of a wave passing from one medium
to another?

Answer: Refraction

36) ………………..also called index of refraction, measure of the bending
of a ray of light when passing from one medium into another.

Answer: Refractive index

37) In microscopy, the term …….is used to describe the ability of a
microscope to distinguish details of given specimen. This is the
resolving power of microscope.

Answer: Resolution

38) ……………..refers to various phenomena that occur when a wave
encounters an obstacle or opening?

Answer: Diffraction

39) ………………is used to examine a specimen. Answer: Wavelength of
light (λ)

40) The smaller is the wavelength of light (λ), the greater is its ability
to ………?

Answer: resolve the points on the object

41) The ………….is the wavelength of light, the greater is its resolving
power.

Answer: Smaller

42) …………….is a number that expresses the ability of a lens to resolve
fine details in an object being observed.

Answer: Numerical aperture

43) Light diffracted by the specimen is presented as an……………?

Answer: inverted cone of half-angle (α)

44) Higher values of numerical aperture permit increasingly oblique
rays to………..?

Answer: enter the objective front lens

45) Most objectives in the magnification range between ………..and ………..
(And higher) are designed for use with immersion oil.

Answer: 60x and 100x
