Challenges
1. Registration of madaaris is a major problem which is debated across the board. The exact
figure of madaaris is not clearly identified. Only those madaaris which are registered with
one of the

2. Madrassa curriculum is under heated debate inside the madaaris and beyond. According to
one
school of thought, madrassa curriculum does not address the modern requirement of the
present era. Many observers believe that some subjects being taught in madaaris foster
provocation, leading students to extremism and militancy.
3. Since 1999, the issue of madaaris reforms has been negotiated between the government
and the administrations of wafaqs or boards. It is not clear what development has been
made so far. What are the demands of the madaaris and which of them have been

foreign countries to promote their agendas. It is considered that madaaris are being used as
platforms for exerting influence of their favored sect(s) by foreign countries.
5. Funding education of foreign students is an important topic of debate between the
government and the madaaris. Modalities of education of foreign students in Pakistani
madaaris are yet to be resolved.
