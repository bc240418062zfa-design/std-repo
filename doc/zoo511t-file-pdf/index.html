Question No 1. Systematics:
According to Simpson 1961: Systematics is the scientific study of kind and diversity
of organisms and of any or all relationships among them. Broad term includes
taxonomy, nomenclature and classification etc.

Question No 2 . Write aims of Systematics?
Invent worlds kinds of organisms (flora and fauna) • Provide method for
identification and communication • To produce coherent and universal system
of classification and • Demonstrate evolutionary implications of biodiversity

Question No 3. Traumatic Variations?

Question No 5. Leg parts name of Arthropods?

Question NO 6 Write the differences between Archea and eubacteria?
Archea Characteristics: Prokaryotic cell ,( carbohydrates and proteins) no cell
wall, introns are present. Ribosomes are present. None are parasitic. Gene
resemblance with eukaryotes.

Eubacteria Characteristic: Prokaryotic cell. Peptidoglycan cell wall. No introns.
No ribosomal proteins. Can be parasitic and can cause diseases. No gene
resemblance.

Question No 7 Cluster Systematics ?

It is for biological classification and understanding evolutionary relationship. It is
specific to biology and taxonomy. It uses clustering algorithms to identify natural
groups.

Question No 8: Genetic Variations ?
Difference in individual members of a population due to genes. Genetic
variations are important for survival and adaptations for species ,evolution and
natural selection.

Question No 9: Non-genetic variations ?
These variations do not depend upon genes.
Individual Variations in time , social variations , post-morten variations
,ecological variations , Traumatic variations are the types of non-genetic
variations.

Question No 10: Genic sterility factors?
1. Reproductively incompatiable species.
2 .Cannot reproduce new young ones.
3. Only closely related species can hybridize successfully. As a result , it gives
genetically sterile species .MULE is an example.

Isotype: it is a duplicate specimen of holotype. They are exact copies or
replicates of the holotype.

Monotype : it is a single specimen that is the only one of it’s kind. It is
unique individual that represents to it’s specie.

Neotype: When scientist do not have a holotype, then they use a special
specie as a reference point in the place of holotype. It is like a backup copy of
holotype.

Perisoma: It refers to the body of spider excluding it’s legs and cephalothorax.
Availability: A scientific name is considered available when it is formally
introduced and made known to the public through a scientific publications,
making it accessible and usable by everyone.

Sereology: it is concerned with the nature and interaction of antigens and
antibodies.

Antigens: are the substances capable for inducing the formation of antibodies
,when introduced into the blood stream of animals.

Dichotomous Key?
It is a device used for identifying unknown organisms to some taxonomic levels
such as species ,genus or family. Sequential key, Analytical key and pathway key.

Sexual Dimorphism?
It refers to physical and behavioral differences between male and female of the
same species. These differences may be in color , size, shape ,structure of body
parts, hormone production, difference in metabolism ,courtship, mating ,nesting
and other social behaviors. For example : king parrot eclectus roratus.

Contributions Of Aristotle?
He is known as the father of biological classification, referred to major groups of
animals .Fish ,birds ,whales and insects• He first used the following terms:

Coleoptera and Diptera. Aristotle made further categories of animals like blood
or bloodless• After his death no system of classification were recognized.

Ecological Characters?
Characteristics of an organism that helps it survive and interact with its
environment. Such as food, adaptaions, migrations ,habitat, hibernation etc.

Use of Formalin in Preservation?
It is used for vertebrate only • Not used for long term storage • Acidic and
difficult to handle • Used when color of animal is important • Alcohols dissolve
most color immediately • Penetrate more easily and internal organs remain in
better condition.

Ecological variations?
These variations refer to different ways in which organisms adapt and respond
to its environment.

Types of ecological variations?
Habitat variations , density-dependent variations ,allometric variations,
neurogenic variations, host-determined variations and variations induced by
climatic conditions.
