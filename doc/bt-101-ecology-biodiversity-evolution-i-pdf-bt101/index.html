Darwin's theory of natural selection states that the organism that are best

In this regard fitness is measured by reproductive success. Fitness of an

Question Number.3
Convergent evolution VS Divergent evolution:
Definition
Convergent Evolution: Convergent evolution is a process by which distantly
related species develop similar structures as adaptations to the environment.
Divergent Evolution: Divergent evolution is a process by which an
interbreeding species diverges into two or more descendant species.
Environment
Convergent Evolution: Both species live within the same environment.
Divergent Evolution: The two species live in different environments than their
ancestor.
Significance
Convergent Evolution: Unrelated organisms evolve similarities while adapting
to a common environment.
Divergent Evolution: The divergence of two different species results in two
species becoming less like the common ancestor.
Developing Structures
Convergent Evolution: Convergent evolution occurs through developing
analogous structures.
Divergent Evolution: Divergent evolution occurs through developing
homologous structures.
Examples
Convergent Evolution: Ostriches, rheas, and emus are examples of convergent
evolution.
Divergent Evolution: Dinosaurs, Darwin’s finches, and forelimb structures of
vertebrates are examples of divergent evolution.

Relationship between invasive species and endangered species:
An invasive species is an organism that causes ecological or economic harm in
a new environment where it is not native. A species of animal or plant that is
seriously at risk of extinction is called endangered species.
Invasive species are capable of causing extinctions of native endangered
plants and animals species reducing biodiversity, competing with native
organisms for limited resources, and altering habitats. This can result in huge
economic impacts and fundamental disruptions of coastal and Great Lakes
ecosystems.
