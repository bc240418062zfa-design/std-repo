Lesson-01

                      WHAT IS RESEARCH?

Topic-001: What is Research?
What is the main focus of the course on research methodology?
The course emphasizes each step involved in research methodology from
identifying the research problem to developing a complete research study.
What are the objectives of the course on research methodology?
The course aims to help students understand research methods in English
Language Teaching (ELT), critically evaluate research problems, formulate research
questions, and present research proposals.
How is research defined?
Research is defined as a systematic inquiry that investigates hypotheses, suggests
new interpretations of data, and poses new questions for future exploration.

What are the objectives of this research methodology course?
The course aims to help students understand fundamental research methods in
English Language Teaching (ELT), critically evaluate research problems, formulate
research questions and objectives, and develop major components of a research
study, such as title, abstract, introduction, research design, discussion, and
conclusion. It also involves presenting a research proposal and defending the
research design.
Topic-002: Definitions of research
How do scientists generally use the term "research" in a scientific context?
In a scientific context, "research" typically refers to the entire scientific method,
including all stages from start to finish.
What are some characteristics of research according to various definitions?
Research is described as a systematic and refined technique of thinking, a reliable,
verifiable, and exhaustive process, and an intellectual process that seeks truth and
new knowledge.
What is the broadest sense of the word "research" and its purpose?
In the broadest sense, research encompasses any formal gathering of data,
information, and facts to advance knowledge. It's conducted with the researcher's
intention and purpose, and it follows the scientific method from start to finish.
The purpose is to add to scientific knowledge, engage in a reliable, verifiable, and
exhaustive process, seek the discovery of truth, formulate hypotheses, collect and
evaluate data, and reach conclusions.
Topic-003: General Characteristics of Research
What are the main characteristics of research?
Research is systematic, logical, empirical, reductive, replicable, and gathers new
knowledge from primary sources. It emphasizes the discovery of general
principles and uses valid data gathering devices.
How does research eliminate personal feelings and preferences?

Research resists the temptation to seek only data that supports hypotheses,
emphasizing objective and logical investigation.
What are the key characteristics of research?
Research is systematic, logical, empirical, reductive, and replicable. It gathers new
knowledge from primary sources, emphasizes the discovery of general principles,
and involves exact, systematic, and accurate investigation using valid data
gathering devices. It maintains logical and objective standards, resists the
temptation to support only hypotheses, eliminates personal feelings and
preferences, and is a patient, unhurried activity. Research is carefully recorded and
reported, and conclusions and generalizations are made carefully and cautiously.
Topic-004: Functions of Research
What is the primary purpose of research?
Research seeks new knowledge for continuous improvement, refines existing
knowledge, and provides baseline data for decision-making.
How does research impact human life?
Research improves various aspects of human life, such as learning, teaching, and
decision-making in specific fields.
What is the primary function of research?
Research primarily seeks new knowledge for continuous improvement. It refines
and extends existing knowledge, providing baseline data and facilitating decision-
making in various fields. Additionally, research contributes to improving learning
and teaching processes and various aspects of human life.
Topic-005: Specific Characteristics of ELT Research
Why is there a substantial amount of research in ELT (English Language
Teaching)?
ELT research provides deep insight, imaginative problem-solving techniques, and a
sound philosophy to improve teaching and learning.
Is ELT research interdisciplinary, and how does it bridge different fields?

ELT research is interdisciplinary, bridging fields like psychology (e.g., motivation
and anxiety) and sociology (e.g., cultural assimilation and social varieties). It is not
as exact as physical sciences and may involve subjectivity.
Why is there a significant amount of research in English Language Teaching
(ELT)?
ELT research is extensive due to its sound philosophy, deep insights, and
imaginative problem-solving techniques. It combines theoretical and applied
research, aiming to enhance teaching and learning. ELT research, while not as
exact as physical sciences, embraces subjectivity and is not restricted to
specialists. It is an interdisciplinary field bridging psychology (e.g., motivation and
anxiety) and sociology (e.g., cultural assimilation and social varieties). It offers a
wide scope for exploration and knowledge generation.
