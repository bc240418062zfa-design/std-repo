1. In a direct request letter, the request can be presented in:
 a) Closing paragraph

 b) Closing sentence
 c) Middle paragraph
 d) First sentence
 Correct option: d) First sentence

2. Bpc is an abbreviation of which of the following?
 a) Blind pasted copy
 b) Blind pasted copy
 c) Blind person copy
 d) Blind photo copy

 Correct option: a) Blind pasted copy

3. All of the following are achieved by adopting an audience-centered tone in a disappointing
news message, EXCEPT:
 a) Understanding that, under the circumstances, your decision was fair and reasonable
 b) Accepting that your disappointing news represents a firm decision
 c) Destroying the receiver’s pride

 d) Remaining well disposed toward your business

 Correct option: c) Destroying the receiver’s pride

4. In routine requests, it is advised to use the direct approach, which means:
 a) Placing the main idea in the middle

 b) Placing the main idea anywhere according to the writer’s choice
 c) Placing the main idea in the end
 d) Placing the main idea in the opening
 Correct option: d) Placing the main idea in the opening

5. The direct method is adopted at the organizing stage of effective business writing for which of
the following purposes?

 a) Presenting evidence
 b) Conveying facts
 c) Presenting a general statement
 d) Communicating a negative message or bad news
 Correct option: d) Communicating a negative message or bad news

6. Which one of the following is the most appropriate sequence for effective writing?
 a) Planning > drafting > organizing > editing > proofreading
 b) Planning > drafting > editing > organizing > proofreading
 c) Planning > organizing > drafting > proofreading > editing

 d) Planning > organizing > drafting > editing > proofreading
 Correct option: d) Planning > organizing > drafting > editing > proofreading

7. Which one of the following options refers to complimentary close?
 a) Confidential
 b) Yours sincerely
 c) Head, Communication Dept.

 d) Dear Sir/Madam
 Correct option: b) Yours sincerely

8. All of the following are different types of Buffer EXCEPT:
 a) Fairness

 b) Bad news
 c) Cooperation
 d) Agreement
 Correct option: d) Agreement

9. The direct plan can be used for which one of the following?
 a) None of the given options
 b) Request refusals
 c) Sales messages
 d) Claims

 Correct option: c) Sales messages

10. While determining the order of the topics, one should review the outline, keeping all of the
following questions in mind, EXCEPT:
 a) Are related topics properly shuffled?

b) Are the ideas of equal importance presented in a parallel manner?
c) Is the sequence of the topics likely to add clarity to my message?
d) Is the sequence of the topics appropriate for the development method I am using?
Correct option: b) Are the ideas of equal importance presented in a parallel manner?
