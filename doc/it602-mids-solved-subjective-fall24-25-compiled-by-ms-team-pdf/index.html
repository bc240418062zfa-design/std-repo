Q5: Benefits of IPv6 over IPv4?

  1. Larger Address Space:
         o   IPv6 has 128-bit addresses vs. IPv4’s 32-bit, allowing more devices.
  2. Auto-Configuration:
         o   Devices can generate their own IP addresses without a DHCP server.
  3. Improved Security:
         o   IPSec encryption is built-in for secure communications.
  4. Better Performance:
         o   No need for NAT (Network Address Translation), reducing
             processing time.
  5. Enhanced Mobility Support:
         o   Allows seamless movement of devices between networks.

Q6: What is Proof of Concept (PoC), and why is it used?

     A Proof of Concept (PoC) is a small-scale prototype used to test the
      feasibility of a system.
     Why is it used?
         1. To identify risks before full implementation.
         2. To test system performance in a controlled environment.
         3. To evaluate vendor solutions before investment.
         4. To demonstrate functionality to stakeholders before final
             development.
