► Because without it, you could never reach the required word-count.

Reference:

Question#1
"The most obvious reason", in the words of Bryman and Bell (p.95), "is that
you want to know what is already known about your area of interest",
including relevant concepts and theories. The process of writing a literature
review should enhance your learning and help you to write a "proper"
dissertation, but these are by-products.
