What is Criterion Base Assessment?

Answer:

Criterion-based assessment is a type of evaluation where a student's performance is measured

against specific, predetermined standards or criteria, rather than being compared to the

performance of others. It focuses on whether the student has achieved the learning objectives

or mastery of the subject matter. This method provides clear expectations and helps to assess

skills or knowledge in relation to defined benchmarks.

Why is it important to check test questions, answer sheets?

Answer:

It is important to check test questions and answer sheets to ensure accuracy, clarity, and

fairness. Reviewing test questions helps identify any errors, ambiguity, or bias, ensuring that

they align with the learning objectives. Checking answer sheets verifies that the scoring is

consistent and correct, reducing the chances of grading mistakes and ensuring that students'

performance is evaluated accurately. This process improves the overall validity and reliability

of the assessment.

Write 3 Purposes of assessment in curriculum?

Answer:

Evaluate Student Learning: Assessment measures students' understanding and mastery of the

curriculum, helping teachers gauge the effectiveness of instruction and identify areas for

improvement.

                                                                                             1

Guide Instructional Planning: Assessments provide feedback to teachers, allowing them to

adjust their teaching strategies, address learning gaps, and enhance the learning experience for

students.

Inform Curriculum Development: The results from assessments can highlight strengths and

weaknesses in the curriculum itself, helping educators refine content and instructional methods

to better meet educational goals.

Importance of pre-designed test for teachers?

Answer:

Pre-designed tests are important for teachers because they provide a standardized way to assess

students’ learning in line with curriculum goals. These tests save time in test preparation,

ensuring consistency and fairness across different classrooms. They also help in evaluating

specific learning objectives and skills, offering reliable data on student performance.

Additionally, pre-designed tests serve as a guide for teachers, helping them align their

instruction with expected outcomes and maintain objectivity in assessment.
