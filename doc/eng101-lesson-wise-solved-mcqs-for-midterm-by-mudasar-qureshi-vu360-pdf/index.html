Lesson 1-2: Introduction to English Comprehension

1. What is the main purpose of English comprehension?

a) To improve writing skills

b) To enhance reading skills

c) To develop speaking skills

d) To learn grammar rules

Answer: b) To enhance reading skills

1. What is the most important aspect of English comprehension?

a) Vocabulary

b) Grammar

c) Pronunciation

d) Understanding

Answer: d) Understanding

1. What is the difference between comprehension and interpretation?

a) Comprehension is understanding the literal meaning, while interpretation is understanding the
implied meaning

b) Comprehension is understanding the implied meaning, while interpretation is understanding the
literal meaning

c) Comprehension is understanding the main idea, while interpretation is understanding the supporting
details

d) Comprehension is understanding the supporting details, while interpretation is understanding the
main idea

Answer: a) Comprehension is understanding the literal meaning, while interpretation is understanding
the implied meaning

1. What is the purpose of the reading comprehension passage in ENG101?

a) To test vocabulary

b) To test grammar

c) To test understanding

d) To test writing skills

Answer: c) To test understanding

1. What is the most effective way to improve English comprehension?

a) Reading widely

b) Practicing grammar exercises

c) Listening to English music

d) Watching English movies

Answer: a) Reading widely

Lesson 3-4: Vocabulary Building

1. What is the meaning of the word "perspicacious"?

a) Having a strong and unpleasant smell

b) Having a keen understanding and insight

c) Having a loud and harsh sound

d) Having a slow and sluggish movement

Answer: b) Having a keen understanding and insight

1. What is the meaning of the word "fastidious"?

a) Careless and sloppy

b) Meticulous and demanding

c) Quick and efficient

d) Slow and lazy

Answer: b) Meticulous and demanding

1. What is the meaning of the word "enigmatic"?

a) Mysterious and difficult to understand

b) Clear and easy to understand

c) Happy and joyful

d) Sad and sorrowful

Answer: a) Mysterious and difficult to understand

1. What is the meaning of the word "inconspicuous"?

a) Clearly visible and noticeable

b) Not clearly visible and noticeable

c) Happy and joyful

d) Sad and sorrowful

Answer: b) Not clearly visible and noticeable

1. What is the meaning of the word "venerable"?

a) Respected and honored

b) Disrespected and dishonored

c) Happy and joyful

d) Sad and sorrowful

Answer: a) Respected and honored

Lesson 5-6: Reading Comprehension

1. What is the main idea of the passage "The Importance of Education"?

a) Education is essential for personal growth and development

b) Education is only for those who can afford it

c) Education is a waste of time and resources

d) Education is only for women

Answer: a) Education is essential for personal growth and development

1. What is the author's purpose in writing the passage "The Impact of Social Media on Society"?

a) To persuade readers to use social media more frequently

b) To inform readers about the benefits of social media

c) To warn readers about the negative effects of social media

d) To entertain readers with a humorous take on social media

Answer: c) To warn readers about the negative effects of social media

1. What is the tone of the passage "The Benefits of Regular Exercise"?

a) Serious and informative

b) Humorous and lighthearted

c) Critical and disapproving

d) Encouraging and motivational

Answer: d) Encouraging and motivational

1. What is the main idea of the passage "The Importance of Time Management"?

a) Time management is essential for success in business

b) Time management is only important for students

c) Time management is a waste of time

d) Time management is only for procrastinators

Answer: a) Time management is essential for success in business

1. What is the author's purpose in writing the passage "The Effects of Climate Change"?

a) To persuade readers to take action against climate change

b) To inform readers about the causes of climate change

c) To warn readers about the consequences of climate change

d) To entertain readers with a humorous take on climate change

Answer: c) To warn readers about the consequences of climate change

Lesson 7-8: Grammar and Sentence Structure

1. What is the correct form of the verb in the sentence "If I ______ (be) you, I would not do that"?

a) am

b) is

c) be

d) were

Answer: d) were

1. What is the function of the word "that" in the sentence "The book that is on the table is mine"?

a) Subject

b) Object

c) Modifier

d) Pronoun

Answer: c) Modifier

1. What is the correct form of the pronoun in the sentence "Everyone ______ (have) their own
opinion"?

a) has

b) have

c) had

d) having

Answer: a) has

1. What is the function of the word "which" in the sentence "The book, which is on the table, is mine"?

a) Subject

b) Object

c) Modifier

d) Pronoun

Answer: c) Modifier

1. What is the correct form of the verb in the sentence "If I had studied harder, I ______ (pass) the
exam"?

a) pass

b) passed

c) would pass

d) would have passed

Answer: d) would have passed
