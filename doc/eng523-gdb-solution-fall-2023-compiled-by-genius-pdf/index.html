Q. How have modern communication platforms, such as social media or texting,
influenced the use and evolution of discourse markers?

SOLUTION:
In today's digital age, communication platforms like social media and texting have
significantly impacted the use and development of discourse markers—those
small words or phrases that structure conversations. Emojis and Stickers
employed to convey emotions and tone in brief digital exchanges, addressing the
challenges of expressing sarcasm, humor, or excitement through text alone.
Abbreviations and acronyms like LOL, OMG, or BRB have become commonplace
discourse markers in digital communication, helping convey reactions, express
agreement, or signal pauses. These shortcuts have integrated into online
language, contributing to the evolution of discourse markers.
Social media platforms have introduced new methods of signaling discourse
through features like hashtags, mentions, and emojis. Hashtags categorize and
connect posts on similar topics, mentions direct messages to specific individuals,
fostering interaction. Emojis provide visual cues, enhancing the conveyance of
emotions and reactions in texts.
In essence, modern communication platforms have shaped the adaptation and
evolution of discourse markers by aligning with the rapid, brief nature of digital

Q. How do discourse markers contribute to the structure and coherence of
conversations?

SOLUTION:
Discourse markers are essential for structuring and enhancing the coherence of
conversations. They signal relationships between different parts of a
conversation, guiding the audience through the flow of information. Here's a
summary of their contributions:
Signaling Relationships:
Addition: Markers like "and," "also," and "moreover" connect related ideas,
contributing to a logical flow.
Contrast: Words such as "but" and "however" highlight differences or contrasts
between ideas.
Causation: Markers like "because" and "therefore" indicate cause-and-effect
relationships.
Enumeration: Words like "firstly" and "secondly" organize information, aiding
audience comprehension.
Managing Turn-Taking:
Discourse markers like "well" and "so" signal transitions between speakers or
topics, enhancing overall coherence.
Expressing Attitude and Politeness:
Markers like "please" and "thank you" contribute to the politeness and tone of a
conversation, shaping its cohesion.
Providing Clarification:

statements, ensuring audience understanding.

Expressing Uncertainty or Hedging:
Markers like "perhaps" and "maybe" convey uncertainty, adding nuance to the
