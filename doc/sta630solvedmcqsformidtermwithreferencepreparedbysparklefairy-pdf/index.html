Deductive and Inductive Reasoning:

Deductive logic or deduction is moving from general principles to specific (particular
instances). One starts from general laws and applies it to specific instances whereas
inductive logic or induction is moving from specific to general principles or from facts to
theories. In induction, one starts from observed data and forms a generalization which
explains the relationship b/w the observed objects.

Exploratory research is type of research which is used to explore a new topic or issue in
order to learn about it. It addresses the “what” question

Limitations:

Exploratory research is difficult to conduct because there are few guidelines to follow

Goals/target/purpose of exploratory research:

Exploratory research is conducted in order to

1. Become familiar with the basic facts

2. Develop well-grounded (strong) picture of the situation

3. Generate new ideas or hypothesis

4. Develop techniques and sense of direction for the future research

Which of the following is a source of exploratory research?

     Secondary data analysis

     Experience surveys

     Case studies and pilot studies

     All of the above (Page-22)

Reference:

Sources of Exploratory research:

In case of exploratory research, researcher may use different sources to gather information
such as

1. Experience survey- in which researcher tries to contact the individuals (people) who have
knowledge about particular research problem

2. Secondary data analysis-it is quick source of background information which is
preliminary review of data collected for another purpose (review of already collected data)
to clear issues in the early stages of research.

3. Case study-the purpose of case study is to get information from one or few situations that
are similar to the researcher’s problem situation.

4. Pilot study-it implies that some aspect of research is done on a small scale and for this
purpose, focus group discussions can be carried out.
