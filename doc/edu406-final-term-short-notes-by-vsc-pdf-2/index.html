through personalized attention and differentiated instruction.

6. Positive Teacher-Student Relationships: A well-managed classroom fosters positive
relationships, trust, and mutual respect between teachers and students, enhancing the learning
experience.

7. Stress Reduction: Effective management reduces stress for both teachers and students,
creating a more harmonious and enjoyable learning environment.
