1. Biosensor?
    A biosensor is a device that measures biological or chemical
   reactions by generating signals proportional to the concentration of
   an Analyte in the reaction.
2. Bioreceptor
   A molecule that specifically recognises the Analyte is known as a
   bioreceptor. Enzymes, cells, aptamers, deoxyribonucleic acid
   (DNA) and antibodies are some examples of bio receptors. The
   process of signal generation in the form of light, heat, pH, charge
   or mass change, etc.
3. Carbon nanotubes use advantages?
   A carbon nanotube is a tube made of carbon with diameters
   typically measured in nanometres. Single-wall carbon nanotubes
   are one of the allotropes of carbon, intermediate between fullerene
   cages and flat graphene, with diameters in the range of a
   nanometre.
   Carbon Nanotubes Properties • CNTs have high thermal
   conductivity. • CNTs have high electrical conductivity. • CNTs
   aspect ratio. • CNTs are very elastic ~18% elongation to failure. •
   CNTs have very high tensile strength. • CNTs are highly flexible
   can be bent considerably without damage. • CNTs have a low
   thermal expansion coefficient.
   arbon nanotubes have several advantages:

  1. *Strength*: They are incredibly strong, even though they are
  very small.

  2. *Conductivity*: They can conduct electricity and heat very well.

  3. *Lightweight*: They are lightweight, which makes them useful
  in materials like composites.

  4. *Flexibility*: They can bend and flex without breaking, making
  them useful in flexible electronics.

   5. *High aspect ratio*: They have a high length-to-diameter ratio,
   which means they have a lot of surface area for their size, making
   them useful in applications like sensors and drug delivery systems.
4. Use of x-ray lithography
   X-rays have wavelengths of 0.04nm to 0.5 nm. X-ray lithography
   was first established by Spears and Smith.
   X-ray lithography is like using a special light to make very detailed
   designs on tiny things. Instead of regular light, we use X-rays,
   which are super small and can make really tiny patterns. We need a
   mask with the design we want and a special material that can react
   to the X-rays. X-ray lithography helps us make small electronic
   chips with very detailed features, quickly and accurately. It's like
   drawing with a super tiny pen!

9. Quantum dots act as semiconductors and how relate to band gap?
• Quantum dots are usually regarded as semiconductors by definition.

10.     Nanotechnology
  Nanotechnology is the science and technology of manipulating
  materials at the nanoscale, which is about 1 to 100 nanometers in
  size.
  Nanotechnology is used in medicine, electronics, energy,
  environment, materials, agriculture, textiles, cosmetics, aerospace,
  and automotive industries.

11.     Moral nanoparticle characteristics.
  Characteristics of nanoparticles include:
  - Small size (1 to 100 nanometers)
  - Large surface area to volume ratio
  - Unique optical, electrical, and magnetic properties
  - High reactivity and chemical activity
  - Ability to penetrate biological barriers
  - Potential for controlled drug delivery and targeting
12.     Basic components of dendrimers
  Basic dendrimer components are • an interior core • Interior layers
  composed of repeating units radically attached to cores. • Exterior
  layer (terminal functionality) attached to interior generations.
13.     Vaccine delivered at Nano Level.
    Deliver vaccines at the nano level, we can use different methods:

  1. Nano beads: Inert solid bead Size range 20-200nm. These are
  tiny solid beads that can carry vaccine antigens on their surface.
  They stimulate the immune system to produce a response.

  2. Polymeric nanoparticles: These are safe, biodegradable particles
  made of polymers. They encapsulate vaccine antigens, protecting
  them and helping to trigger immune responses.

  3. Nano emulsions: Size of globule (100-400nm). 9These are small
  droplets made of oil and water. They can carry vaccine antigens
  and are stable without refrigeration. They're safe and easy to
  administer, even without needles.
