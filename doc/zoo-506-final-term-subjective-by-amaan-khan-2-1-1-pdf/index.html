10) Advantage of weeds destruction in cultural control? 3
The destruction of vegetable trash and weeds, frequently called clean culture, is also an important
factor, especially in the control of hibernating insects. Weeds not only interfere with successful crop
growth by crowding, but they may reduce the vigor of the crop by competing for plant food in the
soil.
The destruction of weeds and brush along fences is of advantage in the reduction of insects.
But it results in the elimination of nesting and hiding places for insectivorous and game birds.

14) Economic importance of insects? 5
No crop of either vegetable or animal origin appears to be entirely free from insect injury. Field, truck
and fruit of crops, both growing and in storage, household goods and food products, forests and the
wood products derived from them, domestic animals and animal products, all are more or less liable
to insect attack. Although, it is the general practice to express losses in terms of dollar valuation. It
probably would be safer to give them in bushels, tons, because the relative values of different crops
and products change from year to year. Because there never has been a season free from the ravages
of insects which might serve as a standard for comparison, much of this loss is not appreciated. If
there could once be such a year entirely insect free, the difference would be apparent at once.

16) Method of biological control.                   5
Parasitic and predaceous insects as well as diseases of insects have already been discussed. As vital
factors in the natural control of injurious insects. It is possible, however, for man to encourage such
beneficial forms by introducing them into new areas. By growing them artificially for liberation
whenever they become scarce in nature. Following this successful attempt many other parasites and
predators have been imported.
Which in many instances have proved valuable in reducing the numbers of foreign pests.

17) Order, family and symptoms of tobacco cutworm.                       5
Family: Noctuidae
Order: Lepidoptera.
Symptoms:
Scrapping the epidermal layer, leaving the skeleton of veins of leaf.
During severe attack, only the stem and side shoots will be standing in the field without any leaf or
bolls.
Larvae feed the leaves by making small holes.
