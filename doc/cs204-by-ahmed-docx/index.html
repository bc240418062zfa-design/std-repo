Question. 1                                                                                                               (6 Marks)

Ali and Asad work for a highly competitive company dealing with sensitive research and development projects. They often have confidential discussions and share critical information. However, an unidentified attacker has managed to compromise their communication.

The attacker covertly intercepts all the exchanged messages between Ali and Asad, gaining access to their sensitive data. The attacker can read, modify, or even manipulate the content of the messages without raising suspicion. They may also impersonate one of the employees to deceive the other party.

After analyzing the above scenario, answer the following questions:

(a)   What is the name of the cyber-attack on Ali and Asad's Communication?
(b)  Identify at least three potential attack risks for Ali, Asad, and their company.

Answer

(a)   What is the name of the cyber-attack on Ali and Asad's Communication

(a) The cyber-attack on Ali and Asad's communication can be classified as a "Man-in-the-Middle" (MitM) attack. In this type of attack, an unauthorized third party intercepts and possibly alters the communication between two parties without their knowledge or consent.

(b)  Identify at least three potential attack risks for Ali, Asad, and their company

(b) There are several potential attack risks for Ali, Asad, and their company in this scenario:

1. Unauthorized Access: The attacker gaining access to sensitive data through interception puts the confidentiality and integrity of the information at risk. The attacker can read and modify the content of the messages, potentially exposing sensitive research and development projects or inserting malicious content.

2. Impersonation: The attacker can impersonate either Ali or Asad, deceiving the other party. This can lead to misinformation, unauthorized actions, or disclosure of sensitive information to the attacker.

3. Data Manipulation: As the attacker has control over the intercepted messages, they can manipulate the content in ways that may not be immediately detected. This can lead to false information being shared between Ali and Asad, affecting their decision-making processes and potentially causing harm to the company.

4. Reputation Damage: If the attacker succeeds in compromising the communication between Ali and Asad, it can lead to reputation damage for both individuals and their company. The unauthorized disclosure of sensitive information or the manipulation of data can erode trust from stakeholders, partners, or clients.

5. Legal and Regulatory Consequences: Depending on the nature of the compromised information, there may be legal and regulatory consequences for Ali, Asad, and their company. If the company deals with confidential customer data or is subject to industry-specific regulations, the breach of communication can result in penalties, lawsuits, or other legal actions.

6. Intellectual Property Theft: If the attacker gains access to sensitive research and development projects, they can steal valuable intellectual property. This can have severe consequences for the company's competitive advantage, market position, and future innovations.

It's important for Ali, Asad, and their company to implement strong security measures, such as encryption, authentication protocols, and secure communication channels, to mitigate these risks and protect their sensitive information.

Question. 2                                                                                                                         (9 Marks)
Zeta Corporation is a leading technology company that has recently developed an innovative software application that revolutionizes how people manage their finances. The application securely collects financial data from users' bank accounts and provides personalized budgeting and financial planning features.
However, a rival company named ABC Tech became aware of Zeta Corporation's groundbreaking software and decided to gain unauthorized access to it. The rival company hires a skilled hacker to breach Zeta Corporation's network security and gain access to the source code and proprietary algorithms of the financial type of application. With this stolen information, ABC Tech plans to create a similar software product with the same user interface without investing in research and development.
Upon discovering the security breach, Zeta Corporation contacts law enforcement and reports the incident. The case is now under investigation by the cybercrime wing.

After reading the above scenario, answer the following questions:

    1. Which personnel shall have the authority to access and inspect the operation of any specified information system to search?

    2. If Zeta Corporation develops a new technology for its software improvement, which type of intellectual property would it use to stop others from copying its invention?

    3. This cybercrime can be categorized under which Pakistani Cybercrime Act?
