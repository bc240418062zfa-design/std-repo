   learning outcomes. The following outcomes and test items some of its common uses.

   i.     How many syllables are there in word Argentina? (4)
   ii.    In the number 612, what value does the 6 represent? (600)
   iii.   If an airplane flying northwest made a 180 degree turn, what direction would it be

   Essay type questions are divided into two types
