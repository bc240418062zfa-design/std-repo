Q: Will Linguistics surpass literature in future? Build an argumentative analysis of your own
on this hypothesis. Your response should be original.

Solution:1

Language plays an important role in everyday aspects of human life. Generally,
English teachers are always concerned about the kind of ideas and information
they impart to their students. In this modern technological age, there are many
ways and resources available to acquire language skills. But language taught and
acquired through literature has its own powerful consequences. Literature is one of
the most in-demand content categories for the English classroom today. It is
confirmed that teaching language through literature promotes effective language
learning. Language through literature expands linguistic knowledge, facilitates
intelligence, stimulates creativity, improves cultural awareness and develops
psychological aspects. Learning a language through literature facilitates
expression, develops reading skills and strategies, and increases fluency and
fluency. Experiencing language through literature encourages dreaming of future

worlds and provides lifelong experience and encourages learners to identify
themselves in different roles.

Literature – Importance and its effectiveness

Literature plays a prominent role in foreign language acquisition and is revived as
an important part of language teaching in view of linguistic and cultural aspects.
Moreover, it has a reinforcing effect in the language class. In English language
teaching, literature is usually used to understand the language. Literature, a part of
the whole humanity, is grappling with universal problems. It paves the way for
acquiring knowledge about multiple structures of a language. Literature serves as a
tool to expand some new methods and techniques in language teaching and
learners can gain more benefits in language learning and proficiency. Literature
helps learners to acquire literary context and facilitate their everyday use and
characters inspire and motivate to overcome various obstacles. It provides their
communication skills of the language.

Solution: 2

The hypothesis that linguistics will surpass literature in the future is both
interesting and complex . linguistics is the study of language , while literature is
the art of storytelling while the two disciplines have some similarities they are
fundamentally different and cannot be compared in a straightforward manner .on
the one hand, the development of technology has allowed for a greater
understanding of language , and this has led to the emergence of new forms of
linguistics and natural language processing . on the other hand , literature has
always been an important part of human culture and will likely remain so in the
future.if the definition of surpass is taken to mean become more important or
influential than then linguistics may indeed surpass literature in the future .as
technology of language and its usage will become ever more important and this
may lead to the emergence of new forms of linguistics that are more influential
than traditional forms of literature.for example the development of artificial
intelligence and natural language processing has allowed for the automation of
many tasks that were once done by humans, and this has led to an increase in the
importance of linguistics .in conclusion , it is difficult to definitively answer

whether linguistics will surpass literature in the future , while technology has
allowed for the emergence of new forms of linguistics , it is unlikely that
linguistics will ever completely replace literature in the future . linguistics will
continue to grow , literature will remain a vital part of our culture and our society
for centuries to come.
