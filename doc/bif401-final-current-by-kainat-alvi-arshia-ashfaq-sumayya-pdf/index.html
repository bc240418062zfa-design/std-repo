   11)        What is similarity and identity?
   ❖ Identity
Identity means the counting number of nucleotides or amino acids which exactly match when two
biological sequences are matched.
Formula for Identity:
Identity = No. of Matches / smaller length × 100
   ❖ Similarity
Similarity means the comparison between two different sequences calculated by alignment approach.
In both identity and similarity the dots are not counted.
   12)        Substitution and Indel

 Indel lead to Gaps in alignment. Indels were addition or removal of amino acids from protein

Substitution:

   14)        Overall Goal of folding in Protein Structure?
The overall goal of protein folding is to achieve a unique, stable, and energetically favorable structure.
This is known as Anfinsen's thermodynamic hypothesis. Proteins fold in a way that minimizes their
free kinetic energy and adopts a structure that allows them to function properly.

   16)       Hydrophobicity of amino acid?
The hydrophobicity of an amino acid refers to its tendency to repel or avoid water molecules.
Hydrophobic amino acids have nonpolar side chains that are not attracted to water, while hydrophilic
amino acids have polar or charged side chains that interact favorably with water. The hydrophobicity
of amino acids plays a crucial role in protein folding, protein-protein interactions, and membrane
protein insertion.
   17)       Relationship b/w mRNA, protein and gene?
Genes contain the instructions for building proteins. The information in genes is transcribed into
mRNA molecules, which serve as intermediates that carry the genetic code from the DNA to the
ribosomes. Ribosomes then translate the mRNA into a specific sequence of amino acids, leading to
the synthesis of proteins. This flow of information from gene to mRNA to protein is vital for the
proper functioning of cells and organisms.
   18)       Folding tunnel how it work in protein folding?
A folding tunnel, also known as a protein folding tunnel, is a pathway within a protein structure that
allows the protein to fold from polypeptide chain into its proper three-dimensional shape. It's like a
tunnel that guides the folding process, ensuring that the protein reaches its functional conformation.
This folding tunnel is crucial for the proper functioning of proteins and plays a role in preventing
misfolding and aggregation. The folding tunnel helps shield hydrophobic regions and aids in the
formation of stabilizing interactions, contributing to efficient and accurate protein folding.
