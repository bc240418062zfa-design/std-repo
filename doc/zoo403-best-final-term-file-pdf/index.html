Question No:01
Discuss types of territory.
There are four types of territory:
     1) Mating territory: place of mating for the partner.
     2) Breeding territory: It contains only a nesting or mating site. It is
        relatively small.
     3) Feeding territory: It is larger than breeding territory because it
        contains sufficient food to support the owner of territory.
     4) Multi- Stage territory: This is the largest type of territory. It includes
        the aspects of both breeding & feeding of territory.

Question No:02
How parental care occur in mammals:
In mammals, lactating females nourish the young and involved in most of
the caring behavior. Males usually play no role in raising young . In some
mammalian species such as lion, males protect the female and young . In
such species a male or small group of males takes care of many females at
once in harem.

Question No:03
What do organisms compete for ?
Members of same species compete for available resources. All organisms
compete for resources they need to survive such as air, water , food &
space .

Question No:04
Social organization in primates:
Variety of social organization exists in primates. The group could comprise of
one male & a group of females called harem, a multi male group or a large
troupe. White handed gibbons are monogamous species. A pair lives with
their offspring. The offspring move away after they become sexually mature.
They are territorial.

Question No:05
Significance of social organization:
    • Social aggregation is defense both passive & active form predators.
    • It offers several benefits to animal reproduction.
    • There is more care of offspring through communal feeding &
      protection.
    • There is reduction in predation pressure by improved detection &
      repulsion of enemies.

What is territoriality with example:
Territory is an area defended against other members of same species . It
provides food, water supplies, nesting area and refuge from danger.
Example: Bear , dogs & cats , lemurs etc use scent marking to signal the
boundaries of their Territories.

Question No:15
Polygyny & polyandrous system:
Polygyny system:
    • It involves an exclusive relationship of one male with two or more
      females.
    • Polygynous species are generally dimorphic.
    • Males are often showier and larger than females.
    Polyandrous system:
        • One female has an exclusive relationship with two or more males.
        • Polyandrous species are also dimorphic.
        • Females are generally ornamented and larger than males.
