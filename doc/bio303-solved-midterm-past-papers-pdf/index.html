What is ncRNAs? (5)

Ans: • Large & Small Noncoding Regulatory RNAs
• One of the most exciting discoveries in the last decade of eukaryotic regulatory biology has
been the identification and characterization of regulatory nonprotein coding RNAs
(ncRNAs).

• NcRNAs exist in two general size classes,
• small consisting of microRNA (miRNAs) and silencing (siRNAs)
and
• Large consisting of long noncoding RNAs (lncRNAs)
• The small ncRNAs termed microRNA (miRNAs) and silencing (siRNAs) typically inhibit gene
expression at the level of specific protein production by
• targeting mRNAs through one of several distinct mechanisms.
• Both siRNAs and miRNAs typically hybridize, via the formation of RNA–RNA hybridization
to their targeted mRNAs
