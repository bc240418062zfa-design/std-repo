                                         Chapter 1

                                   INTRODUCTION

1.1.   Personal Motivations

   As a linguist and language teacher, I have always been fascinated by the complex

relationship between language and identity. Growing up in a multilingual family and

community, I have witnessed firsthand how language choices can shape one's sense of self and

belonging. My educational background in linguistics and language education has equipped me

with a solid understanding of language acquisition and language contact phenomena.

Professionally, I have worked with bilingual students and immigrants, observing how they

navigate language barriers and cultural differences in their daily lives. This research topic

allows me to combine my personal and professional interests, exploring the intricate dynamics

of language and identity.

1.2.   Introduction

   Language maintenance and identity construction among bilingual immigrants have become

pressing issues in today's globalized world, where migration and cultural diversity are

increasingly prevalent.

   This study explores the impact of code-switching on language maintenance and identity

construction among bilingual immigrants, examining how they navigate language choices and

identity performances in everyday conversations.

   Research has shown that code-switching is a common linguistic strategy among bilinguals,

but its implications for language maintenance and identity construction are still debated. Some

scholars argue that code-switching facilitates language maintenance, while others see it as a

                                                                                            2

threat to heritage language preservation. Meanwhile, identity construction among bilingual

immigrants is shaped by complex factors, including language, culture, and social context.

1.3.   Statement of the Problem:

   This study aims to investigate the relationship between code-switching and language

maintenance and identity construction among bilingual immigrants, seeking to answer the

following research questions: How does code-switching influence language maintenance

among bilingual immigrants? How does code-switching shape identity construction among

bilingual immigrants? What are the implications of code-switching for language teaching and

learning among bilingual immigrants?

1.4.   Research Objectives

      To examine the frequency and functions of code-switching among bilingual immigrants

       in everyday conversations

      To explore the role of code-switching in shaping identity construction among bilingual

       immigrants

      To identify the implications of code-switching for language teaching and learning

       among bilingual immigrants

1.5.   Research Questions

      How does code-switching influence language maintenance among bilingual

       immigrants?

      How does code-switching shape identity construction among bilingual immigrants?

      What are the implications of code-switching for language teaching and learning among

       bilingual immigrants?

1.6.    Significance of the Study

    This study on the impact of code-switching on language maintenance and identity

construction among bilingual immigrants has significant implications for:

               Language teaching and learning: Understanding the role of code-switching in

                language maintenance and identity construction can inform language instruction

                and curriculum design for bilingual immigrants.

               Language policy and planning: The findings can inform language policies and

                programs that support language maintenance and cultural diversity among

                immigrant communities.

               Intercultural communication: This study can shed light on how code-switching

                facilitates or hinders effective communication across linguistic and cultural

                boundaries.

               Identity research: The study's focus on identity construction among bilingual

                immigrants can contribute to a deeper understanding of the complex

                relationships between language, culture, and identity.

1.7.    Delimitation

This study delimits itself to:

               Examining the language practices of bilingual immigrants in everyday

                conversations

               Focusing on the impact of code-switching on language maintenance and identity

                construction

               Using discourse analysis as the primary research methodology

               Conducting the study in a specific cultural and linguistic context (e.g., Spanish-

                English bilingual immigrants in the United States)
