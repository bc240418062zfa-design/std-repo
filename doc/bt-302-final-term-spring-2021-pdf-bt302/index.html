Q1. Functions of Cytokines?                  5 Marks
• Used as biological response modifier for treatment of various diseases
• Cytokine is a general term but more specific term used according to their cell of origin
• Monokines: produced by mononuclear phagocytic cells
• Lymphokines: produced by activated lymphocytes
Cytokines function as cascade signaling in the form of cytokine network
Cytokines are considered as redundant
• Redundancy: is due to the nature of cytokine receptors

Q2. Subregion of variable region of antibody?                        5 Marks
Variable region
• Present on heavy & light chains of Immunoglobulins
Two sub-regions
1) Hypervariable (HVR)
• Based on variability of amino acid sequences
• Complementarity determining region
2) Framework Regions
• Regions between hypervariable regions
• Groups & sub-groups of immunoglobulins
• Products of different variable region genes

Q5. Primary and secondary antibody response?                  2 Marks
Primary Ab response

Secondary Ab response

• Major class is IgG but may IgE or IgA but less IgM

Q9. Differentiate between Organ Specific autoimmunity and Non-Organ
Specific autoimmunity. 2 Marks
Organ Specific autoimmunity
• Immune response against organ associated specific antigens
• Antibodies against organ associated antigens damage the organ
• Target organs
• Skin
• Thyroid gland
• Muscles
Non-Organ Specific autoimmunity
• Immune response not against organ associated specific antigens
• Antibodies against not organ associated antigens
• Systemic autoimmune diseases
• Mainly target organs: skin, joints, soft tissues etc.

Q10. Write down the mechanism of Oxygen independent killing?                        3
Marks
1) Oxygen Independent
• No need of oxygen for such kind of intracellular killing of bacteria
• Granules & Vesicles of phagocytes secrete hydrolytic proteins

Effort By Amaan Khan

• Those proteins are bacteriocidal in nature according to their modes of action
Effector Molecule Function:
Cationic proteins (including cathepsin): Damage to microbial membranes
Lysozyme: Splits mucopeptide in bacterial cell wall
Lactoferrin: Deprives proliferating bacteria of iron
Proteolytic and hydrolytic enzymes: Digestion of killed organisms

Q11. How the fragment of antibodies is obtained? Briefly elucide the
properties of the fragment?    5 Marks
Antibodies Fragments
• Produced by proteolytic enzymes i.e papain
• Two fragments are produced which have structure & function relationship
1) Fab
2) Fc

1) Fab
• Fragment for antigen binding (Fab)
• Papain causes the cleavage at hinge regions to produce two identical fragments containing one
heavy & one light chain
• Having monovalent valency for antigen binding
2) Fc
• Crystalizable Fragment (Fc)
• Contain two heavy chains with CH2 & CH3 domains
• Performed effector functions

Q15. Cytokines in adaptive immune system?                           5 Marks
• Cytokines which act primarily for Adaptive (Specific) immune system
1) IL-2
• Produced by Th cells but Tc also produced in lesser extent
• Stimulate B-cell division Can also activate NK cells s& monocytes
• IL-2 acts in autocrine manner on T-cells
• Activation of T-cells result in expression of IL2R & production of IL-2
• IL-2 binds to IL-2R and promotes cell division
• IL-2R decay if T-cells are not activated & eventually proliferative phase ends
2) IL-4
• IL-4 is produced by
macrophages &Th2 cells
