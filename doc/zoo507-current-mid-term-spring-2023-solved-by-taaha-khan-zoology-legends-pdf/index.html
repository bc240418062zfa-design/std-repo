How intraspecific interaction maintains the natural
ecosystem? 5 marks
Intraspecific interactions, or interactions between
individuals of the same species, play a crucial role in
maintaining the balance and stability of natural
ecosystems.
Here are some ways intraspecific interactions help
maintain the Natural balance.

            Zoology Legends
1. Competition: Intraspecific competition occurs when
individuals of the same species compete for limited
resources such as food, water, and territory. It ensures
that no single species dominates the ecosystem and
maintains a balance between population growth and
available resources.
2. Density-dependent regulation: High population
densities can lead to increased competition for
resources, territorial disputes, and higher transmission
rates of diseases. These interactions act as regulatory
mechanisms, preventing populations from growing
unchecked and becoming unsustainable.
3. Social structure and cooperation: In many species,
intraspecific interactions involve social structures and
cooperation. Individuals within a species may form
groups or societies that perform various functions like
foraging, defense, and reproduction. These social
interactions enhance the survival and reproductive
success of individuals.
