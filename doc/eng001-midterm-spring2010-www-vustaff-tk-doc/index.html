It would have been fixed on the weekend. (passive voice)


Question No: 13    ( Marks: 3 )
 A misplaced modifier is a word, phrase, or clause that is improperly separated from the word it describes.
Indicate the error of misplaced modifier in the sentence from options given below

1.  Making only minimum wage, I nearly earned $2,000 last summer.
a. Misplaced phrases

2.  The bus station was located by a river which was made of red brick.
b. Misplaced words

3.  A fish was found in the Pacific Ocean that had been considered extinct.
c. Misplaced clauses

Question No: 14    ( Marks: 3 )
 Write a brief note on Conjunction and its types.
Conjunctions are words which join two sentence clause together.

Question No: 15    ( Marks: 5 )
 "A good writer should be considerate towards his/her readers." Briefly explain with an example.
it means that when writer is writing something he or she should think who his reader is. What they want to read. The piece of writing should not be too hard or complicated for his readers to understand. It should have verity and interesting elements to keep the reader captivated.
