Q1. What is the Dewey model?
Answer:
The Dewey Decimal Classification (DDC) system, often referred to as the Dewey model, is a widely used library classification system. It was created by Melvil Dewey in 1876 and is named after him. The DDC system organizes library materials into different subject areas and assigns them unique numerical codes to facilitate their arrangement and retrieval.

Q2 .what is the frame?
Answer:
frame generally refers to a cognitive or conceptual framework used to organize, interpret, and make sense of information or knowledge. It provides a structure or lens through which educators and learners understand and approach a particular subject or topic.

Q3. Definitions of framing?
Answer:
 "framing" refers to the intentional selection and presentation of information, content, or activities in a way that shapes learners' understanding, perspectives, and interpretations. It involves setting the context, providing a framework, and highlighting certain aspects to guide learners' thinking and sense-making processes.
Q4. Describe action research?
Answer:
Action research is a systematic and collaborative inquiry approach that involves conducting research to address practical problems or challenges within a specific context, typically in educational or organizational settings. It is an iterative process that combines research, reflection, and action, with the goal of generating practical knowledge and implementing positive changes.

Answer:
Critical thinking is a cognitive process that involves analyzing, evaluating, and synthesizing information to form reasoned judgments or make informed decisions. While the specific number of steps in critical thinking may vary depending on the source or framework, the following five-step model is commonly used:

2. Evaluate the information
3. Gather information
4. Analyze and interpret
5. Formulate a response or solutioN

Q6. Relation between mentor and model 5no
Answer:
Ultimately, the relationship between a mentor and a model involves the transmission of knowledge, skills, and inspiration from a person with experience and expertise to someone seeking growth and development.

Q7. Limitations and benefits of reflective practice?
Answer: Reflective practice refers to the process of self-examination and self-evaluation that professionals engage in to gain insights into their own actions, thoughts, and experiences. While reflective practice offers numerous benefits, it also has certain limitations.

Q8. Technical development?
Answer:
Technical development refers to the process of acquiring, improving, and expanding technical knowledge, skills, and abilities related to a particular field or domain. It involves the continuous learning, application, and refinement of technical expertise to keep up with advancements, industry standards, and emerging technologies.

Q9. why reflective practice is a tool for learning?
Answer:
Reflective practice is a powerful tool for learning because it facilitates the integration of knowledge, skills, and experiences, leading to deeper understanding and growth.

Q10. what is work based learning?
Answer:
Work-based learning (WBL) is an educational approach that integrates real-world work experiences and learning activities into a structured curriculum or training program. It involves providing students or participants with opportunities to acquire knowledge, skills, and competencies through practical work experiences within authentic workplace settings.

Q11. What is team working?
Answer:
Team working refers to the collaborative effort of a group of individuals who come together to achieve a common goal or complete a specific task. It involves working together, sharing responsibilities, and leveraging the collective skills and knowledge of team members to accomplish objectives more effectively and efficiently than individuals working alone.

Q12. Schon view of reflection?
Answer:
Schön's Model of Reflection, also known as the Reflective Practitioner Model, was proposed by Donald Schön, an influential theorist in the field of education and professional practice. Schön's model focuses on the process of reflection and its role in professional learning and development. The model suggests that professionals engage in reflection-in-action and reflection-on-action to enhance their expertise and problem-solving abilities.

Q13. Rodger model of reflection?
Answer:
Initially, Rogers conceives of reflection as a minimalist technique designed to maintain absolute fidelity to the client's emotional experience to facilitate catharsis. Reflection, in Rogers' original formu- lation, is a product of therapist self-restraint.

Q14: Define AI?
Answer:
AI, or Artificial Intelligence, refers to the development and implementation of computer systems that possess the ability to perform tasks that typically require human intelligence. AI systems aim to simulate human cognitive processes, such as learning, reasoning, problem-solving, perception, and decision-making, in an autonomous and intelligent manner.

Q15. Define Situational learning?
Answer:
Situational learning, also known as situated learning or situated cognition, is an educational theory that emphasizes learning within authentic or real-world contexts and situations. It proposes that learning is most effective when it occurs in meaningful and relevant situations rather than isolated or abstract environments.
