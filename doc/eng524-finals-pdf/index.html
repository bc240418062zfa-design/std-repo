Q.5
Write political discourse
analysis?

Answer
Political discourse analysis is a
field of discourse analysis which
focuses on discourse in

political forums (such as debates,
speeches, and hearings) as the
phenomenon of interest.
Political discourse is the formal
exchange of reasoned views as to
which of several alternative
courses of action should be taken
to solve a societal problem.
Defining political discourse is not
a straight forward matter. Some
analysts define the
political so broadly that any
discourse may be political.
Growing interest in the area of
political
discourse since the early 1980s.

 4. Political discourse draws on

  metaphorical reasoning.

Q.18
Give importance to discourse
strategies?

Answer.

 1. Framing: the selection of an
  angle of the (complex) reality.
 2. Positioning: involves
  constructing social actors into a
  certain relationship with
  others.
 3. Legitimation: consists in
  justifying and sanctioning a
  certain action or power.

 4. Politicization: attribution of a
  political nature or status to a
  certain reality, such as climate
  change.
 5. Ideological Standpoints: The
  notion that ideologies are
  embedded in discourses is a
  central claim of discourse
  studies, especially ‘‘critical’’
  versions like CDA.
