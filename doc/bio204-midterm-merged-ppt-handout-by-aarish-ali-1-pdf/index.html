Turbiditimetric Method
 A spectrophotometer is used to determine turbidity
  ("cloudiness") by measuring the amount of light that
  passed through a suspension of cells.
 More cells→ more turbidity;
 more turbidity→ less light passing through the
  suspension
 %T is percent transmission - fewer cells present (less
  turbidity) will allow more light to pass through, the
  %T is higher when the cell number is lower.
 Absorbance is the opposite of %T.

                    or              (Lambert-Beer Law)
