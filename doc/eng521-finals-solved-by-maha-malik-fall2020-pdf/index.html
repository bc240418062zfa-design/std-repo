Q. Explain overlapping as a classroom management strategy. (Topic 58)

Overlapping means handling two or more situations or activities simultaneously. A teacher who
can instruct a group of students while responding to student concerns from outside of the group
can keep an entire classroom working smoothly.

Q. Name three assigning roles to advanced students (Topic 133)

    Group leaders
    Monitors
    Teaching assistants

Q. problem in discipline in schools. (Topic 134)

More discipline problems are the result of boredom or alienation. If students are interested in the
class, many discipline problems disappear.

Q characteristics of Discipline plan. (Topic 135)

   1. Variety of activities:
    Plan a variety of activities that appeal to students with different learning styles and
      interests.
    This will usually keep the attention of the majority of students.
   2. Establish routines:
    Starting the class with class agenda on the board can help students to focus and prepare
      for the day’s class.
    Set up signals that the students understand-for quiet, for silence, and so on—using hand
      signals, a bell, or some other method.

Q. What is benefit of teacher availability for student’s motivation? (Topic 144)

Teacher availability

As much as possible be available to students before and after class to establish personal
relationships, so that they feel that they are individuals in the eyes of teacher.

Activity goals

   1. Make students aware of the goals of each learning activity.
   2. If they understand why they are doing the activity, they will participate more willingly.

Activities to be success oriented

   1. Make all activities success oriented.
   2. Students will participate willingly in tasks that seem achievable.
   3. When they have confidence in their success, they will be motivated to try.

behavior expectation? (Topic 146)

Benchmarks

   1. Benchmarking is used to record performance by using a specific indicator.
   2. Benchmarks are specific points of reference connected to levels of performance against

Development of Benchmarks

Benchmarking can be done for:

Q. Identify some of the behaviors of students that need counseling services in schools?
(Topic 150)

When the records of students behavior show deterioration, proper counseling services must be
sought to address the issues.

Professional support is required to deal with:

    Aggressive behavior
    Bullyism
    Criminal behavior
    Sadistic behavior
    Extreme shyness
    Lack of self-efficacy

Counselors must become part of the school culture and must be available all the time on campus
to handle day to day behavioral issues.

Q. how does development of self efficiency and empathy enhances student success in
classroom? (Topic 151)

Empathy: Empathy is extending help to students and showing concern towards them. Teachers
should try their best to put themselves in their students’ shoes and solve problems.

Efficacy: Self-efficacy is facilitated by a person’s beliefs and expectations about his/her ability
to achieve certain tasks successfully or demonstrate certain behaviors.

Q. Performance accomplishment? (Topic 153)

    The manner in which accomplishments are received has an influence on an individual’s
     self-efficacy, expectations and actions.
    In the classroom, for example, poor grades and other negative assessments of ability can
     lower self-efficacy beliefs.

Q. Three factors that develop self efficacy and one that hampers it (Topic 154)

Family, teachers and friends all play a significant role in verbal persuasion about appropriate
behaviors. Beliefs about self are influenced by the message conveyed by others. Encouragement
supports self-efficacy, criticism hampers it.

Q. Five teacher rules that help in establishing caring classroom? (Topic 160)

In order to establish caring classroom, it is important to frame rules that are socially-emotionally

Q. Affective objectives in lesson plans (Topic 162)

   1. Behavior modification happens over time
   2. Drastic changes in behaviors are not long lasting
   3. Integrate affective objectives in the lessons
   4. Make students and other stakeholders realize that they are as important as cognitive
