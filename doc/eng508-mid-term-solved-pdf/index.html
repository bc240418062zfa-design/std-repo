Q. write a note on metaphor (3)(imp)
Metaphor is a type of figurative speech. Typically, a metaphor ascribes to an entity a property that
it does not, strictly and literally speaking, possess, although not all metaphors fit this definition.
Metaphors are not restricted to any particular type of word or phrase (Allott, 1988). The
metaphorical element of a sentence can be a noun phrase, as in ‗John is an iceberg.‘ Verbs can
also be used metaphorically, as in ‗Flintoff drilled the ball to the boundary.

Q. Explain compositionality principal
This theory says that the meaning of a piece of language is based solely on the meaning of its parts,
and the way they are put together. With this theory we come to know that semantics has a very
strong relation with syntax. To understand the structure of a sentence we need to have enough
knowledge about its components such as noun, verb, adjective etc.
Q. Write a brief note on cataphoric expression.
Cataphoric expressions link forward to a referent in the text that follows. This type is opposite to
anaphora
Q. COOPERATIVE PRINCIPLE
The cooperative principle is a principle of conversation that was proposed by Grice 1975, stating
that participants expect that each will make a “conversational contribution such as is required, at
the stage at which it occurs, by the accepted purpose or direction of the talk exchange.” Relevance
theory is the study of how the assumption of relevance holds texts together meaningfully.
Q. how cognitive and communicative theory relates to relevance theory?
Any external stimulus or internal representation which provides an input to cognitive processes
may be relevant to an individual at some time. Utterances raise expectations of relevance, not
because speakers are expected to obey a cooperative Principle and maxims, but because the search
for relevance is a basic feature of human cognition.
Q. Communicative Principle of Relevance
It is an attempt to work out one of Grice’s central claims that an essential feature of most human
communication, both verbal and non-verbal, is the expression and recognition of intentions. We
keep on producing stimuli which gain other people’s attention and thus lay the ground for
communication. It is the tendency to maximize relevance that makes it possible to predict and
manipulate the mental states of others knowing your tendency to pick out the most relevant inputs.
