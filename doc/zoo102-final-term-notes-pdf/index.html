Q.1 Write Electron Transport Chain and its location, purpose and
also its components?
Electron Transport Chain:
It is an organized set of electron carriers and enzymes with enhanced
oxidation reduction reactions which extract the energy of electrons.
Location of Electron Transport Chain:

Inner mitochondrial membrane

Purpose of Electron Transport Chain:

     • Accept electrons from NADH and FADH2

     • Pass electrons through redox center

     • Take the energy of electrons

     • Move H+ across inner mitochondrial membrane

Components of Electron Transport Chain:

     • NADH dehydrogenase

     • Flavoproteins: FAD, FMN

     • Ubiquinone: CoE Q

     • Cytochromes

     • Fe-S proteins

Q.6 Write Energy from Proteins and Excretion of Nitrogenous
Wastes?

1. Energy from Proteins:

  • Keto acids are funneled to Krebs cycle to yield ATP

  • 1g of proteins give approximately 4kcal energy

  • Approximately equal to glucose

2. Excretion of Nitrogenous Wastes:

Ammonia is formed during deamination of Amino acid.In aquatic organisms
ammonia is released from moist surfaces.For release of ammonia large
amount of water is required.In land animals ammonia is converted to urea
.Urea excreted through urinary system.Moderate amount of water for
urea.Animals with acute shortage of water, release uric acid.

Q.7 How we can control Metabolism?

1. Cells are Very Efficient:

Cells do not waste energy.If some molecule in excess, its synthesis is switched off

2. Feedback Inhibition:

The most common method for control of metabolism.In this method the
product inhibits enzyme that catalyzes the key step.

e.g., ATP inhibits phosphofructokinase in glycolysis

3. Control of Catabolism:

If a cell is working hard its ATP is decreases.Decrease in ATP start anaerobic
respiration.Anaerobic respiration slows when ATP is sufficient

4. Control of Anabolism:

In anabolism cells expends energy for biomolecules synthesis.Anabolism is
mainly controlled by regulating the enzyme activity at strategic points
