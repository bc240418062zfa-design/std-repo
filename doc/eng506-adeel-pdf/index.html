A social dialect is really called a “social REGISTER” of a specifc language. It refers to the type of speech,
pronunciaton, and vocabulary used to communicate in the language with a specifc social class or group
with in the greater society. A regional dialect, on the other hand, is one that is strictly defned by
geography, and as such, is used among all people of all social classes/groups within that geography. It’s
a natural deviaton from the standard dialect through a long process of language evoluton, rather than
a social constructon used to more efectvely communicate between those of diferent socio-economic
status within a given area sharing the same standard language.
