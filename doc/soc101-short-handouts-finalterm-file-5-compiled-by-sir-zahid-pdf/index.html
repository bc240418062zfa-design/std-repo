Topic-103: Victimless Crime

What are victimless crimes?

Victimless crimes, or public order crimes, involve activities like prostitution,
unlawful gambling, and drug usage where there's no obvious victim.

Why do some argue that victimless crimes still have victims?

Some argue that these crimes disrupt social order and harm society's moral
fabric, causing harm to all members of society.

Topic-104: Crime against people: Murder, Rape, Personal Assault, Child
Abuse, Harassment

Define crimes against persons.

Crimes against persons involve direct or implied threat or use of force against
another.

What is the definition of aggravated assault?

Aggravated assault is an unlawful attack by one person on another with the
intent to cause serious or grievous physical injury.

Provide examples of crimes against people.

Examples include murder, manslaughter, aggravated assault, robbery, forced
rape, child abuse, and harassment.

Topic-107: Formal Social Control Mechanism: Criminal Justice System

What is the purpose of formal social control mechanisms in complex societies?

Formal social control mechanisms are crucial for upholding social order and policing
behavior.

What institutions make up the criminal justice system?

The criminal justice system consists of the police, courts, and correctional facilities.

What is the goal of the criminal justice system?

The goal is to uphold the law and punish those who break it.

Why is the criminal justice system considered fundamental to the state's power
and authority?

The state's power and authority are fundamentally dependent on the criminal justice
system.

What are common criticisms of the criminal justice system?

It is often criticized, especially regarding questions of bias and impartiality.

Topic-108: Criminal Justice System: The Police

What is the main accountability of police officers in the criminal justice system?

Police officers have the main accountability for enforcing the law.

Besides apprehending offenders, what is another key responsibility of the
police?

They uphold law and order to reduce crime.

How has technology impacted police work since the 1960s?

Technological advancements have led to more complex communication systems,
better-equipped squad cars, and advanced weapons.

What significant policing tactic was adopted by many cities in the early 1990s?

Many cities adopted community policing tactics to rebuild community trust.

Topic-109: Criminal Justice System: The Courts

What percentage of criminal cases are settled before a trial begins?

Over 90% of criminal cases are settled before a trial begins.

What is a plea bargain in the context of criminal cases?

It is a negotiation between prosecuting and defense attorneys where the defendant
consents to a guilty plea to a less serious charge.

What role do judges play in the court phase of criminal justice?

Judges have a considerable degree of discretion in accepting pleas, admitting
evidence, guiding juries, and determining punishments.

What initiates the corrections phase in the criminal justice system?

The corrections phase begins when a defendant is found guilty or enters a plea
agreement.

Topic-110: Criminal Justice System: The Prison

Why are prisons a crucial part of the criminal justice system?

Prisons house convicted criminals and keep them away from society.

What are the uses of prisons in the criminal justice system?

Prisons serve as deterrents, rehabilitation centers, and places for punishment.

Who is in charge of running the prison system in Pakistan?

The provincial governments of Pakistan are in charge of running the prison system.

What criticisms have been directed at the prison system in Pakistan?
