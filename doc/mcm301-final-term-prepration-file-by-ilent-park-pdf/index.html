                       Lesson 19: Making a Good Speech

Deep Explanation:

Yeh bilkul waisi baat hai jaise koi student class mein presentation dene se pehle
mirror ke saamne practice karta hai, taake stage par confidence aur clarity dono ho.
Jaise ek cricketer match se pehle practice nets mein jaata hai, waisi hi speaker ko bhi
apni speech "rehearse" karni hoti hai taake real audience ke saamne ghabraye na.

Key Points:

   1. Audience Awareness: Yaad rakho ke audience tumhari baat dobara nahi sun sakti, is liye
       ideas ko easy aur clear tareeke se present karo.
   2. Eye Contact: Audience se aankh milana zaroori hai, warna woh sochenge tumhe confidence
       nahi hai aur tumhari baat ka asar kam ho jayega.
   3. Gestures and Movement: Body movement aur hath ke gestures se important points ko
       highlight karo, lekin statue ki tarah khade na raho.
   4. Avoid Memorizing or Reading: Speech ko ratta na lagao aur na hi likh kar parho, sirf chhote
       notes ek page par rakho.
   5. Rehearsal: Speech ko bol kar practice karo jab tak comfortable na ho jao, aur time limit ka
       khaas khayal rakho.
   6. Recording Yourself: Agar mumkin ho to apni speech record karo aur sun kar tone, clarity, aur
       speed check karo.
   7. Know the Room: Speech wali jagah pehle se dekh lo taake andaza ho seating aur loudness ka.
   8. Overcoming Stage Fright: Room, audience, aur apna material acha tarah jaan lo, aur khud ko
       successful imagine karo taake nervousness kam ho.
   9. Informative Speech Types: Yeh speeches objects, processes, events, ya concepts ke baare
       mein hoti hain jo audience ko information dene ke liye hoti hain.
   10. Persuasive Speech: Yeh speech audience ki belief ya action change karne ke liye hoti hai,
       jisme Question of Fact, Value, ya Policy shamil hote hain.
   11. Organizing Persuasive Speeches: Inko Problem-Solution, Problem-Cause-Solution,
       Comparative Advantages, ya Motivated Sequence patterns mein organize kiya ja sakta hai.
   12. Ceremonial Speeches: Yeh tributes, acceptance speeches, ya master of ceremonies jaisi
       special occasion speeches hoti hain.

Example:

Socho ek student ne class presentation dene se pehle apna room dekh liya, dost
banaye audience mein, aur speech ko kaafi baar practice ki. Stage par jaa kar usne
audience se eye contact rakha, hath se gestures kiye, aur apne notes ek hi page par
rakhe. Result yeh hua ke uski nervousness kam ho gayi aur audience ne uski baat ache
se samjhi. Yahi hai ek good speech banane ka tareeqa.

Conceptual Questions:

Q: Why is eye contact considered important during a speech, even though it might
feel uncomfortable?

Answer: Eye contact se audience ko feel hota hai ke speaker confident hai aur unhe
seedha address kar raha hai.

        Jaise agar koi speaker hamesha floor ya ceiling dekhe, audience sochegi ke woh nervous hai
         ya unprepared hai, jis se uski credibility kam ho jaati hai.

Q: What is the difference between an informative speech and a persuasive speech?

Answer: Informative speech sirf information dene ke liye hoti hai, jis mein speaker
sirf facts batata hai bina opinion ke. Persuasive speech mein speaker audience ki
belief ya action ko change karne ki koshish karta hai.

        Jaise agar koi speech sirf "social media kya hai" bataye to woh informative hai, lekin agar
         woh kahe "social media ka istemal kam karna chahiye" to woh persuasive hai.

Lesson Quiz

   1. What should a speaker avoid doing with their presentation notes to prevent sounding like
         they are reading? (Memorizing or writing out the full speech word-for-word)
   2.    What technique helps reduce stage fright by mentally rehearsing success before speaking?
         (Visualization)
   3.    Which type of informative speech focuses on "how-to" perform an action? (Process speech)
   4.    What are the three kinds of persuasive speeches based on questions of fact, value, and what
         else? (Policy)
   5.    Which organizational pattern is best suited for speeches that seek immediate audience
         action? (Motivated Sequence)

                    Lesson 20: Handling Questions & Answers

Deep Explanation

Yeh bilkul waisi baat hai jaise exam dene se pehle tum guess karte ho ke paper mein
kaunse questions aa sakte hain, aur unki taiyari pehle se kar lete ho. Speaker bhi
presentation dene se pehle sochta hai ke audience kya pooch sakti hai, taake stage par
confuse na ho.

Steps:

   1. Anticipating: Iska matlab hai ke presenter pehle se sochta hai ke audience kya questions
         pooch sakti hai.
   2.    Answering: Har anticipated question ka jawab pehle se likh kar taiyar karna chahiye.
   3.    Revising: Agar same questions har baar aate hain to presentation ke content mein hi unka
         jawab shamil kar dena chahiye.
   4.    Rehearsing: Q&A session ki practice associates ke saath karni chahiye taake confidence aaye.

Key Points:

   1. Previous Experience: Agar pehle bhi same topic par speech di hai to purane questions yaad
       rakh kar unki taiyari karo.
   2. Common Concerns: Log aksar time, cost, aur impact se related sawal poochte hain.
   3. Planning: Presentation taiyar karte waqt sochna chahiye ke audience kis baat mein
       interested hogi.
   4. Input from Associates: Doston ya colleagues se practice karwana faida mand hota hai, woh
       naye sawal sochne mein madad karte hain.
   5. Summary vs Expanded Answer: Har sawal ka chhota jawab aur agar zaroorat ho to detailed
       jawab dono taiyar rakhne chahiye.
   6. Conceptual vs Concrete Questions: Kuch log big-picture sawal poochte hain aur kuch log
       detailed specific sawal poochte hain, dono ki taiyari zaroori hai.
