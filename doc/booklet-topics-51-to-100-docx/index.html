    58. Online Store: Components of Online Store -- Active Online Presence
Just like another business, online businesses need to attract customers in order to successful generation of business. So online store businesses must focus on continuous marketing activities while using multiple channels so the customer know about the existence of online store. Not only this, customers should be updated regularly by running different campaigns in order to attract them. Promotions, offers, sales and discounts should be properly communicated to customers constantly and they must be related to customers in order to increase their interest in buying and for that content on store should be updated in real-time.
    59. Online Store: Components of Online Store -- Mobile Responsive Website
Technology not only makes things easy for businesses but add challenges as well. Customers are accessing Desktop and Mobile devices to access online stores, ecommerce store must ensure customers enjoy same user experience regardless of the device. One such thing is responsive website which means website should be open properly without disorientation in Mobile and other devices (tablet). Mobile users are increasing day by day; roughly there are 5 billion mobile users in 2018 as compared to 4.01 billion in 2013. This increase in numbers makes responsive website a key success factor for online businesses. Examples of responsive websites are as follows:
    • www.amazon.com
    • www.aliexpress.com
And the example of non-responsive website is:
    • www.fccollege.edu.pk
    60. Online Store: Components of Online Store -- Guest Sign In
Another important factor is the ease of purchasing a product from an online store because online shopping is about convenience with no-bargain facility. Some store required a hectic procedure to create an account before they can actually checkout with the product. Guest Sign is to enable customer to proceed with online shopping without forcing to register first. According to a research in 2017, “creating an account” was the second biggest reason behind abandoning purchase during checkout followed by “too long and complex checkout process” on third number.

    61. Online Store: Components of Online Store -- Product Catalogue and Branded Products
Customers’ needs information about the product they are interested to purchase. Not only this, they want to see the product online just to increase their level of satisfaction. So it is of utmost importance to create a product && product details page along with add high quality product images in different zoom/angles. In order to support customers in making buying decision, an online store must have:
    • Product description, price, quantity available should be mentioned explicitly
    • Specification of product to be mention with warranty
    • High quality images of the products
    • Shipping details like time and cost etc.
    62. Online Store: Components of Online Store -- Product Review and Rating
Product reviews and rating from previous buyers are another source of convincing a customer to make a purchase decision. Customers may find information about the utility and quality of the product they are interested in and that increase the probability of checkout. Positive reviews will increase the level of trust between the store and customers, which is the biggest challenge in ecommerce, as they give good impression about the product quality, utility, reliability of product and store.
