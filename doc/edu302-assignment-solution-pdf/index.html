Question 1
As you have studied about the principles of growth and development. Write the
educational implications of these principles. (Any five)                    (10 marks)

Educational Implication of the principles of Growth & Development.

Educational implication of the principles of Growth and Development is as follows:
We can apply the Principles of Development in many ways in the field of education. Such as –
First of all, a key principle of the development process is continuity. That is, the development
process is a continuous process. As the development process is a continuous process, we must
continue our efforts until the development of different fields of human life is completed. Here the

development of different fields means Physical Development, Mental Development, Social
Development, Emotional Development, Moral Development etc.

Secondly, another important principle of the development process is the principle of individual
differences. The essence of this principle is that one person is different from another. This different
can appear in a person from any direction. This difference is also seen in the person in terms of
mental capacity. That is, differences in mental capacity can be observed from person to person due
to the principle of individual differences. So, in the field of education, we should choose the
curriculum and method of teaching for students by judging their differences in the mental capacity.
This will make the learning of students easy and enjoyable.

Thirdly, another principle of the development process is to move from the general to the special.
We can apply this principle in education. By applying this principle, the education of the students
can be taken forward. In this case, the experiences related to the generality can be taught to the
students before the experiences related to the specialty or the appropriate plan can be adopted in
this regard.

Fourthly, the two important principles of the developmental process are the Cephalocaudal
Tendency and the Proximodistal Tendency. The key word of the cephalocaudal tendency is that
the development process starts from the head and progresses to the feet. And the key word of the
proximodistal tendencies is that the developmental process starts from the center of the body and
progresses other parts of the body. With the consideration of these two tendencies in mind, we can
choose the right learning experience for students and create the right learning environment. This
will help the students to develop completely according to the above tendency.

Fifthly, one of main principles of development is that the development process is the result of the
interaction between the heredity and the environment. So, as the development process is the result
of the interaction between the heredity and the environment, the student’s learning can be taken
forward by making the correlation between the heredity and the environment in the field of
education and the appropriate plan can be adopted in this regard.

As an elementary school teacher how can you use Gardner’s theory of multiple
intelligences in the classroom?                                           (10 marks)

How to Use Multiple Intelligences in the Classroom
Get to Know Your Students Better

There are many MI assessment tools available online and to print for use in your
classroom. These tests can provide a fascinating snapshot of your students’ innate
abilities. Students should think about how to use their strengths to help them in all
subjects. Maybe that musically intelligent student should make up a song to help her
learn the Periodic Table of Elements. Or that visually inclined student should draw a
picture to help remember the life cycle of a butterfly.

Expand Upon Traditional Activities

Traditional school activities focus primarily on linguistic and logical-mathematical
intelligences. But here are some ways to reach any intelligence type in your classroom:

Linguistic

Ask students to write a story about what they’re studying. If they’re learning a process,
have them write an instruction manual or give a “how to” speech. Writing scripts, making
videos, or designing a brochure are other good activities for this intelligence.

Logical-Mathematical

Beyond doing math problems, this intelligence focuses on logical reasoning and
problem solving. Can students conduct a survey and graph or chart the results? For
English or history classes, use a debate to teach the basics of logic and rhetoric. Ask
students to function the way a real-life scientist does – using logic and/or math to solve
a problem or propose a hypothesis.

scientific process. Use manipulatives in math, have them build a model with clay, or
