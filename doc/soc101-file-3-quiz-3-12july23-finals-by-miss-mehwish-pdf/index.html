1. Which type of view of society was developed by Weber?

Options:

a) Triploid

b) Bipolar

c) Multidimensional

d) Unipolar

Correct Option: c) Multidimensional

2. Which of the following factors is instrumental in the erosion of traditional family and marriage values?

Options:

a) Social and economic

b) Archeological factors

c) Biological and physiological

d) Cultural factors

Correct Option: d) Cultural factors

3. Schooling is the ___________ process of transmission of knowledge.

Options:

a) Informal

b) Informal

c) Preliminary

d) Formal

Correct Option: d) Formal

4. Feminist approach advocates all of the following EXCEPT:

Options:

a) Social equality for men and women

b) Gender discrimination in society

c) Promoting gender autonomy

d) Elimination of gender violence

Correct Option: b) Gender discrimination in society

5. Sadaf is a social scientist. She believes that poverty is a side effect of the capitalist system that is most
associated with which of the following perspective?

Options:

a) Functionalist

b) Structuralism

c) Interactionism

d) Conflict

Correct Option: d) Conflict

6. What is shown by sociological research on the impact of tracking in schools?

Options:

a) Tracking doesn't benefit students

b) Tracking works best where there is a proper system

c) None of the given options

d) Tracking reinforces previously existing inequalities

Correct Option: d) Tracking reinforces previously existing inequalities

7. The social-conflict explanation for social stratification is:

Options:

a) Social stratification is inevitable and universal phenomena.

b) A society reflects the interests of the more powerful members of society.

c) Social stratification systems remain stable over time.

d) Social inequality benefits society that plays a vital part in the operation of society.

Correct Option: b) A society reflects the interests of the more powerful members of society.

8. The idea that poverty is caused by shortcomings in the poor themselves is called:

Options:

a) The culture of poverty

b) Feminization of poverty

c) Absolute poverty

d) Relative poverty

Correct Option: a) The culture of poverty

9. Male games and activities in the playground usually promote which type of traits?

Options:

a) Conflicting

b) Masculine

c) Traditional

d) Feminine

Correct Option: b) Masculine

10. The two partners may divorce and enter into a new union at a time this practice is most commonly
found in:

Options:

a) Technologically advanced societies

b) Industrial society

c) Traditional society

d) Open society

Correct Option: b) Industrial society
