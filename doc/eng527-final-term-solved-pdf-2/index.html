MCQs / Short-answer Questions
   1. Hip-hop is identified in language gender as...
       Hyper-masculinity / masculine identity — the handout discusses hip-hop in relation to
       hyper-masculinity, toughness, rage and the “pimp/mack” persona.
   2. The distinctive use of one or more languages is called...
       Bilingualism / multilingualism (depending on the exact options).
       The handout specifically discusses gender and bilingualism and the use of linguistic
       resources in different contexts.
   3. Who introduced "dictionaries grammar"?
       Hellinger and Pauwels (2007) — they argue that “dictionaries, like grammars, are sites
       of codification and normative language.”
   4. Whose writer identified the term "they" as a singular gender?
       The handout supports “they” as a gender-neutral/generic pronoun, but the specific
       writer credited with introducing singular they is not clearly identified in the supplied
       handout section. The handout does state that feminists promoted “they” instead of
       generic “he.”
       Exam tip: If your MCQ options include “they = gender-neutral pronoun,” choose
       that.
   5. In detergent ads, women are seen while men are absent. What type of stereotype
       does it show?
       Gender stereotyping / traditional gender-role stereotype — women are associated
       with domestic work and household responsibilities.
   6. The habits of women using rising and falling tones in speech shows...
       Gender differences in intonation / sex-differentiated speaking styles. Lakoff
       particularly associated women's speech with rising/question intonation.
   7. Butler's theory described performance is done by...
       Gender is performed through repeated social practices/acts. Butler's approach treats
       gender as performance, rather than something simply possessed.
   8. In traditional movies, the female style is commonly shown in plot...
       Submissive/domestic roles, while men are portrayed as dominant. Traditional Pakistani
       TV productions are described as using androcentric plots in which women occupy
       secondary roles.
   9. Which is used to act power?
       Language / discourse. Language can be used to construct, maintain or challenge power
       relations.
   10. English is used as ___ language in advanced educational areas of Pakistan.
       A language of higher/advanced education (the wording of this question appears
       abbreviated).
       The handout discusses English in relation to education and linguistic capital, but the exact
       sentence in your question is not clearly reproduced in the searchable handout.
   11. Safe emojis shown as in culture...
       Emojis that are less sensitive to different cultures. These are specifically called “safe
       emojis.”
   12. Who introduced linguistic governmentality of neoliberalism?
       Inoue (2007).

Short Questions
1. What approach do workplace discourse analysts use to study the enactment of gender
identity, and why is it preferred?

They use a micro-analytic, discursive approach based on naturally occurring/authentic
workplace data. It is preferred because it shows what people actually do in workplace
communication, rather than relying on what people report they do. Authentic recordings also
capture the nuances of identity work in situated interaction.

2. State two main demands of secular feminists in the Muslim context.

Two important demands are:

   1. Reform/repeal discriminatory Islamic laws and promote equality and secular
      principles.
   2. A secular state that remains neutral toward religions and guarantees freedom of
      worship and non-practice.

3. Which framework in feminist sociolinguistic research focuses on phonological variation?

Community of Practice (CoP) framework.
The handout specifically says phonological variation is at the heart of much feminist
sociolinguistic research using the community of practice framework.

4. Give three examples that show women's invisibility in textbooks.

   1. Women are less represented as speakers in dialogues.
   2. Women are less represented as people referred to in texts.
   3. Women are less represented in visuals/images.

5. Enlist three categories/examples of hurtful jokes.

The handout identifies hurtful humour such as:

   1. Sarcasm
   2. Put-downs
   3. Ethnic/anti-group jokes, including anti-men or anti-women jokes.
