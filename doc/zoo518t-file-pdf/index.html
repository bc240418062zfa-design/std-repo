                             SHORT QUESTIONS(3)
   1)    Name the parts of legs in insects?
Legs of insects (adults and larvae) are jointed and have five parts:
   1) coxa,
   2) trochanter,
   3) femur,
   4) tibia, and
   5) tarsus.
   2)    Name the part of head of insects/Structures of head?
Structures found on the head are
   1) A pair of antennae
   2) Two compound eyes (ocelli)
   3) The mouth parts

   3)    Define Cerci?
A pair of many-segmented, antenna-like structures, sometimes short, sometimes long,
may occur at the end of the abdomen. And these are called cerci. They probably serve
as organs of touch and possibly organ of smell in some cases.
   4)    What is coxa in insects?
The coxa is the segment that articulates with the body, frequently partly lying in a more
or less cup shaped hollow of the latter. It may be short or long and is generally freely
movable on the body and powerful. This arrangement allows for movement and
flexibility.
   5)    What are tracheal gills?
Insects that live in water during their early stages of life cannot breathe air into their
bodies through spiracles like they do when they are on land. Instead, they have special
structures called tracheal gills that allow them to obtain oxygen from the water. These
tracheal gills help them breathe while they’re in the water.
   6)    What is meant by contact insecticides?
It was formerly supposed that certain materials called contact insecticides, which kill
insects by contact with their bodies. They caused death by entering the spiracles and
closing them up, thus producing suffocation.
   7)    Define Ovipositor?
The ovipositor is a combination of different parts at the end of the abdomen in female
insects that lay their eggs inside objects. It helps them make a hole or slit in the object
and guide the eggs into it. It usually consists of about three pairs of parts, which can be
long or short, slender or stout, depending on the insect. In some insects, the ovipositor
has transformed into a sting because they no longer need to make holes for egg laying.
   8)    How skeleton is formed in Arthropoda?

In arthropods, the skeleton is formed by a process called ecdysis or molting. When an
arthropod grows, its exoskeleton becomes too small and rigid. To accommodate
growth, the arthropod sheds its old exoskeleton and forms a new one. This process
allows them to renew and replace their skeleton as they continue to grow. The surface
of the body is covered by a secretion which hardens on exposure to the air, forming an
outside new shell or external skeleton called exoskeleton.
   9)    Differentiate between Pulivillus, Pulvilli, And Epodium ?
Pulivillus: a soft, padlike structure located at the base of each claw on the feet of
certain insects.
Pulvilli: Pulvilli are lobes or pads that are located between the tarsal claws of many
insects.
Empodium: Between the pulvilli there is often a spine that is called the empodium.
   10) Differentiate between exoskeleton of Arthropoda and
       Molluska/How form exoskeleton of insects?
Both mollusks and arthropods have an external skeleton, which gives them a shell-like
appearance. However, the materials that make up their skeletons are different.
   • Mollusks have a skeleton made mostly of calcium carbonate. e.g. snails.
   • While arthropods have a skeleton made of chitin, which is similar to horn.
     Sometimes, arthropods also have calcareous salts deposited in their chitin. e.g.
     spiders and insects.

   11) Which order of insects are Hemimetabola?
In the Hemimetabola, then the stages of life are
   1) Egg
   2) Nymph
   3) Adult
Found in In three of the orders: (from handout)
   1) Ephemerida (mayflies)
   2) Odonata (dragonflies, damselflies)
   3) Plecoptera (stoneflies)
Additional
   1) Hemiptera (aphids)
   2) Diptera (housefly)
   3) Orthoptera (grasshopper)
   4) Lepidoptera (bugworm)
   5) Hymenoptera ( honeybees, wasps)
   6) Coleoptera (beetles, weevils)

   12) Write the structure present on the thorax and abdomen/
       Segment name of thorax?
On the thorax are the wings and legs. The thorax has its three segments usually quite
clearly marked
   1) Prothorax (anterior most segment)
   2) Mesothorax (middle)
   3) Metathorax (Posterior)

The basic number of segments in the abdomen is eleven plus the telson which bears
the anus. Each segment of the abdomen consists of a
   1) Dorsal sclerite, the tergum, and a
   2) Ventral sclerite, the sternum,
These joined to one another laterally by a pleural membrane. On the abdomen are
various organs such as the ovipositor, sting, cerci and styli, present in some cases,
absent in others.

                              LONG QUESTIONS(5)
   1)     Write the general types of Larvae.
   1) Larvae of butterflies and moths are commonly called caterpillars,
   2) Those of beetles are usually called grubs,
   3) Those of flies are called maggots,
   4) Larvae found boring in wood, however, whether they will become moths, beetles
      or other insects, are uniformly called borers.

   2)     Characteristics of Insects/ which characters you identify in
          an insect?
   o Bilaterally symmetrical
   o In the Hexapoda or insects the segments of the body are grouped in three distinct
     sections: 1. Head 2. Thorax 3. Abdomen
   o A pair of antennae is present on the head
   o The six legs(3 pairs) are attached to the thorax
   o The four wings(2 pairs) usually present
   o The animals breathe by air tubes
   o The animal is covered by a Shell like skeleton present outside the body, horny in
     nature.
   o Reproductive organs present near the hinder end of the body.
   3)     Types of Insect Head on the basis of their Positions?
Based on the inclination of long axis of the head and orientation of mouth parts there
are three types of insect heads.
        ❖ Hypognathous (Hypo – Below: Gnathous – Jaw):
 Head perpendicular to longitudinal body axis (mouthparts pointed downward). This
includes insects like ants, locusts and beetles.
