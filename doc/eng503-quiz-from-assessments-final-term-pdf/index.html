1. In thematic roles the entity for whose benefit the action was performed is known as_______.
   (beneficiary)
2. ‘Crown’ for ‘royalty’ is an example of ____. (metonymy)
3. In linguistics,________ , are words which sound alike or are spelled alike, but have different
   meanings. (Homonyms)
4. Semantic/Thematic roles were introduced in generative grammar during the mid 1960s and
   early 1970s and are also called ______ roles, ______ roles, semantic cases and deep cases.
   (participant, theta)
5. Is the following correct or incorrect?
Words with very closely related/similar meanings are called antonyms. (Incorrect)

6. Is the following correct or incorrect?
A relation of inclusion; a hyponym includes the meaning of a more general word, e.g., dog and cat
are hyponyms of animal. (Correct)

7. Is the following correct or incorrect?
Such a relationship between opposites where the positive of one term does not necessarily imply
the negative of the other, for example rich/poor, is known as gradable antonym. (Correct)

8. Thematic roles were introduced in _____ grammar during the mid 1906s and early 1970s
   (generative)
9. Is the following correct or incorrect? One word or phrase is substituted for another with which
   it is closely associated. E.g., ‘crown’ for ‘royalty’ is an example of metonymy. (correct)
10.Is the following correct or incorrect? A collocation is made up of two or more words that are
   commonly used together in English. E.g., ‘close a deal’, ‘open an account.’ (correct)
11.‘Close a deal’, ‘open an account’ are examples of _____. (collocation)
12.According to Searle (1976), all speech acts fall into _____ main types. (five)
13.The branch of linguistics dealing with language in use and the contexts in which it is used is
   called ______. (pragmatics)
14.Relationship between the linguistic form and communicative function is of central interest in
   _____. (pragmatics)
15.Use of one form with its asserted meaning is conventionally interpreted with the presupposition
   that another (non-asserted) meaning is also understood. This is the definition of which kind of
   presupposition? (Lexical)
16.Is the following correct or incorrect?
Pragmatics cannot vary from culture to culture. (Incorrect)

17.An act by which a speaker (or writer) uses language to enable a listener (or reader) to identify
   something is known as? (Reference)
