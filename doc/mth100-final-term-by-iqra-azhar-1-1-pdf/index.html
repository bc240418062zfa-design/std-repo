Q 13 the 3rd and the 4th terms of a geometric progression are 12 and 8 respectively. Find the
sum to infinity of the progression.
. Let's assume that the first term is 'a' and the common ratio is 'r'.

MTH100 Final Term subjective by Iqra Azhar
The 3rd term of the GP is given as 12, which means:
a * r^2 = 12
The 4th term of the GP is given as 8, which means:
a * r^3 = 8
Now, we can divide the second equation by the first equation to find the common ratio 'r':
(a * r^3) / (a * r^2) = 8 / 12
r = 8 / 12
r = 2/3
Now that we have the common ratio 'r', we can use it to find the first term 'a' using the first
equation:
a * (2/3)^2 = 12
a * 4/9 = 12
a = 12 * 9 / 4
a = 27
So, the first term 'a' is 27, and the common ratio 'r' is 2/3.
Now, to find the sum to infinity (S) of the geometric progression, we can use the formula:
S = a / (1 - r)
S = 27 / (1 - 2/3)
Now, let's calculate the sum:
S = 27 / (1/3)
S = 27 * 3
S = 81               So, the sum to infinity of the geometric progression is 81.
Question: 14 In how many ways can a team of 6 candidates can be formed out of a total of
10 candidates such that two particular candidates should be included in each department?
Step 1: Select the two particular candidates who must be included in each team. There are 2
ways to do this.

MTH100 Final Term subjective by Iqra Azhar

Step 2: Now, we need to select the remaining 4 candidates from the remaining 10 - 2 = 8
candidates. Since order doesn't matter, we will use combinations.The number of ways to
choose 4 candidates from 8 is denoted as "8 choose 4" and can be calculated using the
combination formula:
C(n, r) = n! / (r! * (n - r)!) where n is the total number of candidates and r is the number of
candidates we want to select.       C(8, 4) = 8! / (4! * (8 - 4)!)
C(8, 4) = 70
Step 3: Now, multiply the results from Step 1 and Step 2 to get the total number of ways:
Total number of ways = Step 1 * Step 2
Total number of ways = 2 * 70
Total number of ways = 140
So, there are 140 ways to form a team of 6 candidates, including two particular candidates in
each team, out of a total of 10 candidates.
