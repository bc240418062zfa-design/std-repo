Lesson-06

          Contact Induced Varieties of English
Topic 031: Pidgins and Creoles
What is a pidgin?
A pidgin is a simplified language that emerges when people with no common
language need to communicate.
Define "language contact."
Language contact is when speakers of different languages interact, resulting in
the transfer of linguistic features.
How does the term "pidgin" originate?
It originates from various sources, including a Chinese corruption of "business"
and the Portuguese word "ocupaçao."

What is a creole?
A creole is a pidgin that becomes the first language of a new generation of
speakers.
Explain the concept of "language contact" and its relevance to pidgins.
"Language contact" is a phenomenon where speakers of different languages
interact, leading to the transfer of linguistic features between them. In the
context of pidgins, language contact is essential because it is the interaction
between speakers of different languages that necessitates the creation of a
simplified communication system, or a pidgin. (Topic 031)
Topic 032: Process of Pidginization-I
What historical events contributed to pidginization?
European colonization in Africa and Asia, as well as New World slavery,
contributed to pidginization.
Why were slaves drawn from various language backgrounds?
Slaves were deliberately drawn from different language backgrounds to prevent
rebellion against their masters.
What is the process of pidginization?
Pidginization is the simplification of a language used by speakers separated by
different languages.
Explain the process of pidginization in terms of language simplification.
Pidginization involves the simplification of a language used by groups of speakers
who are separated from each other by different languages. This simplification
occurs because speakers of different languages must find a common way to
communicate, leading to the creation of a simplified linguistic system or pidgin.
(Topic 032)
Topic 033: Process of Pidginization-II
What characterizes the simplification of a dominant language in pidginization?

It involves simplifying grammar, using fewer words, and having a restricted range
of phonological and syntactic options.
Provide examples of words in a pidgin.
Examples include "bros" for friend, "show" for come or arrive, and "haus" for
house.
What are common views about pidgins?
Common views include considering pidgins as marginal, degenerate, or
imperfectly learned languages.
Can you provide examples of words from a pidgin language?
Certainly, in a pidgin language, words are often simplified and may include terms
like "bros" for friend, "show" for to come or arrive, and "haus" for house. These
examples illustrate the simplification of words in pidgin languages for easier
communication. (Topic 033)
Topic 034: Process of Creolization-I
How does a creole differ from a pidgin?
A creole evolves from a pidgin into a richer language and has native speakers.
What is the origin of the term "creole"?
It comes from the Portuguese word "criar," meaning to nurse, breed, or bring up.
What are the differences between pidgins and creoles in terms of vocabulary
and grammar?
Creoles have a larger vocabulary and more complicated grammatical resources
compared to pidgins.
Topic 035: Process of Creolization-II
Q: What is a creole continuum, and how does it arise?
A creole continuum arises from the relationship with a superstrate language, like
Standard English.
What is "decreolization"?

Decreolization is the process where an increase in varieties leads to more
influence from the Standard English, often involving social stratification.
What are the components of the creole continuum?
It includes the acrolect (high speech), mesolect (middle speech), and basilect (low
speech).
Topic 036: Geographical Distribution
Where are pidgins and creoles primarily distributed?
They are mainly found in the equatorial belt, including the Caribbean, the coasts
of South America, West Africa, and islands in the Indian and Pacific Oceans.
How many English-based pidgins and creoles are listed by Hancock?
Hancock lists 35 English-based pidgins and creoles out of a total of 127.
How does language distribution reflect the history of regions?
The language distribution reflects the social and political history of regions, with
some countries having only English-based creoles, and others having only French-
based ones.

Lesson-07

         Pidgins and Creoles: Theories of Origin
Topic 037: Independent Parallel Development Theory
What are the three main groups of Theories of Origin for pidgins and creoles?
Polygenesis (Evolved from various sources) 2. Monogenesis (Evolved from a single
source) 3. Universal strategies (Derived from universal strategies)
What does the Independent Parallel Development Theory (Polygenetic) suggest
about the development of pidgins and creoles?
Pidgins and creoles developed independently from a variety of origins, with
similarities due to shared circumstances of origin.

Topic 038: Nautical Jargon Theory
How did the Nautical Jargon Theory explain the development of pidgins?
It suggested that pidgins developed from a shipboard lingua franca influenced by
nautical jargon, passed on to different cultures through sailors on trading routes.
What are some characteristics of the Nautical Jargon Theory?
It consisted of a core vocabulary of nautical items and a simplified grammar.
Examples include "hivim" for "heave," "kapsite" for "capsize," and "haisim" for
"hoist."
