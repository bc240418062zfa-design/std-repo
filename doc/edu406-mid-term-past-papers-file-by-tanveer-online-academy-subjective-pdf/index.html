Why factual data is used in conceptual learning?

Answer:

Factual data is used in conceptual learning to provide a foundation of concrete knowledge,

making abstract ideas easier to understand. It helps learners build connections between theory

and real-world applications, enhancing comprehension and retention. By anchoring concepts

in facts, learners can critically analyze and apply these ideas effectively.

Roles of teacher in the classroom?

Answer:

In the classroom, a teacher acts as:

      Facilitator: Guides learning and encourages exploration.

      Mentor: Supports personal and academic growth.

      Role Model: Demonstrates positive behaviours.

      Organizer: Manages the classroom environment.

      Evaluator: Assesses progress and gives feedback.

Importance of critical thinking?

Answer:

Critical thinking is essential as it enables individuals to analyze information objectively, make

informed decisions, and solve problems effectively. It fosters independence, enhances

creativity, and promotes open-mindedness, allowing people to navigate complex issues, avoid

biases, and develop well-reasoned conclusions. Critical thinking is foundational for personal

growth, effective communication, and success in both academic and professional settings.

Definition of Reframing?

Answer:

Reframing refers to the process of looking at a situation, problem, or belief from a new

perspective to gain a different understanding. This technique helps challenge assumptions,

uncover alternative solutions, and broaden one’s viewpoint, leading to more effective problem-

Reflection in action and Reflection on action?

Answer:

Reflection-in-action: Occurs during the activity, where the individual thinks on their feet,

adjusting and making changes in real-time based on what’s happening. It’s an immediate,

adaptive response to challenges.

Reflection-on-action: Happens after the activity, where the individual reviews and analyzes

what occurred, identifying strengths, weaknesses, and areas for improvement. This reflection

aids in planning better strategies for future situations.

Personal Practice Theory (PPT)?

Answer:

PPT is the systematic set of beliefs (theories) guiding teachers’ practices (practical) that are

based on their prior life experiences (personal).
