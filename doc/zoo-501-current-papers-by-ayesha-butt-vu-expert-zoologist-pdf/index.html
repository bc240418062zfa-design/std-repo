**MCQs with Answers:**

1. Which factor controls the formation of myogenesis? (MyoD)
2. What are the disorders resulting from failure in neural tube closure? (Spina bifida
and anencephaly)
3. Which factors control the process of the cell cycle? (Cyclins and cyclin-dependent
kinases)
4. Describe the fertilization process in chickens. (Fertilization occurs in the
infundibulum of the oviduct, where the sperm fuses with the ovum)
5. What is the primary unit of MPF (Maturation Promoting Factor)? (Cyclin B-CDK1
complex)
6. What is the cleavage pattern in birds? (Discoidal meroblastic)
7. What is the role of cortical granules? (Prevent polyspermy by modifying the zona
pellucida after fertilization)
8. Describe the three vesicles formed from the brain during development.
(Prosencephalon, mesencephalon, and rhombencephalon)
9. What is the location of coniferous forests in Pakistan? (Northern regions, including
Khyber Pakhtunkhwa and Gilgit-Baltistan)
10. How many chromosomes does a human have? Options: 46, 23, 22 (46)
11. Sex without fertilization is common in which of the following? Options:
multicellular, unicellular, fungi, plants (Fungi)
12. What is sperm? (The male gamete responsible for fertilizing the female ovum)

13. Which organs develop from the embryonic ectoderm? (Nervous system, skin, and
sensory organs)
14. During primary neurulation, cells present at the dorsal-most portion of the neural
tube become which type of cells? (Neural crest cells)
15. Sea urchins exhibit which type of cleavage? (Radial holoblastic)
16. The myelencephalon eventually becomes which part of the brain? (Medulla
oblongata)
17. Fertilization occurs in which part of the oviduct, a region close to the ovary?
(Ampulla)
18. Teratology is the study of what? (Birth defects and abnormalities in development)
19. One agent of ventral specification is which molecule? (Sonic hedgehog)
20. In birds, what is critical in determining the anterior-posterior axis? (Gravity)
21. What is the significance of fertilization? (Combines genetic material from two
parents to create genetic diversity)
22. Describe the difference between mantle and ventricular zones. (Mantle zone contains
neuronal cell bodies; ventricular zone contains proliferating neural progenitor cells)
23. Explain the regulation of gene expression. (Involves transcriptional, post-
transcriptional, translational, and post-translational mechanisms)
24. What is the role of the enhancer if mutated? (A mutated enhancer can disrupt the
regulation of gene expression, leading to reduced or altered gene activity)
