   6. Methods of plowing and cultivation in cultural

Time and method of plowing and cultivating form a fourth factor in the cultural control
of insects.
1. Many insect pests overwinter in larval or pupal cells in the ground
2. These cells can often be broken up by fall plowing so the insects will not survive the winter

3. Cultivation in the summer frequently breaks up pupation cells
4. But plowing or cultivation to be effective must be timed according to the life history of the
   insect that is being controlled, otherwise it is useless.
5. For the control of some insects it is necessary to refrain from cultivation at certain times
6. The depth of plowing varies sometimes according to the habits of the insect.
