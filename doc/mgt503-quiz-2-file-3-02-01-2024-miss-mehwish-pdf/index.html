1. Which one of the following is a weakness of MBO?

 Options:

 A) Clarify priorities and expectation

 B) Coordination of goals and plans

 C) Requires training of employees

 D) Foster employee motivation

 Correct Answer: C) Requires training of employees

2. When objectives are not written down or rarely verbalized, and the planning is general and lacks
continuity, which of the following types of planning is used?

 Options:

 A) Economic planning
 B) Informal planning

 C) Environmental planning
 D) Formal planning

 Correct Answer: B) Informal planning

3. A form of group decision making in which a group is used to achieve a consensus of expert opinion is
called:
 Options:

 A) Interacting group

 B) Nominal group

 C) Brainstorming

 D) Delphi technique

 Correct Answer: D) Delphi technique

4. PERT Chart assists in ___________ the tasks within an organization.

 Options:
 A) Scheduling

 B) Finalizing

 C) Evaluating

 D) Budgeting

 Correct Answer: A) Scheduling

5. Which one of the following is a strength of MBO (Management by Objective)?

 Options:
 A) Requires training of employees

 B) Clarify priorities and expectation
 C) Overemphasis of quantitative goals

 D) Tends to falter without strong commitment
 Correct Answer: B) Clarify priorities and expectation

6. The action stage in the strategic management process is:

 Options:

 A) Strategy evaluation
 B) Strategy analysis

 C) Strategy implementation

 D) Strategy formulation

 Correct Answer: C) Strategy implementation

7. Which of the following is a philosophy based on converting organizational objectives into personal
objectives?

 Options:

 A) JIT (Just-In-Time)

 B) OPO (Objectives and Key Results)
 C) MBO (Management by Objectives)

 D) ROI (Return on Investment)
 Correct Answer: C) MBO (Management by Objectives)

8. MBO (Management by Objective) makes ______ objectives by devising a process in which they
cascade down through the organization.

 Options:

 A) Directional
 B) Strategic

 C) Realistic

 D) Operational

 Correct Answer: D) Operational

9. Which of the following BEST describes directional plans?

 Options:
 A) Identify general guidelines

 B) Meet the needs of a unique situation
 C) Have clearly defined objectives

 D) Last for 3–5 years
 Correct Answer: A) Identify general guidelines

10. Goals are often called which of the following?

  Options:

  A) A lot of other names by various organizations

  B) Managements’ do or die objectives

  C) The basis for all the other functions

  D) The foundation of planning

  Correct Answer: D) The foundation of planning
