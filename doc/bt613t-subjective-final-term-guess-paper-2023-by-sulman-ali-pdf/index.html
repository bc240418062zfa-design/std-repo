Role of Cytokinesis on plants:
Cytokinesis in plants is the process of cell division where the cytoplasm divides, creating two
daughter cells. It ensures growth, tissue repair, and reproduction by distributing cellular
components, enabling development and maintaining plant structure.

Role of Cytokinins on plants:
Cytokinin, a vital plant hormone, plays a multifaceted role in plant growth and development. It
promotes cell division, influencing shoot formation, leaf expansion, and branching. Cytokinin
regulates nutrient transport, enhancing root growth and nutrient uptake. It also affects processes
such as seed germination, delayed senescence, and flowering, contributing to overall plant vigor,
adaptation, and reproductive success.

1= Plant cell suspensions consist of cells with diverse morphology and state of aggregation. Two
morphological types of cells can be distinguished: 2= Cell aggregates made up of small cells, 3=
Large and elongated single cells, 4= Proportion of the cell type depends on the passage of culture
and nature of auxin.

What are environmental effects of pesticides?

Widespread use of pesticides in agriculture has experts worried due to their long term
environmental damage. Environmental effects of pesticides include ecotoxicity, water
contamination, soil degradation, air pollution, disruption of natural ecosystems, and impacts on
beneficial insects, birds, and aquatic organisms.

What are the precautions to use Murashige and Skoog medium?
Take precautions: Adjust medium pH before adding the gelling agent—acidic pH yields semi-
solid flowing gel, alkaline pH forms hardened gel. Use distilled/tissue culture grade water to
prevent salt precipitation and ensure proper gelation. Avoid concentrated solutions to prevent salt
precipitation.

Sever 6 symptoms of pesticides poisoning?
Symptoms of severe poisoning includes vomiting, convulsions, loss of reflexes,
unconsciousness, inability to breathe, fever muscle twitching, thirst, constriction of eye pupils
(eye pupils become small), increased rate of breathing.

Drawbacks of pesticides on environment?
Pesticides can harm the environment through various means: contaminating soil and water,
harming non-target organisms, disrupting ecosystems, and leading to pesticide-resistant pests.
Additionally, they can affect human health and biodiversity, posing long-term ecological risks
and necessitating cautious and sustainable pest management approaches.

Advantage of GM Crops:
Genetically modified (GM) crops offer several benefits: increased crop yield and resilience,
reduced need for chemical pesticides, enhanced nutritional content, and prolonged shelf life.
They can address food security challenges, minimize environmental impact, and support
sustainable agriculture. However, careful regulation is essential to ensure safety, prevent
unintended consequences, and maintain biodiversity.

Method of regeneration:

cultured on nutrient media containing hormones to induce cell division and differentiation. This
results in callus formation, which is then stimulated to form shoots or roots. These plantlets are
eventually transplanted to soil. Regeneration is crucial for cloning, breeding, and propagation of
plants with desired traits.
