communication.

Five disadvantages of distance education include limited social interaction, technology
dependency, learner isolation, less immediate feedback, and self-discipline challenges.

Five guidelines for data collection: define objectives clearly, choose appropriate tools, ensure
participant consent, maintain confidentiality, and validate collected data through triangulation.

Five types of business letters include inquiry letters, complaint letters, adjustment letters, order
letters, and sales letters.

and project review meetings.

straightforward and purposeful language use.

Activities to practice for increasing awareness of multiculturalism include storytelling from

Bad news letter communicates unpleasant or negative information, structured with a buffer

Cultural biases, humor, animals — how we deal with this? These elements should be handled

Describe High-Context culture: In high-context cultures (e.g., Japan, Arab countries),
communication relies heavily on implicit messages, shared context, and non-verbal cues.
Relationships and trust are crucial, and words often have multiple layers of meaning.

Difference between Masculine culture and feminine culture: Masculine cultures emphasize
competition, achievement, and assertiveness (e.g., USA, Germany), while feminine cultures
value cooperation, modesty, care, and quality of life (e.g., Sweden, Norway).

Difference between master degree and doctoral research: Master’s research is usually
application-based, shorter, and less original, while doctoral research is longer, original, and
contributes new knowledge to the field.
