Case: Agriculture is the primary source of livelihood for the rural population and ensures food availability to rural and urban inhabitants.
It is an important economic sector since it supplies raw materials to the nation's major industries and contributes significantly to export
revenue. But since independence (1947), Pakistan agriculture sector has continued to be influenced by traditional landlords or "jagirdars"
and influential landowning families. The influence of jagirdars has hindered the modernization of the agriculture sector. Traditional
farming methods, limited access to credit and technology, and the resistance to change can slow agricultural development. Efforts have
been made over the years to address these issues through land reform programs, changes in land tenure systems, and pro-poor agricultural
policies. However, the legacy of jagirdari systems and the power of influential landowners continue to shape the agriculture sector and
rural society in many parts of Pakistan.
Requirement:
Being the students of the “Issues in the economy of Pakistan”, Discuss the root causes of feudalism in agriculture sector of Pakistan.What
initiatives (except mentioned above) should be taken by the government to eliminate the feudalism practices in the agriculture
sector? (Discuss any two)
Answer:
The feudalism in the agriculture sector of Pakistan can be attributed to several root causes. Firstly, the historical influence of jagirdars,
who were traditional landlords, and influential landowning families has hindered the modernization of the sector. Their power and
control over land have perpetuated a system of patronage and dependency, where small farmers are dependent on them for resources

and support. This has created a power imbalance and limited the opportunities for small farmers to innovate and improve their
productivity.
Another cause is the resistance to change and the adherence to traditional farming methods. Many landowners and farmers are reluctant
to adopt modern techniques, such as mechanization and improved irrigation systems, due to the lack of awareness, access to credit and
technology, and fear of financial risks. This resistance to change further perpetuates the feudal system and hampers agricultural
development.
To eliminate feudalism practices in the agriculture sector, the government can take several initiatives. Firstly, it should focus on
implementing effective land reforms and changing the land tenure system. This would help in redistributing land ownership and breaking
the concentration of power in the hands of a few influential landowners. It would provide small farmers with greater access to land,
resources, and opportunities.
Secondly, the government should promote the use of modern agricultural techniques and provide support to farmers in terms of training,
credit facilities, and access to technology. This would help in increasing productivity, improving efficiency, and reducing the dependence
on traditional landlords. It would empower small farmers and enable them to compete in the market on an equal footing.
Addressing the root causes of feudalism in the agriculture sector of Pakistan requires a comprehensive approach that combines land
reforms, modernization of farming practices, and support for small farmers. By implementing these initiatives, the government can
create a more inclusive and equitable agricultural sector that promotes economic development and reduces dependency on feudal
practices.
