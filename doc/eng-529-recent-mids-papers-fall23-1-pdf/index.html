Q. Define 2 techniques/Features of Javascript?
Two prominent features of JavaScript are:
Closures: Closures allow functions to remember the variables and parameters in
the scope where they were created. They enable inner functions to access the
variables of their outer function, even after the outer function has finished
executing. This feature is powerful for creating modular and encapsulated code.
Asynchronous programming with Promises or Async/Await: JavaScript
supports asynchronous operations using Promises or the Async/Await syntax. This
allows for non-blocking code execution, enabling tasks like fetching data from a
server, reading files, or performing time-consuming operations without freezing the
entire program. This asynchronous capability helps in building responsive and
efficient applications.

Write down a note on CMC( computer madiated communication)
Computer-Mediated Communication (CMC) refers to the exchange of information,
ideas, or emotions through digital devices. It encompasses various forms of
communication facilitated by technology, including emails, instant messaging,
social media, video conferences, and online forums.
Key aspects of CMC:

      •   Accessibility and Convenience
      •   Diverse Communication Channels
      •   Anonymity and Identity
      •   Synchronous and Asynchronous Communication
      •   Impact on Social Dynamics
      •   Challenges and Considerations
CMC has significantly transformed how individuals, businesses, and societies
communicate, fostering a digital landscape where interactions occur at a rapid pace
across diverse platforms and mediums.
