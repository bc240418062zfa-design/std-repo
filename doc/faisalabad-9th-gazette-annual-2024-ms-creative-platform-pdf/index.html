  BOARD OF INTERMEDIATE AND SECONDARY EDUCATION, FAISALABAD
                                          1. INTRODUCTION
      The main function of the Board of Intermediate & Secondary Education (BISE) Faisalabad is to
conduct examinations for the award of certificates / Diplomas i.e. Secondary School Certificate (SSC),
Intermediate Certificate (HSSC) and Pakistani / Classical Languages to the successful candidates. In
addition, the BISE Faisalabad also conducts other examinations as referred by the Controlling Authority /
Government of Punjab.

MESSAGE OF THE CHAIRPERSON
      Establishment of educational culture is our national aspiration. Human resource development, talent
promotion and overall societal uplift are its direct outcomes. Examinations & Evaluation system is the key
indicator of the success of any educational system. BISE Faisalabad is committed to achieving this assigned
task by establishing its integrity through a policy of fair play, transparency and opportunity to all. Right
from its inception, this body is striving for the promotion of educational norms through the delivery of
high-quality service and erudite grooming of the curricular and co-curricular potential of the student by
sticking to its statutory framework and professional obligations. On behalf of all my working partners, I
affirm a strong commitment to our assigned goals and objectives.

     The schedule of Secondary School Certificate 9th Class (First Annual) Examination, 2024 was notified
