Penetrance and Expressivity

Penetrance measures the proportion of individuals in a population who carry a specific gene and
express the related trait. Less than 100% penetrance is a result of modifiers, epistasis,
suppressors, and environmental conditions.

In genetics, expressivity is the degree to which a phenotype is expressed by individuals having a
particular genotype. (Alternately, it may refer to the expression of particular gene by individuals
having a certain phenotype.)

Low and full Penetrant

Low Penetrance An allele with low penetrance will only sometimes produce the symptom or trait with
which it has been associated at a detectable level. In low penetrance, it is difficult to distinguish
environmental from genetic factors.

Highly Penetrant If an allele is highly penetrant, and then the trait it produces will almost always be
apparent in an individual carrying the allele.
