   2. Coniferous Forests Climate :
Climate is cold. Long and harsh Winters is for more than six months. Mean annual temperature is
below 0°C,
   3. Climate of Temperate Deciduous Forests :
 These forests occur in the areas of moderate climatic conditions such as Annual rainfall is 75 to 150
cm, Winter lasts for four to six months, Temperature ranges between 10 to 20°C.
   4. Distribution of marine ecosystem
Marine ecosystem covers nearly 71 % of the earth’s surface with an average depth of about 4000 m.
Fresh water rivers eventually empty into ocean. Salinity of open sea is 3.6% is quite constant. Sodium
and chlorine-86% of the sea salt and the rest is due other elements such as sulphur, magnesium,
potassium and calcium.
   5. Climate of marine ecosystem
Temperatures in the ocean range from just around freezing at the pole and in the deep waters, to
tropical clear waters that are as warm as a bathtub. The average temperature of all oceans is about
39°F (4°C). Heat from the sun warms only the surface of the water. Deep down, oceans everywhere
are cold and dark.
   6. Sand Dunes:
  Extensive areas of undulating sand dunes, absence of cultivation and extensive semi desert.
  Thal desert, Thar and Cholistan desert, Nuski and Chagai sund dunes areas.
   7. Ecosphere
- largest and nearly self sufficient biological system Including all living organisms of earth
interacting with the physical environment (Lithosphere-the Soil, Hydrosphere-water, Atmosphere-air
whereas Biosphere- life zone of the earth)
Ecosphere = Biosphere + Lithosphere + Hydrosphere + Atmosphere)
   8. Define ecology and geology
Ecology (from Greek word: οἶκος, “house”, or “environment“ and logos means “study of”) 2. Ecology
is the scientific study of the interactions between organisms and their environment
Geology is the scientific study of the Earth’s solid materials, including rocks, minerals, and the
processes that shape the Earth’s structure.

   o all organisms living on Earth
   o all physical and chemical surroundings.
   o for this reason, the field is usually divided into different levels of study including: organismal
     ecology, population ecology, community ecology and ecosystem ecology.

   11.         Decomposers
Feed on dead and decayed plants or animals. Make up the final tropic level in food chain. They
decompose the dead and decay matter and helps in recycling the nutrients.
They are classified into two class
   1) Micro-Decomposers: bacteria’ fungi’ protozoa
   2) Macro-Decomposers: earth worm’ nematodes’ molluscals

   12.         Difference b/w directly and indirectly available resources.
Directly available resources are those that can be readily accessed and utilized by organisms
without the need for any additional processing or conversion. Examples include sunlight, air, water,
and food sources like fruits or nuts.
 Indirectly available resources, on the other hand, require some form of processing or
conversion before they can be used by organisms. Examples include wood that needs to be converted
into fuel or building materials, minerals that need to be extracted and refined, or agricultural crops
that need to be harvested and processed before they can be consumed.
   13.    How the abundance of ozone in stratosphere and troposphere
     determined?
Ozone abundances in the stratosphere and troposphere are determined by,
   o     Amount of reactive gases
   o     Sunlight intensity
   o     Temperature

   14.         Causes of global warming
   o   Pollution from coal, natural gas and oil
   o   Carbon dioxide from airplanes and vehicles
   o   Population increase
   o   Deforestation

   15.         Effect of global warming
Global warming means that the Earth is getting hotter because of human activities. When the Earth
gets hotter, it causes problems like rising sea levels, really hot days, and changes in weather patterns.
It can also hurt animals and plants by changing where they live and making it harder for them to
survive.
   16.         What are the Salient features of Baluchistan desert scrub forest .
Baluchistan Desert Scrub Forests:
Usually associated with higher hills, stony plateaus. Very cold winters, no monsoon influence but
occasional winter and spring showers. Typified by Northern Kalat, lower parts of Suleiman hills, most
of the Balochistan valleys, Kurram valley, most of Waziristan. Heavily overgrazed.
Fauna: Hill fox, Balochistan race of Black bear, Hyaena, Leopard, Marbled Pole Cat, caracal Cat,
Urial, Goitred Gazella, Tibetan Hare, porcupine, Migratory Hamster, Bolachistan Gerbil
