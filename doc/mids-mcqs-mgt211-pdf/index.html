Theme 1 - The Human Journey of Business — From the Stone Age to the Age of AI

Q1. Which of the following describes the Neolithic Revolution’s impact on business?
A) Rise of barter systems
B) Start of industrialization
C) Use of AI in trade
D) Emergence of stock markets
✅ Correct Answer: A
💡The Neolithic Revolution led to agriculture and surplus trade, giving rise to early barter
systems.

Q2. What was the key economic activity during the Stone Age?
A) Manufacturing
B) Hunting and gathering
C) Agriculture
D) Trading with coins
✅ Correct Answer: B
💡 Early humans survived by hunting animals and gathering food before organized trade began.

Q3. Which ancient civilization used the Nile River as a trade route and maintained state-run
commerce?
A) Indus Valley
B) Mesopotamia
C) Egypt
D) China
✅ Correct Answer: C
💡 Egypt used the Nile for transportation and trade, with a structured system managed by the
state.

Q4. The “double coincidence of wants” problem was associated with:
A) Credit-based systems
B) Barter trade
C) Banking
D) Use of currency
✅ Correct Answer: B
💡 Barter systems required each trader to have what the other wanted, making transactions
inefficient.

Q5. Which of the following was a major innovation of the First Industrial Revolution?
A) Assembly line
B) Steam engine
C) Internet
D) Artificial Intelligence
✅ Correct Answer: B
💡 Steam power and mechanization transformed industries during the First Industrial Revolution.

Q6. Which period marked the shift from agrarian economies to factory-based production?
A) Stone Age
B) First Industrial Revolution
C) Third Industrial Revolution
D) Information Age
✅ Correct Answer: B
💡 The First Industrial Revolution mechanized production, replacing agriculture as the main
economic base.

Q7. The Silk Road connected China with which major region for trade?
A) Africa
B) Europe
C) Australia
D) America
✅ Correct Answer: B
💡 The Silk Road linked China with Europe, facilitating trade in silk, tea, and spices.

Q8. Which invention greatly improved communication during the Second Industrial Revolution?
A) Printing press
B) Telegraph
C) Radio
D) Internet
✅ Correct Answer: B
💡 The telegraph revolutionized long-distance communication and business coordination.

Q9. The Third Industrial Revolution was mainly driven by:
A) Coal power

B) AI and robotics
C) Computers and automation
D) Steam engines
✅ Correct Answer: C
💡 The digital revolution introduced computers, automation, and early internet technologies.

Q10. The Fourth Industrial Revolution is best characterized by:
A) Water-powered mills
B) Assembly lines
C) Artificial Intelligence and IoT
D) Railroads
✅ Correct Answer: C
💡 Industry 4.0 integrates AI, smart systems, and interconnected devices for automated
operations.

Q11. Which of the following is an example of a Fourth Industrial Revolution company?
A) Ford Motor (1910s)
B) Tesla Gigafactory
C) East India Company
D) Nokia (1990s)
✅ Correct Answer: B
💡 Tesla uses AI-driven robotics and data analytics — hallmarks of the Fourth Industrial
Revolution.

Q12. Which of the following represents an example of early record-keeping in trade?
A) Computer databases
B) Cuneiform tablets
C) Blockchain
D) Scrolls in Latin
✅ Correct Answer: B
💡 Mesopotamians used clay tablets and cuneiform to record trade transactions.

Q13. What does the “Age of AI” primarily emphasize in business today?
A) Manual labor and production
B) Machine learning and automation
C) Agricultural practices
D) Gold-based trading systems

✅ Correct Answer: B
💡 Businesses use machine learning and automation to enhance efficiency and decision-making.

Q14. The Agora in ancient Greece was primarily known as:
A) A religious site
B) A marketplace and public forum
C) A government palace
D) A shipyard
✅ Correct Answer: B
💡 The Agora served as both a market and a meeting place for trade and discussion.

Q15. Which ancient civilization emphasized trust (“xin”) as a core business ethic?
A) Mesopotamia
B) Egypt
C) China
D) Indus Valley
✅ Correct Answer: C
💡 Chinese traders valued sincerity and trust, key elements in long-term trade relations.

Q16. The concept of "ethical capitalism" is predicted to emerge in which industrial era?
A) First
B) Second
C) Fourth
D) Fifth
✅ Correct Answer: D
💡 The Fifth Industrial Revolution is expected to blend human ethics with advanced technology.

Q17. A major feature of industrial revolutions has been:
A) Decline in global trade
B) Stagnation in technology
C) Transformation in business operations
D) Return to barter systems
✅ Correct Answer: C
💡 Each revolution changed how businesses produce, manage, and deliver goods and services.

Q18. The term “bio-digital fusion” refers to:
A) Merger of agriculture and digital farming
B) Integration of biology and digital technology
C) Use of fertilizers in production
D) Trade between biotech firms
✅ Correct Answer: B
💡 Bio-digital fusion combines biological science and technology for human-machine
collaboration.

Q19. The earliest form of currency emerged to solve which problem?
A) Overproduction
B) Lack of transportation
C) Barter inefficiency
D) Inflation
✅ Correct Answer: C
💡 Money replaced barter by providing a common medium of exchange.

Q20. Which phase of the business cycle follows a “trough”?
A) Recession
B) Expansion
C) Peak
D) Depression
✅ Correct Answer: B
💡 After a trough, economic recovery begins with increased spending and production.

Q21. The Indus Valley Civilization contributed to business history through:
A) Steam-powered tools
B) Standardized weights and measures
C) Use of gold coins
D) Papyrus contracts
✅ Correct Answer: B
💡 The Indus Valley used standardized trade weights for fair exchanges.

Q22. “Blockchain” technology in business today is most similar to which ancient practice?
A) Oral agreements
B) Ledger recording
C) Barter trade

D) Handicrafts
✅ Correct Answer: B
💡 Blockchain serves as a modern, digital ledger for recording transactions securely.
