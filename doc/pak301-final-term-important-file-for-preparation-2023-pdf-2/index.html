      • A) Lahore Resolution
      • B) Objectives Resolution
      • C) Nehru Report
      • D) Mountbatten Plan
      • Answer: B) Objectives Resolution
      • Explanation: The Objectives Resolution provided parameters and principles for the constitutional
          development of Pakistan.
6. Question: According to the Objectives Resolution, who holds sovereignty over the universe?
      • A) The people of Pakistan
      • B) The President of Pakistan
      • C) Almighty Allah
      • D) The Prime Minister of Pakistan
      • Answer: C) Almighty Allah
      • Explanation: The Objectives Resolution declared that sovereignty over the entire universe belongs to
          Almighty Allah alone.
7. Question: What was the main objection raised by non-Muslims against the Objectives Resolution?
      • A) Promotion of extremism
      • B) Mixing religion and politics
      • C) Lack of representation
      • D) Lack of autonomy for provinces
      • Answer: B) Mixing religion and politics
      • Explanation: Non-Muslims objected to the perceived mixing of religion and politics in the Objectives
          Resolution.
8. Question: Which political philosophy was emphasized by the Objectives Resolution?
      • A) Western democracy
      • B) Secularism
      • C) Sovereignty of the people
      • D) Sovereignty of Allah
      • Answer: D) Sovereignty of Allah
      • Explanation: The Objectives Resolution emphasized the sovereignty of Allah as a distinctive political
          philosophy.
