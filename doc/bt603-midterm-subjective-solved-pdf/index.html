Q: Define Henry’s Law?

Henry’s Law describes the solubility of O2 in nutrient solution in relation to the O2 partial pressure in the gas phase
                         Po
                  C* =
                         H
C* is the oxygen saturation concentration of the nutrient solution,

Po is the partial pressure of the gas in the gas phase

H is Henry's constant, which is specific for the gas and the liquid phase.

Q:   What is Replica Plating Method? (5)
Mutants can be detected by selecting or testing for an altered phenotype. Positive selection involves the selection of
mutant cells and rejection of non-mutated cells. To identify mutants that have gained some physical trait, Replica plating
is used for negative selection. Negative selection identifies mutants that have lost some physical trait - for example,
auxotrophs that have nutritional requirements not possessed by the parent (nonmutated) cell.
