Enlist the demands of madaaris reforms.

Madaaris often demand for following reforms:

   1. The wafaq degrees of Matric (Sanwiya Aama) and Intermediate (Sanwiya
      Khaasa) are not approved yet, whereas its higher degree, Shahadat ul-
      Aalmia is recognized as equal to MA Islamic Studies and Arabic. The
      government should accept these lower-level degrees as equal to the formal
      educational system.
   2. The wafaq should be affiliated with the Federal Ministry of Education or
      Federal Ministry of Religious Affairs. Despite passing of the 18th
      amendment, the madaaris prefer to remain part of the federal government.
      Madaaris should also link with the Higher Education Commission (a
      Government higher studies oversight body) and all education grants
      should be distributed among madaaris through it.

   3. The Madaaris should be exempted from all utility bills, and this will be
      the best way of cooperation from government side.
   4. Obstacles of bureaucracy for registration of madaaris should be removed
      in the registration process.
   5. Opportunities should be provided for madaaris students for easy
      admission in universities and participation in competitive exams.
   6. The government should provide opportunities of job to wafaq students in
      all its departments.

Mdraasa and politics?
Religious schools, known as madrassas, play a significant role in Pakistani
politics, especially through their connections with religious political parties.
These parties, representing different sects like Deobandis, Barelvis, Ahl-e-
Hadith, and Shia, have strong ties to specific madrassas. A study found that a
majority of madrassas have political affiliations, with most linked to religious
political parties. While some madrassa boards prefer to stay apolitical, others like
Rabita ul-Madaris support specific political parties. It is suggested that students
focus on their education and avoid active political involvement until completing
their studies to maintain a balance between education and politics.
Q. Peace education history elements?
The history of peace education includes efforts after major conflicts, like the
World Wars, to teach people how to resolve disputes without violence. Key
elements involve teaching about human rights, promoting understanding and
tolerance among different cultures, and developing skills for conflict resolution.

Impact of Literacy on social environment?
When people can read and write, they can better understand information, communicate
effectively, and participate in society. Higher literacy rates can lead to more informed citizens,
improved economic opportunities, and a stronger sense of community. It helps reduce poverty
and promotes social equality.
