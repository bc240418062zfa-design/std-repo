There are four main kinds of scales and numerous variations of each kind.

   10.        Limnology
Limnology is the study of the structural and functional interrelationships of organisms of inland
waters as they are affected by their dynamic physical, chemical, and biotic environments.

   11.        Middle /marginal feeder
 Marginal feeders prefers algae and other submerged vegetation, • • And is primarily a herbivorous
column feeder. • • Example: • • Rohu

   12.        Parasitic Feeder
A parasitic feeder is an organism that feeds on another organism, known as the host, without killing
it. The parasitic feeder benefits from the relationship, while the host is harmed.
Example:
   -   Tapeworms (parasite) feed on the nutrients absorbed by the intestines of their host (e.g.,
       humans, animals)
   -   Lice (parasite) feed on the blood, skin, or feathers of their host (e.g., humans, birds)
   -   Leeches (parasite) feed on the blood of their host (e.g., fish, humans)

   13.        What are dorsal fins and their functions in locomotion
Dorsal fins are located on the back or on the top of the fish, and aid the fish in sharp turns or stops,
and assist the fish in rolling. Fish may have up to three distinct dorsal fins, known as proximal,
middle , and distal dorsal fins, however several fish have just two dorsal fins with the middle and
distal fins fused together.

   14.        What is caudal peduncle?
The caudal peduncle is the base of the caudal fin. Peduncle means stem, and the caudal peduncle is
where the strong swimming muscles of the tail are found. Together, the caudal fin acts like a
“propeller” for the fish, and the caudal peduncle acts like a motor.

   15.        What do you know about fugu poisoning?/Pufferfish Poisoning
Tetrodotoxic poisoning occurs following the ingestion of pufferfish, boxfish or Porcupine fish. This is
also known as puffer or fugu poisoning. The poisoning is due to toxin known as tetrodotoxin which is
concentrated in the internal organs such as liver, ovary and intestines. The skin also contains some
glands which secrete these toxins. Usually the body muscles are free of the toxin.

   16.         Examples of Herbivorous fishes
   o Parrotfish
   o Damselfish
   o Surgeonfish
Species of these herbivorous fish include: parrotfish, which eat corals that break Off the reef and
excrete them as white sand; damselfish, which fend off the Macro algae growth that kills coral; and
surgeonfish, whose most famous representative is Dory from Finding Nemo.

   –      Chondrichthyes: includes all cartilaginous fishes
