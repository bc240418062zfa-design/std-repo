What is ecological niche.
An ecological niche describes how a species lives and interacts with other organisms in a habitat.
It can be thought of as the role or job that a species has in nature.

Characters of Bacteria:
Bacteria play many roles in our ecosystem. Bacteria are decomposers which break down dead
material and recycle it. By decomposing dead organic matter, they contribute to soil structure
and the cycles of nature. Bacteria provide nutrients like nitrogen and phosphorus to plants as well
as to produce growth hormones. They are also needed for the carbon and nitrogen cycles.

Layers of atmosphere and their heights:
     Exosphere: 700 to 10,000 km (440 to 6,200 miles)
     Thermosphere: 80 to 700 km (50 to 440 miles)
     Mesosphere: 50 to 80 km (31 to 50 miles)
     Stratosphere: 12 to 50 km (7 to 31 miles)
     Troposphere: 0 to 12 km (0 to 7 miles)

Structure and Function of Golgi Apparatus.
Structure:
It is Located near the end of the ER close to the nucleus and Composed of several layers of
cisternae (fluid-filled sacs) • It Has three different parts: 1. cis-Golgi 2. medial-Golgi 3. trans-
Golgi
Functions:
1= cis is first cisternae. 2= trans is final cisternae. 3= From trans, proteins are packaged into
vesicles destined to lysosomes, secretory vesicles, or the cell surface. Transport vesicles are used

to move back and forth between the ER and Golgi bodies 4= One side of the Golgi body receives
transport vesicles produced by the ER. 5= This side is called cis face. 6= Opposite side releasing
vesicle is trans face.

What is the difference between Analogy & homology?
Homology:
Homology involves the study of organs that have evolved from same origin or from common
ancestor but have different functions. It is a Divergent evolution.
For example:
    Wings of bat and Hands of humans.
Analogy:
Analogy involves the study of organs that are evolved from different ancestors but perform a
similar function. It is a Convergent Evolution.
For example:
    Wings of bat and wings of insects.

Reproduction in arthropods?
Arthropods reproduce by sexual reproduction, which involves the generation and fusion of
gametes. Most arthropods are either male or female, and they undergo internal fertilization. Once

the egg has been fertilized, the female usually lays the egg, and it continues developing outside
of the mother’s body.

Reproductive System:
1= Mostly dioecious 2= Paired genital opening 3= Male often packages sperm in a
spermatophore 4= Transfer them into the female genital tract 5= Copulation occurs. 6= Sperms
are transferred via modified pedipalp.

What is autolysis and putrefaction:
Autolysis: Breaking down of tissues by the body's own internal chemicals and enzymes
Putrefaction: Breakdown of tissues by bacteria. And releases compounds such as cadaverine
and putrescine causing decaying odor.

Write a note on rate of evolution:
The rate of evolution is quantified as the speed of genetic or morphological change in a lineage
over a period of time. The speed at which a molecular entity (such as a protein, gene, etc.)
evolves is of considerable interest in evolutionary biology.

Carbon cycle:
    Carbon enters the atmosphere as CO2.
    CO2 is absorbed by autotrophs such as green plants.
    Animals consume plants, thereby, incorporating carbon into their system.
    Animals and plants die, their bodies decompose and carbon is reabsorbed back into the
     atmosphere.
