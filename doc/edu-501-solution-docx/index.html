Question 1:
       Write few strategies which help you to build parents- teacher relationships?
Answer:
Children spend a large part of the day with their teachers. Teachers and children form an important bond during the school year, and children often look to their teachers as mentors and confidants. As a parent, it is important for you to have a strong parent-teacher relationship in order to help your child succeed as well as monitor their progress and development.
Here are some techniques to help you develop that relationship:
    1) Communication:
Communicate with your child's teacher at the beginning of the school year and throughout the school year. This can be done through a friendly email where you introduce yourself and tell them a little about your child. You can let the teacher know that you would appreciate feedback on your child's progress. This will show the teacher from the start that you want to play a role in your child's education.

    2) Let the teacher know if there is something happen at home:
Teachers have no way of knowing the personal problems that are taking place in a child's life. Perhaps the child's parents are divorcing and the child is reacting by acting out at school. If you are a parent, the best thing you can do is prevent the problem and inform the child's teacher. A teacher can recommend strategies for your child to better cope with this difficult situation and will be more understanding if they see certain behaviors.

    3) Ask teacher for guidelines:
 If your child is struggling to understand a concept and you are having trouble explaining it or helping them with their homework, talk to the teacher for tactics. Maybe your child needs one-on-one time with their teacher in the form of tutoring or maybe your teacher can give your child extra exercises to practice at home.

    4) Make sure your child has a proper routine:
When your kids come home from school, set aside homework time and put away technology. You can talk to your child's teachers about strategies to ensure your child completes his work effectively.
    5) Read everything that teachers sent at home:
Go through your child's backpack with them to see what assignments and homework they have to complete. A forgetful young child might forget homework so when they get home they should empty their bag and see what they have to work on overnight.
    6) Attend meetings:
During Corona, this can be done on Zoom app about your child's efforts, interests, strengths and challenges. Don't just ask about their grades, ask how they are doing socially and developmentally. You can discuss what goals you and your child want to achieve during the year.

Question 2:
         In your opinion, which leadership style is best to develop motivations among teachers?
Answer:
     The ability or skill to lead a group of peoples in an institute or organization.
Following are the different types of leadership styles:
    • Autocratic leadership
    • Democratic leadership
    • Transactional leadership
    • Transformational leadership
    • Laissez- faire leadership
    • Strategic leadership
    • Coach style leadership
    • Bureaucratic leadership
Among all of the leadership style, transformational leadership style is best to develop motivation among teachers because this kind of leadership always focuses on transformation and improvement of functions and capabilities. There may be tasks and schedules assigned, and leaders who follow this style may ask employees to constantly push their boundaries. Most growth oriented companies tend to adopt this kind of leadership style.
Burns (1978) understanding of transformational leadership showed that this theory differs fundamentally from other leadership theories in its orientation to a long term vision, focusing on the development of teacher’s motivation and thereby transforming teachers into leaders and moral agents. By following the literature research related to the results of leadership on the performance of educational institutions, it can be concluded about the high productivity of transformational leadership, which entails a change in the culture of the organization for the sake of its effectiveness and efficiency.
With a collaborative approach, transformational leaders empower their school teachers to have a say in decision making processes and enable joint goal setting. Through role modeling, these leaders create a culture of innovation and improvement and a shared sense of purpose. This creates the foundation for growth and success.
Transformational leaders are able to influence school outcomes by outlining high performance expectations, developing teachers through support, building productive relationships, and providing instructional support.
According to researcher Bernard Bass, the four attributes of transformational leadership are defined as idealized influence, inspirational motivation, intellectual stimulation, and individualized consideration.
Transformational leaders inspire trust, admiration, loyalty, and respect which inspires teacher motivation, morale, and performance. The influence of transformational leaders has been described as creating feelings. Transformational leaders are able to monitor and manage not only their own emotions, but also the emotions of others. This gives them the ability to charge their teams and drive success.
Studies have shown that transformational leadership has a direct effect on teacher performance, teacher taking steps to improve classroom practice. Findings also show that transformational leadership increases teachers' job satisfaction and strengthens their commitment to professional growth. The result is better student achievement and sustained progress throughout the school system
