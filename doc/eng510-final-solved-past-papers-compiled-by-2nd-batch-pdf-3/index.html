Define Reduplication.
Reduplication in linguistics is a morphological process in which the root or stem of a word (or
part of it) or even the whole word is repeated exactly or with a slight change.
Define Ethnography of communication.
Hymes is best known for his founding role in the ethnography of communication. He proposed
the term „ethnography of speaking‟. But later he amended it to „ethnography of communication‟.
It was a new approach to understanding language in use. Ethnographies of communication must
„discover and explicate the competence that enables members of a community to conduct and
interpret speech‟. The exploration and documentation of communicative competence within a
speech community is the essence of the ethnography of communication. As a means to this end,

Define Diglossia.
Diglossia is a relatively stable language situation in which, in addition to the primary dialects of
the language there is a very divergent, highly codified superposed variety. Ferguson's definition
of diglossia: “The vehicle of a large and respected body of written literature, either of an earlier
period or in another speech community, which is learned largely by formal education and is used
for most written and formal spoken purposes but is not used by any sector of the community for
ordinary conversation”.

High and Low varieties of language. Define.
High and low refer to the varieties of the same language used in different situations. Low is the
non-standard form spoken by regular people in their everyday lives. High is the standard and/or
upper variety that is used in official situations and as the written form.
High/low distinction is used when the standard variety of the language differs significantly from
its colloquial dialects. Sometimes the difference between the high and low varieties is so big that
the language has become diglossic. That's the case for example with Arabic and Swiss German.
Everyday speech spoken by Arabs and German-speaking Swiss people are local dialects and thus
low varieties but their written language as well as the language spoken in official situations is
the high variety, in Switzerland High German, and in the Arab countries Standard Arabic.
High and low varieties are said to have their own domains where they are used. They represent
the same language but the choice between them is determined by social settings.

It is very important for sociolinguists to have a sense of what the patterns of associations are
between people who are friends or roughly social equals within a community. This is because the
diffusion of linguistic change happens relatively fast and very efficiently along what we might call
horizontal channels e.g., within one age group and a social cohort. What we might call
vertical channels e.g., channels between generations or across big social divides are a

knowledge that kids talk like their friends, not their parents.
