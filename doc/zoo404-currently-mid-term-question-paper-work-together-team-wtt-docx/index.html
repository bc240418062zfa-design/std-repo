    5- Examples and Effects of Acclimatization and acclimation
Acclimatization
• An adaptive change within an Individual animal.
• Happens due to chronic Exposure to new,
• naturally Occurring environmental Conditions.
Example
• An animal migrates to a high Altitude area
• Faces low oxygen partial Pressure
Effects
• Lung ventilation rate increases Initially
• Ventilation drops to normal in Few days
• Animal has acclimatized to the High-altitude
Acclimation
• Changes are induced Experimentally in the Laboratory
Both of these are:
• Acquired characters
• Restricted to one or few Members of species
• Not inheritable
• Have no evolutionary Significance
• Reversible
Example
• Simulating high-altitude Conditions in lab.
• An animal physiologist places the Animal in a hypobaric chamber.
Effects
• Breathing rate reacts in the same Way.
• Animal becomes acclimated to The experimental conditions.
