          Critical Discourse Studies in Applied Contexts

Topic-01: The Applied Principles of CDS

What are some economic shifts discussed in Critical Discourse Studies (CDS)?

Examples: "post-Fordism," "information society," "knowledge economy," "liquid

How does Fairclough (1999) describe the communication rise in economic
exchange?

Fairclough uses the term "textual mediation" of social reality.

Scheeres (2003)?

Name some scholars known for tracking language's role in post-industrial

Examples: Norman Fairclough, Deborah Cameron, Monika Heller.

According to Lin (2014), what are three areas where CDS is deployed in applied
linguistics?

Answer: i) Studies of textbooks and school curricula, ii) School leadership and

What is the founding ideal of Critical Discourse Studies (CDS)?

According to Fairclough and Wodak (2010), what are some core principles of

Answer: Addressing social problems and socially committed research treating
discourse analysis as a mode of social action.

Topic-03: New Directions for Critical Discourse Studies

According to Thurlow (2018), what is needed for institutionalized critique to be
effective?

Answer: It needs to invoke discourses recognized within the social practice it
critiques.

What is CDS principally committed to, in addition to social critique?

Answer: Exposing gaps in academic orthodoxy and identifying them as

What area of language use does Cook (2008) suggest for further CDS

Answer: Advertising.

What does Pennycook (1990) advocate for in critical applied linguistics?

Answer: To be more humble, listen to alternative views, and avoid preaching
views as the newest and best.

Lecture-20

                              CDA and History

Topic-01: Introduction

What is the historiographical approach to critical discourse analysis (CDA)?

According to Flowerdew (2012), historiographical CDA seeks to reveal hidden
assumptions in historical accounts, emphasizing language's role in their
elaboration.

What does Flowerdew emphasize regarding the construction of meanings of the
past?

Flowerdew emphasizes that exploring the construction of meanings of the past
involves focusing on representations and receptions of discourses.

How does Flowerdew view the past in terms of construction?

Flowerdew argues that the past is not pre-existing; it has to be constructed
through semiotic work, requiring social actors' involvement in identity-building
processes.

What is the objective of a critical perspective in historiographical CDA?

 COMPILED BY SIR ZAHID                                                             4

The objective is to construct a past quotable in all moments, making visible
hegemonic and counter-hegemonic narratives that form part of larger power
struggles.

In what social practices do discourses about the past occur?

Discourses about the past occur in everyday practices like sharing anecdotes in a
family and more institutionalized practices such as the writing of history
textbooks.

Topic-02: Memory, history, and historiography

What is the key focus of historiography?

Historiography focuses on the construction of historical discourse, with a key
emphasis on representation as an issue in constructing discourse about the past.

How does Mariana Achugar differentiate between memory and history?

Memory is connected with lived experience (testimony), while history is more
related to the reformulation of this experience into a scientific discourse.

What is the role of memory in relation to the past?

Memory attempts to be faithful to the past, representing lived time and space,
while history aims to be truthful and represents the locus of enunciation of the
historian.
