Explain genomic libraries?

Biofilter?
Biofiltration is a pollution control technique using a bioreactor containing living material to capture and biologically
degrade pollutants. Common uses include processing waste water, capturing harmful chemicals or silt from surface
runoff, and microbiotic oxidation of contaminants in air.

Biodiesel?
Biodiesel is a form of diesel fuel derived from plants or animals and consisting of long-chain fatty acid esters. It is
typically made by chemically reacting lipids such as animal fat, soybean oil, or some other vegetable oil with an alcohol,
producing a methyl, ethyl or propyl ester.
