Question No: 30 ( Marks: 3 )
Which power is the ultimate source of an individual’s power in organization?
Discuss .

Answer : Legitimate power confers on an individual the legitimate authority to
control and use organizational resources to achieved the organizational goals.
Legitimate power is the ultimate source of an individual’s power in an
organization.
