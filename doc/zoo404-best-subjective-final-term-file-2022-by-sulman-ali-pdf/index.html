What are the conformers and its types
Animals that conform to external changes and Are unable to maintain homeostasis for internal
conditions.
Types: 1= Osmoconformers 2= Oxyconformers 3= Thermoconformers
What is the purpose of ultrafiltrate or ultera secretion:
Ultrafiltration (UF) is used to remove essentially all colloidal particles (0.01 to 1.0 microns) from
water and some of the largest dissolved contaminants. The pore size in a UF membrane is mainly
responsible for determining the type and size of contaminants removed.
How does the Resistance to Flow is Overcome by Pressure:
When heart contracts, pressure in the ventricles increases.This pressure is used to overcome
resistance to flow through the vessels.
Role of ADH:
Amount of water reabsorbed and conc. of urine is controlled by antidiuretic hormone. ADH acts
by increasing water permeability of renal tubules.
How B12 is absorbed In small Intestine?
Vitamin B12 (molecular weight 1357) is the largest water-soluble essential nutrient taken up
intact. Absorbed across the intestinal lumen in distal ileum by a mechanism independent of
intrinsic factor.
Define donnan effect?
Donnan equilibrium implies that: if there is a nondiffusible solute in one of the sol. separated by
semi-permeable membrane, the conc. of sol. on both sides do not equalize. Conc. of sol. with
non-diffusible solute remains high even at equilibrium. This is Donnan effect.
Minns ornithine area cycle?
Ornithine-Urea Cycle is used by all ‘'vertebrates except teleost fishes. it Synthesize urea
primarily in the liver. Two ammonia and one CO2 molecule are added to ornithine to form
Arginine. Enzyme arginase cleaves arginine to remove a molecule of urea and regenerate
ornithine.

Write the factors of ultra filtration?

Filtration takes place entirely passively across three layered wall of the glomerulus into the

Write a note on lipases:
Lipases are Lipid and fat digesting enzymes. Lipases Secreted in pancreatic juice and by
intestinal epithelium and Act on emulsified fat droplets and hydrolyze them into: •
Monoglycerides • Diglycerides • Free fatty acids and glycerol.
Emulsification by bile salts is necessary for lipases to hydrolyze lipids • If bile salts are
insufficient, fats are not emulsified • Fat digestion by lipases remains incomplete. These are
enzymes that the body uses to break down fats in food so they can be absorbed in the intestines.
Lipase is produced in the pancreas, mouth, and stomach. For example Candida rugosa.
Write a Note on blood vessels?
Blood vessels carry blood during circulation.
Three types: 1= Arteries 2= Veins 3= Capillaries
1= Arteries: Arteries carry blood away from heart to organs and tissues. Or Arteries, part of your
circulatory (cardiovascular) system, are the blood vessels that bring oxygen-rich blood from your
heart to all of your body's cells.
2= Veins: Veins return blood to heart. Or Veins are a type of blood vessel that return
deoxygenated blood from your organs back to your heart.
3= Capillaries: Capillaries have thin walls formed of only a single layer of endothelial cells.
Capillaries permit exchange of materials between blood and tissues. Or Capillaries are delicate
blood vessels that exist throughout your body. They transport blood, nutrients and oxygen to
cells in your organs and body systems. Capillaries are the smallest blood vessels in your vascular
system.
How the kidney regulate the PH:
Kidneys maintain pH by two ways: 1= Reabsorption of bicarbonate ions 2= Removal of fixed
acids and H+
Role of ph in body:
PH is the measure of how acidic or basic a substance is. In humans, pH balance plays a role in
keeping the body functioning optimally. The ideal pH of the body is slightly alkaline, which
facilitates certain biochemical reactions like oxygenating the blood.
Effect of Catecholamines on Pacemaker cells:
Adrenergic nerve fibers innervate S-A node, atria, A-V node and ventricles. Norepinephrine is
released from them. Norepinephrine increases the rate of contraction of myocardium. It increases
Na+ and Ca2+ conductance. Na+ and Ca2+ accelerate pacemaker depolarization.
Reabsorption of water occurs in which part of the loop of henle:
Reabsorption of water continues in descending limb. It is freely permeable to water.
Reabsorption at DCT:
Only 15–20 % of initial filtrate volume reaches DCT. Tubular cells of DCT, actively reabsorb
Na+ and Cl-. Distal portions of DCT also contain ion pumps that reabsorb tubular Na+ in
exchange for K+. DCT also reabsorbs HCO3 - ions to regulate urine pH.
Function of acetylcholine:
ACh increases chromaffin cells’ conductance for Ca2+.

The alimentary canal is mainly referred to as the pathway by which food enters our body and
moves out through the anus after digestion. It is a tube-like structure which starts from the mouth
and ends in the anus. The alimentary canal plays a primary role in human digestion and is also
termed as the digestive tract.
