Q6: Define Monozygotic Twins
• Produced from one zygote (identical twins) • They are strikingly alike and of same sex • About
33% have separate placenta • Majority have separate amniotic sac
• Small proportion have one placenta and one amniotic sac • Indicate separation occur after 9 day
of pregnancy • Conjoining risk, condition is Siamese twining

Q7: Dizygotic Twins.3
• Produced from two zygote (Fraternal twins) • Do not have striking similarities • May be of
same or different sex • They have separate placenta and separate amniotic sac

Q9: Difference b/w synchrony and Asynchrony?
• Initially all the cells of embryo divide at the same time this is called synchrony
• As development proceeds, all cells don’t divide simultaneously. This is called asynchrony
