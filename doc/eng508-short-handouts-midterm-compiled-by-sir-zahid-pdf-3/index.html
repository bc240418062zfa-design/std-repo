Topic-001: Definition of Semantics and Pragmatics Learning Theories

What is semantics?

Semantics is the study of literal, de-contextualized, and grammatical meaning in
language.

How is linguistic semantics defined?

Linguistic semantics is the study of how languages organize and express
meanings.

What does semantics analyze in language?

Semantics analyzes the relationships between signs, symbols, and their
representations.

What is semantics and what does it study?

Semantics refers to the branch of linguistics that concerns itself with the study of
literal, de-contextualized, and grammatical meaning. It analyzes how languages
organize and express meanings. Linguistic semantics explores the relationships
between linguistic signs, symbols, and their representations.

Define pragmatics and its scope.

Pragmatics is the study of meaning as communicated by a speaker and
interpreted by a listener. It delves into the "invisible" meaning that goes beyond
the explicit words used. This field includes context, background knowledge, and
cross-cultural variations in communication

Topic-002: The Borderline between Semantics and Pragmatics

What does semantics study?

Semantics studies the contribution of linguistic items' meanings and their
combinations to overall meaning.

Describe lexical semantics.

Lexical semantics deals with the meanings of individual words.

What is compositional semantics concerned with?

 COMPILED BY SIR ZAHID                                                               2

Compositional semantics studies how word meanings combine to form phrase
and sentence meanings.

Where does pragmatics focus in relation to semantics?

Pragmatics focuses on the meaning conveyed by an utterance in a specific context
after semantics.

Explain the difference between semantics and pragmatics.

Semantics is concerned with the meanings contributed by linguistic items and
how they combine to form meaning. On the other hand, pragmatics deals with
the meaning conveyed by an utterance in a specific context. Pragmatics takes
over where semantics ends, focusing on context-specific interpretations.

Topic-003: Sentences and Utterances

What does semantics deal with in terms of linguistic entities?

Semantics deals with the meaning of sentences.

What is the distinction between sentences and utterances?

Sentences are abstract structures, while utterances are concrete instances of
speaking or writing.

How does an utterance differ from a sentence?

Utterances have spatio-temporal and physical properties, whereas sentences do
not.

What is the distinction between sentences and utterances in terms of semantics
and pragmatics?

Semantics primarily deals with sentence-level meaning, studying the meanings of
words and how they combine to form meaning. Pragmatics, however, centers
around utterances – specific instances of speech or writing. Utterances have

Topic-004: Language and Logic

What is the focus of logic?

Logic focuses on the relationships, combinations, and derivations of ideas.

What is the basic unit of traditional logic?

The basic unit of traditional logic is a "proposition."

What do logicians study in terms of meanings?

Logicians study meanings through propositions, which are the contents of
thoughts expressed by declarative sentences.

Define compositionality.

Compositionality states that the meaning of a whole is composed by combining
the meanings of its parts.

Explain the relationship between logic and linguistics.

A: Logic concerns itself with the ways in which ideas relate, combine, and derive
further ideas. In linguistics, logic is important because it helps us understand how
language expresses meaning through the composition of words and phrases. The
basic unit of logic, the proposition, aligns with meanings expressed by sentences
in language.

Topic-005: The Explicit and the Implicit

Define implicit meaning in pragmatics.

Implicit meaning is the intended meaning of a speaker that may differ from the
literal meaning of words.

What does the implicit meaning often convey?

Implicit meaning often conveys more information than what is literally expressed,
making literal meaning underdetermined.
