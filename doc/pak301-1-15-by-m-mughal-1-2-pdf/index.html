Lecture 1
       Ideology of Pakistan
Ideology:
      Set of believes, values and ideas of group and nation.
      review of the existing political, social and economic arrangements
      Set of principles, a framework of action and guidance system.
      Legitimizes or delegitimizes certain actions and philosophies.

Ideology of Pakistan:
     Allama Iqbal gave it a philosophical explanation; Quaid e Azam translated it into political reality.
     Constitution Assembly of Pakistan, by passing Objectives resolution in March 1949 gave it legal sanction
        (authorize).
     Realization that the Muslims are different from Hindus of Sub continent; change the demand to a
        separate state.
     Believe that Islam and Hinduism not only 2 religions; two social orders that produced distinct culture.
     Different culture and tradition; eating habits, music, architecture and script, all are poles apart.
     Basics of Muslim nationhood was neither territorial nor racial or linguistic or ethic rather they were a
        nation.
     An area where they order their lives in accordance with the teaching of Holy Qur’an and Sunnah.

Evolution of “Two Nation Theory”:
     Pledge the redemption of humankind establishing a kind society based on Qur’anic teachings.
     The Arab traders had introduced the new religion, Islam, in the Indian coastal areas.
     Mohammad Bin Qasim was the first Muslim invader who conquered some part of India.
     Mahmood Ghazna launched 17 attacks & opened gates to preach Islam.
     Muslim Sufi (saints) rejecting the vices in the Indian society & presented pure practical picture of the
        teachings of Islam.
     Qutub-ud-Din Aibuk permanently established Muslim dynasty that followed Sultanate and Mughal
        dynasties.
     Deen-e-illahi, Bakhti movements, etc. created reaction amongst the Muslim ulama to preserve the pure
        Islamic character.
     Role of Sheikh Ahmed Sirhindi and others noteworthy.
     The won over the Muslims rulers due to the industrial and scientific developments and modern war
        strategy.
     The War of Independence 1857 was shattering setback to the Indian Muslims.
     Muslim scholar sought to reform the teaching of Islamic law. Prominent name Sir Syed Ahmed Khan
        (1817-1898) who awakened and guided his community.
     Educational drive, the Ali-Garh movement, proved to be the best means of social mobility for the Muslim
        gentry under colonial rule.
     In 1885 the Indian National Congress was founded to indicate the beginning of the Indian nationalist
        movement under the British.
     All India Muslim league was founded in Dhaka 1906.
     The Ali-Garh movement produced educated leadership who could protect the Muslims’ rights.
     Muslims are not a community but a nation with a distinctive history, heritage, culture, civilization, and
        future aspirations.
     Muslims wanted to order their lives in accordance with their ideals and philosophy of life.

Hindi-Urdu Controversy:

       After 1857, Hindi-Urdu Controversy was the major assault by the Hindus on Muslim heritage and legacy
        of Muslim Empire.
       Hindus demanded Hindi as the official language replacing Urdu.
       There were demonstrations against Urdu by the Hindus in Banaras in 1867. That was the start of Hindi-
        Urdu controversy.
       Muslim nationalism is manifested with the sublime principles to implement like:
         1. Rule of Law, socio-economic justice, equity and fair play.
          2. Equality of opportunity to all citizens irrespective of caste, sect, religion or region.
         3. Religious and Cultural tolerance.
         4. Respect for human dignity and rights.
          5. Protection of the rights and interests of non-Muslims and freedom to practice their beliefs and
        religions.

Ideology of Pakistan in the light of statement of
   QUAID E AZAM & ALLAMA IQBAL
 Mohammad Ali Jinnah:

       M.A Jinnah was a History- Making leader.
       Changed the course of history.
       Possessed a visionary leadership, commitment to the cause.
       Political Mobilization capacity.
       Charismatic leader in real sense

Role of Jinnah:

       Played decisive role in articulating ( Bayan Krna ) the Muslim demand.
       Started political career in 1906 by joining the Indian National Congress.
       Elected to the legislative Council in 1909.
       Joined All India Muslim League in 1913.
       Disagreement with Gandhi on the issue of Swaraj (self-rule).
       Resigned from congress in 1920.
       Early efforts to promote Muslim-Hindu unity.
       Lucknow Pact was signed in 1916.
       Hindus accepted the demands of Muslims:
                       1. Separate electorate.
                       2. One third seats in Central Legislature.
                       3. Protection of Minority Right
       Nehru Report, accepted rights were ignored.
       Jinnah presented 14 points in 1929.
       Use term NATION for Muslims of India in Feb 1935.
       March 1936 Bombay, he stated that the Muslims could arrive.
       In 1937, he asserted that there is also a third party in India, the Muslims.
       In 1939, he roared that Muslims and Hindus are two nations.

       It can help to overcome the present crisis.
       It’s a source Inspiration and guidance providing ethical foundation, framework social order and
        civilization.

Guidance & inspiration for constitution-making and governance:

       Talked of the Modern notion of state, constitution, civil and political rights and democracy.
