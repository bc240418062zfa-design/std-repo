                            Quiz from Assessments (Final-Term)

   1. Which of the following can be included in paralinguistic communication? (All of the given
      options, Pitch, Volume, Accent)
   2. Into how many groups the spontaneous gestures are divided? (four)
   3. The term “communication” comes from the Latin ‘comunicare’ meaning
      ______________. (To transmit)
   4. The parts of a written or spoken statement that precede or follow a specific word or passage,
      usually influencing its meaning are called the ___________ of it. (Context)
   5. Who grounded his/her work in the Sapirian tradition, but expanded their concerns with
      language beyond description? (Who grounded his/her work in the Sapirian tradition, but
      expanded their concerns with language beyond description? (Erving Goffman, Susan
      Ervin-Tripp, John Gumperz, All of the given options)
   6. If a Saudi Arab shows respect to an American he does what? (Lowers his voice)
   7. What factor(s) impede Human Communication? (Obfuscation, Distraction, Not
      understanding the context, All of the given options)
   8. According to Hymes, ungrammatical utterances may be socially inappropriate, just as
      grammatical utterances can be socially appropriate. (False)
   9. Which of the following is a type of Nonlinguistic Communication? (Pictures and
      Proximity, All of the given options, Gaze and Posture, Jewelry and Clothes)
   10.Identify whether the given statement is true or false. “The context is the set of
      circumstances or facts that surround a particular event, situation, etc." (True)
   11.Which of the following external factors plays a role in Politeness and Social Interaction?
      (Status, All of the given options, Age, Power)
   12.Brown and Levinson outline four main types of politeness strategies? (Four)
   13.The factors behind the competence of a language can be __________. (All of the given
      options, Connotation, Style. Register)
   14.A complete social event involves ___________. (All of the given options, Speaker and
      Listener, Message, Register)
   15.Who defined competence as the grammar one knows without being necessarily aware of
      it? (Chomsky)
   16.Sociolinguistic ___________ means the ability to use linguistic knowledge and put it to
      the test in real life. (Performance)
   17.Identify whether the given statement is true or false. “Politeness strategies make refusals
      difficult." (False)
   18.Sociolinguistic competence of the target language means knowing its ____________.
      (Turn taking, Address forms, Taboos, All of the given options)
   19.According to Hymes, the ethnography of language is used as ___________. (Both the
      given options, Methodological tool, All of the given options, Empirical studies)
   20.What is the inexorable fate that all languages succumb to? (Extinction, All of the given
      options, Evolution, Transformation)
   21.For Galtung, the world can be divided into how many two domains? (two)
