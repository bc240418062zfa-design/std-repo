Quiz from Assessments (Mid-Term)

                           ENG505_Language Learning Theories
                            Quiz from Assessments (Mid-Term)

1. In Krashen’s Monitor Theory, acquisition and learning develop ______________ in language
   learning. (competence)
2. Cognitive process concentrates on _________________ . (mental process)
3. What type of symbols are present in a language system? (Arbitrary)
4. B.F. Skinner is a ________________. (behaviourist)
5. Universal Grammar refers to the mentalist approach that supports ______________ and
   ___________ . (nativism and cognitivism)
6. …………… proposed a ‘three-level framework’ (1965) based on ‘Approach, Methods, and
   Techniques.’ (Edward Anthony)
7. ………………….of language deals with the understanding of the structurally related elements
   in a language. (Structural principal)
8. Silent Way emphasizes problem solving by involving the ……………..material. (target
   language)
9. Total Physical Response emphasizes instant understanding of the target language by
   responding to the ……………given in the target language. (command)
10.The term "technique" is a broader term for specific ________________ . (tasks)
11.What does LAD stand for? (Language Acquisition Device)
12.Learning belongs to behaviourist approach and deals with a mechanical process of
   ………..formation. (habit)
13.Chomsky refers to competence as a system of rules mastered, and the ability to produce
   ___________________ number of sentences. (infinite)
14.Performance is ‘the actual use of language in …………situations.’ (concrete)
15.Inductive Learning begins with …………….first and requires learners to determine rules
   relevant to the examples. (examples)
16.The behaviourist movement began with John Watson in ______________ . (1913)
17.Russian ______________, Ivan Pavlov, is the founder of ‘Pavlovian Conditioning’.
   (physiologist)
18.Temporal Congruity occurs when two …………are experienced close together in time and, as
   a result, an association may be formed. (stimuli)
19.Classical Conditioning supports the habit formation and opposes the idea of _______________
   as responsible factors for learning. (consciousness)
20.Reinforcement or punishment determines the ______________ . (behavior)
21.He introduced the theory of Verbal Behaviour in _________________ . (1957)
22.Skinner identified following three types of responses that can follow behaviour:
   ___________,_________________, ___________ . (neutral, reinforcer, punisher)
23.The ‘Mediation Theory’ was introduced by ______________________ . (Charles Osgood)
24.‘Law of Effect’ was introduced by _______________. (Edward Thorndike)
25.Law of Exercise: (a) the law of use and (b) the law of _____________. (disuse)
26.Immanuel Kant was a _________________ philosopher. (German)

27.Rene Descartes, a French philosopher, mathematician and scientist, and father of
   ……………..philosophy. (modern)
28.Chomsky’s (1965) idea of __________________ has been a hallmark of language learning
   mechanism. (innateness)
29.The Lenneberg theory is known as __________________ . (Critical Period Hypothesis)
30.Competence is a native speaker’s _________________ of his/her language, system of rules
   mastered, and the ability to understand and produce infinite number of sentences. (knowledge)
31.Robert Gagne (1916 – 2002), an educational psychologist, introduced _____________ levels
   of learning events. (events)
32.Three-Stage Information Processing Model ___________________, short-term memory,
   long-term memory. (sensory register)
33.Vygotsky believed that language is one of the most important psychological tools that effects
   children’s________________ development. (cognitive)
34.Green (2003) states that information processing approach involves three basic steps: encoding,
   storage, and ___________________ . (retrieval)
35.Vygotsky’s Socio Cultural Theory (1896-1934): is based on the __________________
   analysis, the role of language, and the social relation. (developmental)
36.Newell and Simon (1972) proposed _________________ for problem solving. (Information
   Processing System)
37.Connectionist models, cover a wide range of domains, are related to language processing and
   cognitive ______________. (control)
38.Connectionism suggests that cognitive phenomena can be explained with respect to a set of
   general information-processing principles, known as Parallel Distributed Processing. (True)
39.The Associative-Cognitive CREED Theory in SLA was introduced by ___________________
   . (Nick Ellis)
40.PDP models are neutrally inspired information processing models to explain the ways of
   information processing which actually takes place in the ____________. (brain)
41.Sir Frederick Bartlett proposed the theory of _________________________. (Reconstructive
   Memory and Schema)
