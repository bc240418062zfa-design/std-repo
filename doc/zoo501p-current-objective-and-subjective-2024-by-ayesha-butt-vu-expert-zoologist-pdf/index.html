**Short Questions: **:
1. **Vitelline Membrane**: The vitelline membrane is a thin, transparent layer that surrounds the
yolk of an egg. It plays a crucial role in protecting the yolk and regulating the exchange of gases and
water between the egg and its environment.

2. **Use of Ovaprim**: Ovaprim is a synthetic hormone used in aquaculture to induce spawning in
fish. It helps synchronize and enhance the breeding process, making it easier to manage reproduction
in captive fish populations.

3. **Nematode Infection in Humans**: Nematode infections are typically transmitted to humans
through contaminated soil, water, or food. These parasitic worms enter the body, often through the
skin or ingestion, and can cause various diseases.

4. **Importance of Corona Radiata**: The corona radiata consists of follicular cells surrounding the
ovum. During fertilization, it helps protect the egg and provides necessary nutrients until the sperm
penetrates the egg.

5. **Dactylography**: Dactylography, or fingerprinting, is the study and use of fingerprints for
identification purposes. It is a critical tool in forensic science for identifying individuals.

6. **Ovum vs. Zygote**: The ovum is the unfertilized female reproductive cell, while a zygote is the
cell formed after the ovum is fertilized by a sperm. The zygote undergoes cell division to develop into
an embryo.

7. **Distinguishing Fertilized and Unfertilized Eggs**: Fertilized eggs usually show signs of
development, such as cell division or a developing embryo, whereas unfertilized eggs do not.
Microscopic examination can reveal these differences.

8. **Role of Cilia in the Oviduct**: Cilia in the oviduct help transport the egg from the ovary to the
uterus. They create a current that moves the egg along the fallopian tube, aiding in fertilization and
early embryonic development.

9. **Corona Radiata in Fertilization**: The corona radiata provides a protective barrier around the
egg, supports the sperm’s entry during fertilization, and helps in nourishing the ovum before
implantation.
