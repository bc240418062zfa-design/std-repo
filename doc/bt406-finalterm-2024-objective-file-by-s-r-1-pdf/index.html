1. **What is data transformation in the context of research?**
 - A) Changing data to their original form.
 - B) Modifying data to match the researcher's preferences.

 - C) Keeping data unaltered throughout the analysis.

 **Answer: B) Modifying data to match the researcher's preferences.**

2. **Why might researchers prefer asking for respondents' year of birth instead of their age?**

 - A) To increase response bias.
 - B) To complicate data analysis.
 - C) To minimize response bias.

 **Answer: C) To minimize response bias.**

3. **What is a common purpose of collapsing or combining categories of a variable in data
transformation?**
 - A) To increase the number of categories.
 - B) To complicate the analysis.
 - C) To reduce the number of categories.

 **Answer: C) To reduce the number of categories.**
1. **What is the primary purpose of scales and indexes in research?**
 - A) To complicate data analysis.
 - B) To assess the quality of measurement.

 - C) To create confusion in research findings.

 **Answer: B) To assess the quality of measurement.**

2. **How is a scale different from an index in terms of measurement?**

 - A) Scales provide single scores, while indexes combine distinct indicators.

 - B) Scales focus on ordinal-level measures, while indexes emphasize ratio-level measures.
 - C) Scales are used for content or convergent validity, while indexes capture intensity.

 **Answer: A) Scales provide single scores, while indexes combine distinct indicators.**

3. **What does the term "unidimensionality" mean in the context of constructing scales or
indexes?**
 - A) It emphasizes the complexity of measurement.

 - B) It indicates the combination of various constructs.
 - C) It ensures that all items in a measure align and measure a single construct.

 **Answer: C) It ensures that all items in a measure align and measure a single construct.**
What is an important consideration when creating indexes to measure constructs?

A) Including items without face validity.
B) Excluding items with face validity.
C) Measuring each construct with multiple indicators.
Answer: B) Excluding items with face validity.
How is a college quality index created?

A) By measuring only three distinguished characteristics.
B) By combining various elements into a single score.
C) By using a single indicator for each characteristic.
Answer: B) By combining various elements into a single score.

A) All items have equal weight.

Answer: A) All items have equal weight.
