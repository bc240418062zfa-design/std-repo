Q.No.1: Use of Immuno-fluorescence assay? 2 marks

Answer: Immuno-fluorescence is an important immunochemical technique that allows for
detection and localization of a wide variety of antigens in different types of tissues of various cell
preparations. This technique can be used to visualize the structure such as intermediate size-cell
filaments.

Q.No.2: Write different routes for inoculation in animal cells?? 2marks

Answer: Following are the routes for inculation in animal cells;

   1. Chorioallantoic membrane inoculation.
   2. Amniotic inoculation.
   3. Allantoic inoculation.
   4. Yolk sac inoculation.

Q.No.3: What are the effects of attack of viruses on host cells? 2marks

Answer: Following are the effects of attacks of virus on host cells;

   ➢ Genetic alterations.
   ➢ Physical damage
   ➢ Oncogenesis

Q.No.4: Explain the replication cycle of Coronavirus? (5-marks)

Arif Khan 03007607817
Answer:

Cytoplasmic.

   ➢ Attachment of the viral S-protein receptors
   ➢  Endocytosis of the virus into the host cell
   ➢ Fusion of virus membrane with endosomal membrane
   ➢ Genome is released into the cytoplasm.
   ➢ Synthesis and proteolytic cleavage of the replicase polyprotein.
   ➢ A dsRNA genome is synthesized from the genomic ssRNA(+).
   ➢ The dsRNA genome is transcribed by providing viral mRNAs genomes.
   ➢ Synthesis of structural proteins encoded by sub-genomic mRNAs.
   ➢ Assembly and budding at membranes of the endoplasmic reticulum (ER), the
     intermediate compartments, and the Golgi complex.
   ➢ Release of new virions by exocytosis.

Q.No.5: Write down the general characteristics of Virus. (3-5 Marks)

Answer: Following are the general properties of viruses;

   ➢   Non living structures.
   ➢   Non-cellular.
   ➢   Contain a protein coat known as capsid.
   ➢   Have a nucleic acid core containing DNA or RNA.
   ➢   Capable of reproducing only when inside a HOST cell.
   ➢   Some viruses may have spikes to help attach to the host cell.
   ➢   Most viruses infect only specific host cells.

Q.No.6: What is paramyxo?

Answer: Para means beyond and Myxo means mucus. Paramyxo and orthomyxo were formerly
known as myxoviruses.

Q.No.7: Define Virus

Answer: Virus is a Latin word which meaning poison or venom. Viruses are noncellular
particles made up of genetic material and proteins that can invade living cells.

Q.No.9: What is host range of virus?

Answer: The spectrum of host cells that the virus can infect. Host range refers to the spectrum
of host cells in which virus can multiply.

Q.No.10: When genetic interaction occurs between virus?

Answer: Naturally genetic interaction occurs between viruses. It occurs more than one infection
in host.

Q.No.11: Describe uncoating of Viruses?

Answer: During virus entry, capsid shells are primed and disassembled in a complex, tightly-
regulated, multi-step process termed uncoating.

   ➢ Removal of capsid from viruses is called uncoating.
   ➢ Exposing the viral genome in the host cell.
   ➢ The protein coat around genome get disintegrated.
