Question: Reflection in and reflection on

Answer: Reflection in action is thinking during an activity to adjust and improve. Reflection

on action occurs after the activity to analyze and learn from the experience.

Question: Task analysis

Answer: Task analysis involves breaking down a complex task into smaller, manageable

steps to understand the skills needed to complete it.

Question: Misconceptions of learning in isolation

Answer: Learning in isolation can be ineffective as it overlooks the importance of social

interaction, collaboration, and real-world context in the learning process.

Question: Define learning

Answer: Learning is the process of acquiring knowledge, skills, attitudes, or behaviors

through study, experience, or teaching.

Question: Unqualified profession

Answer: An unqualified profession refers to individuals working without the necessary

credentials or expertise, often leading to lower standards and effectiveness.

Question: Bloom's taxonomy

Answer: Bloom's taxonomy is a hierarchical model used to classify educational learning

objectives into cognitive levels, including knowledge, comprehension, application, analysis,

synthesis, and evaluation.

                                                                                                1

Question: Instructional and curriculum difference

Answer: Instruction refers to the methods and strategies used to teach, while the curriculum is

the content and structure of what is being taught.

Question: Why inductive mode is important

Answer: The inductive mode is important because it encourages learners to discover patterns

and principles on their own, fostering critical thinking and deeper understanding.

Question: Why we should prevent unqualified in organizations

Answer: Preventing unqualified individuals in organizations ensures the quality of work,

professionalism, and trust in the services provided, leading to better outcomes.

Question: Elements of plans

Answer: Key elements of a plan include objectives, resources, strategies, timeline, and

evaluation methods.

Question: Write 3 areas that are required to convert novice teachers into expert

teachers

Answer:

   1. Continuous professional development

   2. Classroom management skills

   3. Reflective teaching practices

Question: What prerequisites for essay writing should teachers adapt

Answer: Teachers should ensure students understand the essay structure, thesis development,

and coherence of arguments before starting the writing process.

                                                                                              2

Question: Mention synthesis level question

Answer: A synthesis level question might be: "How would you combine different theories to

create a new approach to solving this problem?"

Question: Techniques to avoid gender-biased discrimination

Answer:

   1. Use gender-neutral language

   2. Ensure equal participation opportunities

   3. Provide diverse role models

Question: Unit plan vs. lesson plan

Answer: A unit plan outlines the goals, content, and assessments for an entire unit, while a

lesson plan details the objectives, materials, and activities for a single class session.

Question: Learning system

Answer: A learning system encompasses the methods, resources, and environments used to

support learning processes.

Question: Planning

Answer: Planning in education refers to organizing lessons, activities, and assessments to

achieve specific learning objectives.

Question: Instructions and curriculum: its importance

Answer: Instructions are important for guiding learning processes, while the curriculum

provides the content and framework, ensuring that educational goals are met.

                                                                                               3

Question: IEP define

Answer: An Individualized Education Plan (IEP) is a personalized educational program

designed to meet the unique needs of students with disabilities.

Question: Two components of unit planning

Answer:

   1. Learning objectives

   2. Assessment methods

Question: Why resources are included in planning

Answer: Resources are included in planning to ensure the necessary tools, materials, and

support are available for successful teaching and learning.

Question: Analysis level

Answer: The analysis level in Bloom’s taxonomy involves breaking information into parts to

understand its structure and relationships.

Question: Five points for teachers becoming professional

Answer:

   1. Continuous learning

   2. Reflective practice

   3. Adhering to ethical standards

   4. Collaboration with peers

   5. Effective classroom management

Question: Rationale

Answer: A rationale is the reasoning or justification behind a particular decision or approach,

explaining why it is necessary or beneficial.

Question: Teaching as a social activity

Answer: Teaching is a social activity because it involves interaction, communication, and

relationships between teachers and learners to facilitate knowledge transfer.
