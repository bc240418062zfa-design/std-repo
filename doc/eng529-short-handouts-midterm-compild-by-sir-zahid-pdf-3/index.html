Topic 1: Definition of Technology

What is the definition of technology according to Merriam-Webster?

Technology is defined as "The practical application of knowledge, especially in a
particular area" or "a manner of accomplishing a task, especially using technical
processes, methods, or knowledge."

Why Technology in Education

What are the key reasons for incorporating technology in education?

Technology saves time and labor, inspires creativity, and connects people to new
ideas and opportunities.

Uses of Technology in Language Teaching and Learning

What are some ways technology can be used in language teaching and learning?

Technology can be used for accessing information, exposure to the target
language, entertainment, creating text, publishing learner work, communication
with other learners, creating a sense of community, and managing and organizing
learning.

Benefits of Learning Technology

Why do classroom teachers often use learning technologies?

Classroom teachers use learning technologies because they can motivate and
engage learners, empower them to take ownership of their learning, and provide
real language exposure.

What are some benefits of using learning technology in the classroom, as
reported by classroom teachers?

Classroom teachers using learning technology report benefits such as increased
learner motivation and engagement. It also empowers learners to take greater
ownership of their learning and exposes them to real language through
technology.

Source of Instant Information

How can the Internet be a valuable resource in the classroom?

The Internet can provide instant information for teachers, allowing them to
answer questions in real-time and show learners how to use reference tools like
dictionaries and phonemic charts.

What are some examples of hand-held devices and their potential in education?

Technology in Language Learning

What is the historical evolution of technology in language learning?

Technology in language learning has evolved since the 1950s, driven by
developments in computer technology and learning theories.

What is CALL in the context of technology in language learning?

CALL stands for Computer-Assisted Language Learning, which is the most widely
used variant of technology in language learning.

Name some other terms associated with technology use in language learning.

Other terms include TELL, WELL, CELL, NBLT, CMC, ICT, e-learning, blended
learning, and MALL (Mobile Assisted Language Learning).

How has mobility been integrated into language learning with technology?

Mobile Assisted Language Learning (MALL) or m-learning has brought mobility
into language learning.

What are some websites recommended for language learning?

Some recommended websites include British Council Teaching English, Onestop
English, Zozanga.com, Manythings.org, and more.

Could you explain what CALL is in the context of technology in language
learning?

Certainly. CALL stands for Computer-Assisted Language Learning. It is the most
widely used variant of technology in language learning. Despite numerous
suggestions to revise the term, CALL continues to be used to refer to the
intersection of technology with second language teaching and learning. However,
there are various other terms associated with technology use in language
learning, such as TELL, WELL, CELL, NBLT, CMC, ICT, e-learning, blended learning,
and MALL (Mobile Assisted Language Learning)/m-learning, which emphasizes
mobility in learning.
