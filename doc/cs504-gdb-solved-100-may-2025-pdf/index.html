A well-established software house named CodeNova Solutions has recently been
approached by a large public university to develop a University Management
System (UMS). Project manager of CodeNova Solutions is confused whether to
completely use artificial intelligence (AI) or not in this Software development;
because with the rise of artificial intelligence (AI) and machine learning (ML)
technologies, the complexity of software development has reached new heights. But
AI is also contributing to ease of software development and solving large problems
instantly.

Now, CodeNova Solutions must decide on the development approach. They have
two main options:

    1. Use traditional development techniques, which involve structured coding
       practices, fixed business logic, and deterministic workflows.
    2. Use AI-based solutions, which involve machine learning models, data
       training, and automation that adapts to changing patterns over time.

Project manager will come to a better choice after a discussion with peers on basis
of the following discussion question.

"Is the introduction of AI and machine learning technologies into software
development contributing to a new type of Software Crisis, or is it just solving
the old problems associated with software complexity?"

problems.

datasets. But they also bring new issues such as lack of transparency, model bias, and
complex debugging, which lead to a different kind of software crisis.
