What is the passing criteria for GENERAL under the VU 20% Two-Half Rule?

Under official Virtual University regulations, students must score at least 20% in the First Half (Quizzes, Assignments, GDBs, and Midterm combined) AND independently at least 20% in the Final-Term examination paper. Scoring under 20% in the final exam results in an automatic F grade regardless of earlier marks.
