Question:   How Public Relation is associated to Mass communication?
            Mass Communication Mass communication is the term used to describe the
            academic study of the various means by which individuals and entities relay
            information through mass media to large segments of the population at the same
            time. It is usually understood to relate to newspaper and magazine publishing,
            radio, television and film, as these are used both for disseminating news and for
            advertising. Public Relation association with Mass Communications The basic
            difference between mass communication and Public relation is that mass
            communication is used to disseminate the information to a larger audience with the
            help of newspaper, TV and radio etc. But Public Relations is a planned and
            sustained effort to establish and maintain goodwill and understanding between an
            organization and its public. But here we can say the mass communication is a tool
            through which public Relations can be established and enhanced with the
Answer:     involvement of mass communication channels.
