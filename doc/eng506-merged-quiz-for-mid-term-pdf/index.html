1. Speaker's education is the main factor that determines the status of a language as a global
   language. (False)
2. Communication in the past was through translators and interpreters. (True)
3. English is a gender neutral language. (True)
4. The process through which people, companies, and governments of different nations interact
   and collaborate is termed as globalisation. (True)
5. Select the factor which is important in making a language a global language. (Economic power)
6. Which of the following English speaking countries is economically supreme? (USA)
7. _______________ is crucial in making a language a global language. (Economic power)
8. When a language loses its all speakers, the situation is termed as -----------------. (language
   death)
9. ______________ are the factors that are involved in linguistic complacency. (All of the given
   options; Lack of money; Lack of opportunity; Lack of interest)
10.What is the disadvantage of having a single global language? (Risks the status of other
   languages)
11.One of the appealing properties of language is its _________ character. (cosmopolitan)
12.There are _____reasons behind the lack of motivation for learning other languages. (many)
13.People’s desire to have shared customs, beliefs and behaviors is termed as ----------------
   identity. (cultural)
14.The term ------------------- means that people are fully satisfied with one language as the only
   tool for communication in all fields of life. (linguistic complacency)
15.Global independance is influential in adopting English as a global language. (True)
16.Emerging localized or indigenized varieties of English are termed as ---------------------. (World
   Englishes)
17.In ____________ century a large number of immigrants came from Midlands and northern
   England. (17)
18.---------------- is a term for emerging localized or indigenized varieties of English. (World
   Englishes)
19.James Cook discovered and charted New Zealand and Australia in _____________. (1770)
20.Who colonized New Zealand? (British)
21.English was brought to Britain by Jutes, Angles and Saxons. (True)
22.The Portuguese colonized New Zealand. (False)
23.In the year 1807, the British Parliament passed an Act for the _____ of slave trade. (abolition)
24.Blacks viewed ___________ language as a tool for achieving an international voice. (English)

   liberation. (English)
