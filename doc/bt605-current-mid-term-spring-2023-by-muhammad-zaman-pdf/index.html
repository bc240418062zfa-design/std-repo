Biosafety:
Biosafety is the prevention of large-scale loss of biological integrity, focusing
both on ecology and human health. These prevention mechanisms include
conduction of regular reviews of the biosafety in laboratory settings, as well
as strict guidelines to follow.

Biosafety is used to protect us from harmful incidents. High security facilities
are necessary when working with Synthetic Biology as there are possibilities
of bioterrorism acts or release of harmful chemicals and or organisms into the
environment.
