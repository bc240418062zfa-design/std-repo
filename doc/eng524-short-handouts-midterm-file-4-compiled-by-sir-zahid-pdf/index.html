             Systemic Functional Linguistics and CDA

Topic-01: Systemic Functional Linguistics (SFL) - Aims and Applications

What is Systemic Functional Linguistics (SFL) considered as?

It is considered a branch of linguistics with specific theoretical principles.

How have SFL linguists applied their analytic tools in studies?

SFL linguists have applied their tools in studies such as education, communication
studies, forensic linguistics, and Critical Discourse Analysis (CDA).

What does SFL focus on within specific social and cultural contexts?

SFL focuses on the meaning-making resources of language within specific social
and cultural contexts.

What are the three overarching components of the semantic system in SFL?

According to SFL, how is meaning realized in texts?

Meaning is realized in texts through the unfolding of social and cultural constructs

What does SFL claim about the study of discourse and grammar?

How do SFL approaches contribute to understanding discourse events?

Topic-02: Objections to SFL in Critical Discourse Analysis

What objection does Van Dijk have to SFL as a framework for the study of
discourse?

Van Dijk objects to SFL due to what he sees as too much linguistic sentence
grammar and esoteric vocabulary.

According to Blommaert, what is the problem of "linguistic bias in CD"?

The problem is that linguistic bias restricts the analysis to textually organized and
explicitly linguistically encoded discourse.

Topic-01: Introduction – Metaphor and CDA

What is metaphor analysis in critical discourse studies (CDS)?

Answer: Metaphor analysis in CDS is gaining credence as a profitable tool
according to Carl John.

What do cognitive approaches to metaphor assert?

Answer: Cognitive approaches assert that metaphor is not just about how we talk
but significantly influences how we think about particular subjects.

How do Lakoff and Johnson argue metaphor's role in reality construction?

Answer: Lakoff and Johnson argue that examining metaphor in text and talk
reveals dominant ways people conceive aspects of reality, constructing
worldviews and versions for addressees.

Why is metaphor analysis considered appropriate for critical research?

Answer: Metaphor analysis is considered appropriate for critical research due to
its ideological potential, shaping and perpetuating particular worldviews in
discourse.

Topic-02: Metaphor and Cognition

How is metaphor viewed as a cognitive process?

Answer: Metaphor is viewed as a cognitive process involving conceptualizing one
thing in terms of another.

What is a key insight from Conceptual Metaphor Theory (CMT)?

Answer: CMT suggests that conceptual metaphors have origins in embodied
experiences, shaping our fundamental conceptual system.

According to Lakoff and Johnson, what is an example of an orientational
metaphor?

Answer: An example is the orientational metaphor that sickness and death are
down, rooted in the embodied experience of lying down due to illness.

Topic-03: Metaphor, Context and Discourse

Why is the analysis of metaphor choice and use important in CDS?

Answer: The analysis is important to understand how context, including social,
cultural, and historical factors, shapes and influences metaphor use in discourse.

What role does contextual dynamics play in metaphor use?

Answer: Contextual dynamics can make metaphor use strategic, with discourse
participants deploying metaphors intentionally to frame topics advantageously.

According to Steen's model, what are the three dimensions of metaphor?

Answer: The three dimensions are linguistic, conceptual, and communicative.
