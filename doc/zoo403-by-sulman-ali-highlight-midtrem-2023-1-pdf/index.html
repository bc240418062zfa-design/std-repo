Insight learning:
A type of learning that uses reason, especially to form conclusions, inferences, or judgments, to
solve a problem. It is based on advanced perceptual abilities such as thought and reasoning.
EXAMPLE: Kohler found that chimpanzees could use insight learning instead of trial-and
error to solve problems. In one example, a banana was placed high out of reach that the
chimpanzees found a way to reach it. They stack boxes on top of each other to reach it and used
sticks to knock the banana down.

what are the types of the learning behaviour in biology.
Habituation, imprinting, classical conditioning, operant conditioning, and cognitive learning.

what are the feature detection: is how our brain filter the info we get from our senses so that we can

Feature detection is a process by which the nervous system sorts or filters complex natural
stimuli in order to extract behaviorally relevant cues.
The three major groups of so-called feature detectors in visual cortex include simple cells,
complex cells, and hypercomplex cells.

Explain instinctive behavior with the example of birds how they make their
nests:
Innate behavior is also called instinctive behavior. Instinct is the ability of an animal to perform
a particular behavior in response to a given stimulus the first time the animal is exposed to the
stimulus. In other words, an instinctive behavior does not have to be learned or practiced.
A bird nest is the spot in which a bird lays and incubates its eggs and raises its young. For most
birds, when a male and female form a breeding pair nest building activities will follow.

Nest Building Behaviour: A nest site is first selected and nest building begins. This can
involve both birds as a pair or either sex alone. Different species of birds build different nests.
Some nests are very simple constructions, whilst others are more complex. Birds usually only
build nests for holding their eggs and rearing chicks.

Neuron components
Neuron has three components: 1= Cell body 2= Axon (moving nerve impulse away from cell
body) 3= Dendron (moving nerve impulse toward the cell body).

Imprinting:
“Formation of a longlasting behavioral attachment to a particular individual or object at a
specific stage in life”
• A type of learning behavior • A very young animal develops attachment with the very first
object it has visual, auditory or tactile experience • It thereafter follows that object

Examples:
• Seagulls • Sensitive period for a parent to bond with its young lasts one to two days • If
bonding does not occur, parent will not care for the infant, leading to death of the offspring.
