1. The tool developed for authors, writers, journalists and other professionals to increases their daily
   productivity and word count is______. Smart typewriter

3. The type of listening in which the listener expresses empathy toward the speaker allowing him or her to
   talk through a problem is______. Therapeutic

4. The_____ App is designed to help students improve paragraph formation, integration of idea, inference
   and abstract thinking and expression. Story builder

5. The interactive whiteboard (IWB) was invented in 1990 by. Palo Alto Research Center

6. Which of the following factors in listening process is responsible for determining whether or not the
   visual or auditory signal is processed and proceeds into short-term memory? Attention

7. One can use____ to create customized illustrations, icons and visuals with a few drags of mouse.
   AutoDraw

8. There is no better App than____ for traditional essay writing in classrooms. Google Docs

9. The technological capacity to manipulate digitized audio and video files increases students ______ in
   listening. Involvement

10. The educational technology goes beyond the use of____ aids because of the ubiquity and usefulness of
   technology in everyday life. Instructional

11. Which of the following means can be used in both web enhanced and blended classes to display work
   created by students as course projects? Power point

12. In the ____ learning mode the mechanism of human expression is also no longer available. Online

13. Computers can be present the text in a variety of ways apart from a plain _____ one form. Printed

14. The best advantage of podcasts is that they provide_____ learning. Mobile

15. The e-reading _____ allows readers to interact with the texts through a range of applications and
   programs. Software

16. An instructor can monitor the comments posted in the _____ groups. Discussion

17. The_____ grade can be devised by defining, what participation means in context of face to face class
   meeting and an online discussion. Participation

18. Students can be informed through _____ emails for continuing online activities in blended courses.
   Weekly

19. The two readily available online tools to most instructions are email and _______. Chat

20. Online lectures offer other _____ as well. Advantages

21. Online chat_____ can be used to conduct virtual office hours. Software

22. One of the biggest errors in attempting _____ courses is lack of clear directions about what will be
   accomplished in each mode and how to coordinate the two. Blended

23. FAQs stand for ______. Frequently asked questions

24. In the ______ period of history, the main focus of foreign language learning was on grammar.
   Renaissance

25. The courses in which both online and face to face instructional elements are required and
   complementary are named as ______ courses. Blended

26. To identify the simple facts presented in a written text is defined as_____ skill. Literal comprehension

27. The ability to choose a _________ means that different learners are not all constrained to learn the same
   materials in the same way but may instead find new answers and solutions to questions and problems.
   Path of learning

28. Results from many ____________ environments show features of simplified registers such as omission
   of subject pronouns, articles as well as use of contractions and abbreviations. Computer mediated
   communication (CMC).

29. Li (2006) conducted a study which looked at __________ students at 3 junior high schools and reported
   that half had been the subject of bullying and about a quarter had been victims of cyberbullying. 264

30. It uses the skills of many people including materials writers, content editors, graphic designers,
   animators, videographers etc. while ____________ professional software. authoring

   sequencing these parts into a hierarchy ranging from simple to more complex is a concept presented by
   ______________. C.T. Fosnot

32. One of the great advantages of ________ over some other forms of communication is the record of both
   one's own messages and the message one receives. Email

33. In terms of CALL, the optimal input for acquisition is not __________ sequenced. grammatically

   revealing thoughts such as ' I think I have a lot of vocabulary that I don't know'. ESL

35. Liuo (1993) described the use of a _________ checking program in a first - year writing and grammar
   course for EFL majors at a University in Taiwan. Grammar

36. For the quality _________ of CALL the evidence suggests that the learner has acquired the Target forms
   that were focused on during the CALL Task. Language learning potential

37. In light of E.G Rozycki proposed ideas the _________ theory has provided the rationale for a variety of
   marketable although short - lived teaching devices. Behaviorist

   with reference to traditional paper-based leaning materials they also hold true for ________ based
