                          Subjective Questions

Please Remember that the best method for fish farming is Composite Fish

Q1. What are the different methods of fish collection? Explain any two methods.
A1. The different methods of fish collection are:
1) Fishing without gears like hand picking, fishing by diving, fishing by trained
animals like dogs, otters etc.
2) Grappling and wounding gears like clamps, tongs and rakes.
3) Lines like handlines, set lines, drift lines, troll lines.
4) Traps like providing hiding places, barriers.

Q2. What are the different types of fixatives used for preserving fish specimens?
A2. The different types of fixatives used are:
1) Formalin - 10% buffered formalin solution
2) Paraformaldehyde - Can be used to make formalin solution
3) Alcohol - Ethanol and Isopropanol

Q3. What is the importance of proper labeling in specimen preservation?

A3. Proper labeling is important as it provides essential information about the
specimen like date and place of collection, ecological details, method of capture etc.
This information is as valuable as the specimen itself for future research.

Q4. What are the different measurements taken for taxonomic description of
fishes?
A4. The different measurements taken are:
1) Total Length
2) Standard Length
3) Head Length
4) Eye Diameter
5) Pectoral Fin Length
6) Pelvic Fin Length
7) Dorsal Fin Length
8) Anal Fin Length

Q5. What information should be present on the museum labels?
A5. Museum labels should contain information about the exact locality, coordinates,
nearest landmark, date of collection, name of collector and any additional ecological
data related to the specimen.

Q6. What organs and specializations help the rohu fish survive in its freshwater
habitat?
A6. Rohu fish has gills, heart, swim bladder and other organs to help it breathe, float,
digest food etc. It also has mucus glands, scales, fins and tail that aids its movement
and adaptation in the aquatic environment. The lateral line sense organs help it locate
prey and sense surroundings.

Q7. What are the different regions of fish skull in Labeo rohita?
A7. The four regions of skull in Labeo rohita are:
1) Ethmoidal region
2) Orbito temporal region
3) Otic region
4) Occipital region

Q8. Differentiate between the exoskeleton and endoskeleton of Labeo rohita.
A8. Exoskeleton comprises of scales and fin rays that provide outer covering and
support. Endoskeleton forms the internal bony framework and includes skull,
vertebral column, ribs and girdles.

Q9. What are the different water quality parameters tested to determine health
of water bodies?
A9. The different water quality parameters tested are:
1) Physical parameters like temperature, color, turbidity etc.
2) Chemical parameters like pH, dissolved oxygen, hardness, BOD, COD etc.
3) Biological parameters indicating microorganisms like bacteria, algae etc.

Q10. What are some natural and anthropogenic factors affecting water quality?
A10. Factors affecting water quality include:
- Atmospheric pollution
- Runoff
- Erosion and Sedimentation
- Discharge of domestic and industrial waste

Q11. Why is dissolved oxygen an important indicator of water quality?

A11. Dissolved oxygen concentration indicates the degree of pollution in water
bodies. Lower DO levels imply poorer water quality and high microbial activity due
to excessive waste.

Q12. How can turbidity affect the usage of water?
A12. High turbidity makes water aesthetically unappealing, reduces ability of light
to pass through water and affects processes like disinfection. It also indicates presence
of suspended impurities.

Q13. What are the structures present under each gill cover of fish?
A13. Under each gill cover there are four gill arches. Each gill arch has gill filaments
on the outside and gill rakers on the inside.

Q14. Which system did the pectoral and pelvic fins of fish evolve into in the first
tetrapods?
A14. Pectoral fins evolved into the front legs and pelvic fins evolved into the hind
legs of primitive tetrapods.

Q15. What is the function of mucus coating on skin of fish?
A15. Mucus coating helps reduce friction and provides protection to the fish skin
from abrasion and water flow.

Q16. What is the basin concept that should be considered while collecting fish
specimens?
A16. The basin concept emphasizes studying the geographical distribution of aquatic
systems based on drainages and basins rather than political boundaries. This provides
a more accurate ecological picture.

Q17. Name the different types of fishing crafts and gears.

A17. The types of fishing crafts are nets, boats/ships. Fishing gears include lines,
traps, wounding gears like spears, hooks etc.

Q18. What are the different types of chemical preservatives used for tissues in
genetic studies?
A18. For DNA analysis without PCR, tissues should be preserved fresh in buffer
solutions. For protein electrophoresis, tissues are preserved fresh or fresh frozen. For
DNA analysis with PCR, small amounts of tissues can be stored in ethanol.

Q19. What technique is used to determine the age of fishes?
A19. The age of fishes can be determined by counting the concentric ridges on scales
called circulii.

Q20. What is the importance of accurate species taxonomy and classification for
conservation efforts?
A20. Accurate taxonomy helps assess biodiversity correctly and identify threatened
species and populations for targeting conservation strategies.

Q21. What structures are found in the inner ear of fishes?
A21. The inner ear has three semicircular canals and three chambers - utriculus,
sacculus and lagena. Each chamber contains an otolith.

Q22. What adaptations help Labeo rohita survive as a herbivorous fish?
A22. Labeo rohita has a long convoluted intestine, absence of stomach, intestinal bulb
for storage and digestion of plant food.
