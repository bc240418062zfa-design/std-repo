Stability Analysis
Stability analysis is the study of the numerical methods to know whether they are stable or unstable or
what are the conditions under which a numerical method while solving an equation, system of equations
or difference equation is stable.
A numerical scheme is unstable when, during the solution procedure the result becomes unbounded
eventually reaching over flow, independent of choice computing system.
The phenomenon of instability arises from the numerical procedure itself tends to amplify errors to the
point that the amplified error obliterates the solution.

In many cases of practical interest stability analysis can be performed with Fourier method. Fourier
analysis is performed by expanding the solution in space Fourier components over the discretization.
Then we can just study the evolution of a generic Fourier component between time steps, since made are
orthogonal and independent of each other. A Fourier mode is expressed as ,
