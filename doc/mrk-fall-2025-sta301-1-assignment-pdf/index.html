Question 2
Analyze the data provided in Question 1 to determine the range and the
interquartile range (IQR)

Solution 2
Data:
        56, 62, 68, 74, 71, 69, 85, 78, 81, 55, 60, 67, 72, 66, 59, 73, 88, 90,
        47, 52, 64, 79, 61, 70
Ascending order:
        47, 52, 55, 56, 59, 60, 61, 62, 64, 66, 67, 68, 69, 70, 71, 72, 73, 74,
        78, 79, 81, 85, 88, 90

1. Range

        Range = Maximum – Minimum

              = 90 – 47 = 43

2. Interquartile Range (IQR)

        Total observations (n) = 24

Lower half:

        47, 52, 55, 56, 59, 60, 61, 62, 64, 66, 67, 68

        Q1 = median of lower half = average of 6th & 7th values

           = (60 + 61) / 2 = 60.5

Upper half:

          69, 70, 71, 72, 73, 74, 78, 79, 81, 85, 88, 90

          Q3 = median of upper half = average of 6th & 7th values

             = (74 + 78) / 2 = 76

IQR = Q3 – Q1

      = 76 – 60.5 = 15.5

So:

      I. Range= 43
      II. Interquartile range (IQR) = 15.5
