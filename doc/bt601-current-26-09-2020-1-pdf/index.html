   1. What is gene?
      A segment of DNA molecule that encodes for synthesis of a single polypeptide
      The entire DNA sequence of an individual/species
   2. How a normal cell is converted into the cancer cell?
      Oncogene – a gene that when mutated or expressed at abnormally high levels
      contributes to converting a normal cell into a cancer cell

   3. Define intron and exon
      Introns:- The non coding region on the viral genome.
      Exons:- The coding regions on the viral genome.

   4. Define lysogens?
      A lysogen or lysogenic bacterium is a bacterial cell in which a phage exists as DNA in its
      dormant state (prophage)

   5. Viroid transmission?
      • By aphids • By cross contamination • Due to mechanical damage to plants as a result
      of horticultural or agricultural practices • From plant to plant by leaf contact

   6. Structure of prions?
      The protein that prions are made of (PrP) is found throughout the body, even in healthy
      people and animals. PrPC is a normal protein found on the membranes of cells. It has
      209 amino acids (in humans) One disulfide bond, A molecular mass of 35–36 kDa A
      mainly alpha-helical structure. The normal protein is not sedimentable; meaning that it
      cannot be separated by centrifuging techniques.

   7. Fluorescence?
      The ability of substances to absorb short wavelengths of light (ultraviolet) and give off
      light at a longer wavelength(visible). It is used in fluorescence microscopy.

   8. T-phage?
      Head and tail • Called binal symmetry • Icosahederal head and helical tail symmetry.
