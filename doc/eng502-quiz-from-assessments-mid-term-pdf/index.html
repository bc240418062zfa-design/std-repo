Quiz from Assessments (Mid-Term)

                             ENG502_Introduction to Linguistics
                             Quiz from Assessments (Mid-Term)

1. According to Sapir, language is a purely human and ___________ method of communicating
   ideas, desires and emotions. (correct)
2. Language is described as a learnt behavior by the ___________ involving a stimulus and a
   response. (structuralists, anthropologist, linguists, behaviourists)
3. According to Sapir’s definition of language; ‘language is not inherited from the parents by the
   child’. (correct)
4. According to Hall, 'language symbols' are the vocal signals that are actually transmitted from
   the sender to receiver in the process of communication and interaction. (correct)
5. It is the job of a _____________ to differentiate between sentence and non sentence sequences.
   (Educationist, Socialist, Capitalist, Linguist)
6. Communication is a process whereby there is an exchange of information between the sender
   and the encoder. (Incorrect)
7. Chomsky is a rationalist rather than empiricist. (correct)
8. According to Chomsky’s belief, a child is born with an ________ capacity to learn a language.
   (organic, genetic, environmental, innate)
9. The scientific study of language and its structure is called _________. (Sociolinguistics,
   Psycholinguistics, Linguistics, Neurolinguistics)
10.Bloch and Trager’s definition of language focuses on the property of __________ in language
   while having no appeal to its communication system. (agreement, organization, structure,
   arbitrariness)
11.In linguistics, __________ communication refers to the fact that some chemicals are emitted
   from the bodies of animals in order to communicate. (elemental, natural, chemo, material)
12.A classification of a language is based on ___________________ and dissimilarities in words
   and grammatical elements. (deformity, depravity, similarities, mutation)
13.‘Bekos’ is a Phrygian word which means ____________________. (egg, bread, banana, butter)
14.If great apes had physically been able to speak, their ‘speech’ would have been similar to how
   we understand today. (Incorrect)
15.There are three reasons for linguistic similarity: genealogical sharing, areal diffusion and
   chance typological commonality. (correct)
16.The three general classes of script include_________, syllabic and alphabetic. (sequential,
   logographic, polygraphic, serial)
17.When was the first Chinese dictionary compiled? (1100-900 BC, 700 500 BC, 1400-1200 BC,
   900-700 BC)
18.Bipedals were the type of apes that could not talk but were able to __________. (speak, fight,
   struggle, walk)
19.Initially, _________________ grammarians discussed word as a linguistic unit of sentence and
   also studied form versus substance. (American, Indian, African, Russian)
