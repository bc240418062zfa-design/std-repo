1. The monthly statement ___________ the customer of outstanding bills.

 Options:

 a) ignores

 b) appeals

 c) confuses

 d) reminds

 Correct option: d) reminds

2. Keeping the customer and building goodwill is one of the purposes of ________________.

 Options:

 a) inquiry letters

 b) collection letters

 c) complaint letters

 d) cover letters

 Correct option: b) collection letters

3. The final letter in the collection series, when all efforts have failed, threatens to turn the matter over
to a ________________.

 Options:

 a) Foreign agency

 b) Collection agency

 c) Security agency

 d) Investigation agency

 Correct option: b) Collection agency

4. One of the various reasons for refusing credit is lack of established credit.

 Options:

 a) False

 b) True

 Correct option: b) True

5. An informal communication network or grapevine tends to be most active when employees believe
the formal communication network is not providing the information they need.

 Options:

 a) False

 b) True

 Correct option: b) True

6. Unsolicited letter is a ______________ of sales letters.

 Options:

 a) Kind

 b) Way

 c) Division

 d) Discussion

 Correct option: a) Kind

7. A collection letter should be written with the assumption that most of the people will ________.

 Options:

 a) not pay

 b) pay

 c) ignore

 d) delay

 Correct option: a) not pay

8. In _________ your appeal for payment should not threaten to take the debtor to court unless you
actually plan to.

 Options:

 a) Stage 3

 b) Stage 4

 c) Stage 2

 d) Stage 1

 Correct option: a) Stage 3

9. The ________ of a memorandum is usually printed.

 Options:

 a) Salutation

 b) Body

 c) Heading

 d) Conclusion

 Correct option: c) Heading

10. A 'collection letter' should tell about the ______________.

  Options:

  a) Amount owed

  b) Required action

  c) Overdue date

  d) All of the above

  Correct option: d) All of the above
