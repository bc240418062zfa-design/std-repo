Key Definitions / Concepts
1. Pragmatic Competence

Ability to use language appropriately according to social situations and context.

2. Discourse Competence

Ability to understand and produce connected spoken or written texts logically.

3. Extensive Reading

Reading large amounts of easy and interesting material for pleasure and fluency development.

4. Intensive Reading (3 Characteristics)

      Focuses on detailed understanding.
      Studies vocabulary and grammar.
      Teacher-guided reading.

5. Independent Reading

Reading done individually by learners according to their interests.

6. Phonemic Awareness

Knowledge that spoken words are made up of separate sounds (phonemes). Example: cat = /k/
/æ/ /t/.

7. Schema Theory

Reading comprehension depends on the reader's prior knowledge and experiences.

8. Fluency Reading

Reading quickly, accurately, and with understanding.

9. Paired Reading

Two learners read together to improve fluency and confidence.
