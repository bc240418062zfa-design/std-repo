Lesson 06
             GRAMMAR TRANSLATION METHOD
Topic-026: What is Grammar Translation Method?
What is the Grammar Translation Method?
The Grammar Translation Method is a method of teaching foreign languages that
focuses on analyzing grammatical rules and translating between the target
language and the native language.
Topic-027: Strengths of Grammar Translation Method
What are the strengths of the Grammar Translation Method?
The strengths of the Grammar Translation Method include forming good academic
knowledge of the target language, training grammar accuracy, and developing
students' memory.
Topic-028: Weaknesses of Grammar Translation Method

What are the weaknesses of the Grammar Translation Method?
The weaknesses of the Grammar Translation Method include a lack of emphasis
on speaking and listening skills, limited use of language for communication, and a
focus on translating sentences rather than on their content.
Topic-029: Grammar Translation Method: Opportunities and Threats
What are the opportunities of the Grammar Translation Method?
The Grammar Translation Method provides opportunities for reading classical
literature, gaining historical knowledge, and practicing translation.
What are the threats of the Grammar Translation Method?
The threats of the Grammar Translation Method include demotivating learners
with mechanical exercises where meaning is not prioritized, and an emphasis on
accurate form over meaningful communication.
Topic-030: Grammar Translation Method in Pakistan
What is the current status of the Grammar Translation Method (GTM)?
The Grammar Translation Method is still used in some educational settings,
especially in Pakistan.
What are some aspects related to GTM in Pakistan?
Aspects related to GTM in Pakistan include its use in schools, the medium of
instruction, differences between public and private schools, and its impact on
student skills, especially in writing.
What are the consequences of the Grammar Translation Method on students in
Pakistan?
Consequences of the Grammar Translation Method in Pakistan include students
facing issues with spoken skills, a lack of communicative competence, and
problems at the undergraduate and graduate levels, particularly in meeting job
market requirements.

Lesson-07
                         THE DIRECT METHOD
Topic-031: What is the Direct Method?
What is the Direct Method (DM)?
The Direct Method is a language teaching method that emphasizes the ability to
use language for communication rather than analyzing it. It focuses on spoken
language, actions, and pictures, with a prohibition on using the mother tongue.
What are the aims of the Direct Method?
The aims of the Direct Method include building a direct way into the world of the
target language, enabling students to communicate in the target language, and
replicating the way students learn their mother tongue.
What were the primary aims of the Direct Method?
The Direct Method aimed to build a direct connection between the learner and
the target language. It focused on creating a relationship between experiences

and language, words and ideas, thoughts and expressions, and rules and
performance. Its primary goal was to teach students how to communicate
effectively in the target language, with a strong belief in mimicking the natural
language learning process without the interference of the native language. In
essence, the DM sought to eliminate translation and teach concepts through
objects or contexts while promoting oral training and indirect grammar teaching.
Topic-032: Strengths and Weaknesses of the Direct Method
What are the strengths of the Direct Method?
The strengths of the Direct Method include practical goals, immersion in a foreign
environment, emphasis on speaking and listening practice, and a focus on
authentic materials.
What are the weaknesses of the Direct Method?
The weaknesses of the Direct Method include an underestimated role of reading
and writing, a prohibition on using the mother tongue, excessive mechanical
drilling, and a lack of flexibility.
