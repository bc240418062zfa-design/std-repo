Q1: There was a Dragon software question for 3 marks.
Dragon software is a dictation tool that converts spoken words into text for
multiple English accents and other languages (e.g., French, German).
It helps language learners improve pronunciation by identifying errors when their
speech doesn’t align with programmed norms.
Provides a feedback loop for learners to refine their speech and improve
comprehension.
Q2: What is L2 writing-based analytics?
Writing-based analytics, like Mi-Writer, track the entire writing process
(planning, drafting, revising, and editing).
These tools record interactions with teachers and peers, offering insights into
writing habits.
They provide personalized feedback, improving both writing quality and learning
outcomes.
Q3: Define blended learning and give its two different learning environments.
Blended Learning: A teaching method combining face-to-face (synchronous) and
online (asynchronous) learning.
Learning Environments:
   1. Face-to-Face: Classroom learning with direct teacher-student interaction.
   2. Online: Independent, flexible learning through digital platforms.
Q4: What are three forms of assessment and e-assessment?
Forms of E-Assessment:
   1. Online tests, quizzes, and exams.
   2. E-submission and e-marking of assignments.
   3. Peer-assessment and student response systems for collaborative learning.
Q5: How do linguists use computer technology?

Linguists use technology to study second language acquisition (SLA) in controlled
environments.
For example, they use computers to conduct grammar tests or speed-based
assessments.
CALL (Computer-Assisted Language Learning) evolved in the 1980s to focus on
using technology for language learning.
Q6: Why was accessing information on the internet not easy in the past?
Early internet access was limited by slow speeds, lack of user-friendly tools, and
restricted resources.
Searching for information required specialized knowledge, and only limited
content was available online.
Q7: What are the benefits of CAPT (Computer-Assisted Pronunciation
Training)?
CAPT Benefits:
   1. Provides a risk-free environment for learners to practice pronunciation.
   2. Offers immediate feedback without judgment.
   3. Can simulate diverse speech sounds and accents for better learning.
Q8: What is self-assessment in e-learning?
Self-assessment allows learners to evaluate their progress and learning
independently.
Benefits: Encourages critical thinking, autonomy, and reflection on learning.
Q9: Define LMS and list its benefits.
LMS (Learning Management System): Software for managing and delivering
educational content online.
Benefits:
   1. Tracks grades, assignments, and resources.
   2. Offers consistency across institutions.
   3. Can integrate with external tools for additional features.

Q10: What is the CCA program?
Colossal Cave Adventure (CCA): A text-based game created in 1975-76 by Will
Crowther.
It combined real-world cave exploration with fantasy elements, marking the
beginning of virtual worlds.
Q11: What is the PLATO program?
PLATO was an educational software system created using the TUTOR
programming language.
It enabled teachers to design their own exercises, focusing on grammar,
vocabulary, and reading skills.
Features included multiple-choice questions, touch-sensitive screens for interactive
learning, and multimedia tools like audio.
Q12: What is meant by ‘affordance’ in digital tools?
Affordance: The potential uses or benefits of a technology perceived by its users.
For example:
   1. Subtitles in videos can aid comprehension.
   2. Interactive features like pausing and annotating videos can enhance learning.
Q13: What are e-book libraries, and why are they important?
E-Book Libraries: Digital collections of books accessible online, like Project
Gutenberg.
Benefits:
   1. Free access to a wide range of books.
   2. Portable and convenient for reading anytime, anywhere.
Q14: What are the benefits of interactive whiteboards?
Interactive Whiteboards: Large touch-sensitive screens used in classrooms.
Benefits:
   1. Make lessons engaging and interactive.

   2. Allow students to touch, draw, and collaborate.
   3. Simplify teaching by integrating multimedia tools like videos, graphs, and
      images.
Q15: What are two types of web-based management education systems?
Web-Based Training Types:
   1. Synchronous: Real-time training through live classes or webinars.
   2. Asynchronous: Self-paced learning with pre-recorded content or online
      resources.
Q16: What is curriculum design?
Curriculum Design: Organizing the learning objectives, content, and methods for
a course.
Purpose: Ensures alignment between what students learn at different stages (e.g.,
elementary to high school).
Q17: What are three general cognitive science principles?
   1. Channel Assumption: The brain processes information through verbal and
      visual channels.
   2. Limited Capacity Assumption: Each channel has a limited capacity for
      processing information.
   3. Active Processing Assumption: Learning happens when learners actively
      organize and integrate new information.
Q18: What are three categories of general technology for L2 learning?
   1. Computer-Assisted Language Learning (CALL): Includes language
      software and online exercises.
   2. Computer-Mediated Communication (CMC): Tools like video
      conferencing for communication.
   3. Computer-Based Language Testing (CBLT): Online language proficiency
      assessments.
Q19: What is the MUD gaming program?

MUD (Multi-User Dungeon): A text-based online role-playing game created in
1978 by Roy Trubshaw.
It allowed multiple players to interact and compete in a virtual environment,
paving the way for modern online games.
Q20: How does technology improve language learning and teaching?
Technology offers tools like multimedia and online resources to make language
learning engaging.
Examples:
   1. Online games and apps for vocabulary.
   2. Multimedia content for grammar and pronunciation practice.
