                                        SHORT QUESTION
   1. Enlist the insects that used as human food.
Man in some parts of the world consumes large quantities of grasshoppers, crickets, ants and other
insects.
The great value of some insects and the great losses, sometimes enormous, caused by others make it
obviously important to encourage the beneficial forms while striving to prevent or at least to reduce
the losses caused by the injurious ones
   2. Cultural method
Cultural control focuses on changing the environment to make it less favorable for insects. This can
include practices like crop rotation, planting resistant varieties, and adjusting irrigation and
fertilization to reduce pest habitat.
   3. Why pests brought in our country?
Pests can be brought into a country through various means such as international trade, travel, or even
natural migration. Sometimes, pests can hitch a ride on imported goods, plants, or animals. They can
also spread through wind, water, or by simply moving from one place to another. It’s important for
countries to have strict regulations and quarantine measures to prevent the introduction and spread
of invasive pests.
   4. What is coxa?
The coxa is the segment that articulates with the body, frequently partly lying in a more or less cup
shaped hollow of the latter. It may be short or long and is generally freely movable on the body and
powerful. This arrangement allows for movement and flexibility.
   5. Integrated pest management?
Integrated Pest Management (IPM) is an approach that focuses on using a combination of techniques
to manage pests in a sustainable and environmentally-friendly way. It involves strategies like
biological control, cultural practices, and targeted pesticide use, all while minimizing harm to
beneficial organisms and the environment. IPM aims to reduce reliance on chemical pesticides and
promote long-term pest management. It’s a great approach for maintaining healthy plants and
ecosystems.
   6. Define pulvilus, pulvilli and empodium?
Pulivillus: a soft, pad like structure located at the base of each claw on the feet of certain insects.
Pulvilli: Pulvilli are lobes or pads that are located between the tarsal claws of many insects.
Empodium: Between the pulvilli there is often a spine that is called the empodium

   10.      What is cerci?
A pair of many-segmented, antenna-like structures, sometimes short, sometimes long, may occur at
the end of the abdomen. And these are called cerci. They probably serve as organs of touch and
possibly organ of smell in some cases.
   11.        How topography affect the insect distribution.
Topography can influence insect population distribution by affecting factors like temperature,
moisture, and habitat availability. For instance, slopes and elevation can create microclimates that
influence insect preferences for nesting, feeding, and survival
   12.        How insect attack on injurious species.
Insects use various strategies to attack injurious species such as:
i)They can use physical mechanisms like predation or parasitism, where they consume or lay eggs on
the harmful species.
ii) Some insects also release chemical substances, like venom, to attack on injurious species.
iii) Some insects lay eggs on injurious species and reduced their reproductive potential.
   13.        Attractant with example.
Attractants or sometimes called attrahents are materials used to lure insects to traps and poisoned
baits. The use of geraniol in Japanese beetle traps, sugar sirups for ant baits, and fermenting sirup
solutions for trapping various moths are good examples.

   17.        function of cuticle in insect
The cuticle in insects serves several important functions. It acts as a protective outer covering,
providing support, waterproofing, and regulating gas exchange. Additionally, the cuticle contains
sensory structures for detecting stimuli.
