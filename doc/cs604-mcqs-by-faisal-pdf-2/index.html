then your _________ system call will fail.

      Pipe (Page#52)

The pipe() system call fails for many reasons, including the following:
1) At least two slots are not empty in the PPFDT 2) too many files or pipes are open in the process
3) Buffer space not available in the kernel          4) File table is full
