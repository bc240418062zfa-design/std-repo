1. The oligonucleotide ligation assay (OLA) is commonly used to detect
___________ known to be associated with human diseases with a high degree of
accuracy.
 Options:
 a) Gene
 b) Antigen
 c) SNPs
 d) Protein
 Correct option: c) SNPs

2. Which statement about the probe is correct?
 Options:
 a) The 5' end of the probe is labeled with a fluorescent reporter molecule.
 b) The 5' end of the probe is labeled with a fluorescent quencher molecule.
 c) The 3' end of the probe is labeled with a fluorescent quencher molecule.
 d) The 3' end of the probe is labeled with a fluorescent reporter molecule.
 Correct option: a) The 5' end of the probe is labeled with a fluorescent reporter
molecule.

3. Which method for the detection of penaeid shrimp viruses is now used
routinely in a number of laboratories around the world?
 Options:
 a) DNA-based detection
 b) ELISA-based detection
 c) Probe-based detection
 d) Protein-based detection
 Correct option: a) DNA-based detection

4. In susceptible individuals, the first exposure to an allergen elicits the production
of high levels of ___________ antibodies that bind to mast cells.
 Options:
 a) IgE
 b) IgM
 c) IgG
 d) IgA
 Correct option: a) IgE
