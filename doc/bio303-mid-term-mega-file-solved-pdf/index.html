 5. Ping pong mechanism?
Ping-pong mechanism of nucleoside diphosphate kinase:
in this mechanism the enzyme binds its first substrate and a phosphoryl group is
transferred to the side chain of His residue. ADP departs, andanother nucleoside (or
deoxynucleoside) diphosphate replaces it, and this is converted to the corresponding triphosphate
by transfer of the phosphoryl group from the phosphohistidine residue.
