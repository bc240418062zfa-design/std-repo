1. non-disruptive service relocation architecture and failover systems are utilized to ensure _________ in
PaaS instances.

Options:

a) Consistency

b) Encryption

c) Reliability

d) Integrity

Correct Option: c) Reliability

2. PaaS consumer receives the _________.

Options:

a) Failover setting

b) Data setting

c) Platform for development

d) SLA monitoring

Correct Option: c) Platform for development

3. SaaS instances are _______ from IaaS and PaaS instances due to the existence of concurrent users.

Options:

a) Unique

b) Identical

c) Permanent

d) Duplicate

Correct Option: a) Unique

4. Design consideration for the Cloud-based solution should contain considerations for power-efficient
design to reduce the environmental effect of the carbon footprint of the Cloud-based solution. This
computing is called__________.

Options:

a) Fog

b) Server-less

c) Green

d) Grid

Correct Option: c) Green

5. Every SaaS deployment has ___________ programming logic, resource requirements, and consumer
workloads.

Options:

a) Different

b) Identical

c) Permanent

d) Duplicate

Correct Option: a) Different

6. The two basic IT resources of IaaS are:

Options:

a) OS and compiler

b) VMs and Cloud storage

c) Interpreter and compiler

d) None of the given

Correct Option: b) VMs and Cloud storage

7. The template __________ can contain the plan overview, goals and objectives, types of events
covered, risk analysis, and the mitigation techniques for each type of risk identified.

Options:

a) Project management

b) Disaster recovery plan

c) Risk management

d) Disk recovery program

Correct Option: b) Disaster recovery plan

8. In cloud solution design matrix, ________ identify the uptime requirements of the cloud application.

Options:

a) Accessibility

b) Audit

c) Backup

d) Availability

Correct Option: d) Availability

9. Bare-metal provisioning is also provided to the consumers with ____________ access.

Options:

a) User level

b) Administrative

c) Restrictive

d) Limit

Correct Option: b) Administrative

10. The broker searches for the ________ options of Cloud providers for the consumer.

Options:

a) Costly

b) Permanent

c) Best

d) Temporary

Correct Option: c) Best
