       Question No 1
       Organizing stage involves a careful outlining of the topic which is helpful for various reasons.
       Discuss those reasons.
Answer:
Careful outlining is helpful for several reasons: One, it improves the clarity of your message; Second, it saves
writing time later (changes in outline are easy than in writing material); and Third, it enables to emphasize
properly the various topics in the message (placing the most important at the beginning is more emphatic than
in the middle)
   1. Question No 2
Almost every customer who makes a claim is emotionally involved, therefore, the indirect method is
usually the best approach for a refusal Discuss
Answer:
Refusing a claim
When refusing a claim, avoid language that might have a negative impact on the reader. Instead, demonstrate
that you understand and have considered the complaint. The, even if the claim is unreasonable, rationally
explain why you are refusing request. Remember, don't apologize and don't rely on company policy. End the
letter on a respectful and action-oriented note.
   2. Question 3
What factors are important to achieve balance between logical appeal and emotional appeal?
Answer:

Finding the right balance between the two types of appeals depends on four factors:
       The actions you wish to motivate
       Your reader's expectations,
       The degree of resistance you must overcome,
       How far you feel empowered to go in selling your point of view
   3.   Question 4
Discuss the characteristics and importance of conciseness for effective written communication.
Answer
Conciseness in written communication means expressing ideas clearly and briefly, without unnecessary words
or details. It focuses on essential information, eliminates redundancy, and uses precise language. Being
concise enhances clarity, saves time, keeps readers engaged, and showcases professionalism. Concise writing
is adaptable for various contexts and improves retention of information. Its importance lies in delivering
messages efficiently and effectively, making the exchange of information more streamlined and impactful.
   4. Question 5
What is a functional resume?
Answer
A functional resume is a type of resume format that emphasizes a candidate's skills and accomplishments
rather than a chronological work history. It groups skills into categories and showcases achievements to
highlight the candidate's abilities and qualifications for a specific role.
   5. Question 7
In an indirect approach for writing a disappointing news letter the disappointing news is deemphasized.
Discuss.
Answer
In an indirect approach to writing a disappointing news letter, the negative message is downplayed or softened
using techniques like positive language, buffers, and suggestions for alternatives. The main goal is to deliver
the disappointing news in a more tactful manner, minimizing its impact and maintaining a positive relationship
with the recipient.
   6. Question 8
Write down the elements of the suggested plan for writing a direct-inquiry letter?
Answer
Elements of a suggested plan for writing a direct-inquiry letter:
Introduction: Begin with a polite greeting and introduce yourself or your company. Clearly state the purpose
of the letter, which is to inquire about a specific topic or request information.

Background Information: Provide context or a brief explanation of why you are making the inquiry. Include
any relevant details that will help the recipient understand the reason for your inquiry.
Specific Inquiry: Clearly and concisely state the specific information you are seeking or the questions you
want to be answered. Make sure your inquiries are well-organized and easy to understand.
Request for Response: Politely ask for a timely response to your inquiry and include your preferred method
of contact (email, phone number, etc.).
Appreciation and Closing: Thank the recipient for their attention and consideration. End the letter with a
courteous closing, such as "Sincerely" or "Thank you," followed by your name and contact information.
Follow-up: If necessary, mention that you will follow up on the inquiry at a specific time or date if you don't
receive a response.
