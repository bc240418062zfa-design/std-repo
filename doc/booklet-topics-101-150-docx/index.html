115. Payment Gateway in Pakistan: What is Payment Gateway
Payment Gateway helps clients (End users) to directly transfer amount into vendor account. This payment gateway does not work under the ownership of online store rather this service is provided to the vendor by a bank or payment processor. Vendor can link their bank account with the payment gateway and the money from the end user is transferred to their linked account. Payment Gateway is integrated into Online Store. The whole process is shown below:

    116. Payment Gateway in Pakistan - Making Online Payment in Pakistan
Receiving and sending Online Payments is major concern and unfortunately Pakistan does not have its own home grown Payment Gateway. Cash on Delivery is largest source of receiving payment and solutions like Western Union does not transfer amount into Bank Accounts. But the development of the payment gateway in is process and Telecom Companies like Zong, Telenor and Jazz have developed their payment solution to receive money into Bank Account. Banks are not lagging behind and HBL has provided Internet Payment Gateway Solution to be integrated into E-Commerce shop. Using Service like Skrill or Payoneer are helping companies and individuals to receive online payment and withdraw it through ATM’s
    117. Payment Gateway - What is PayPal?
Confinity was founded by Max Levchin, Peter Thiel and Luke Nosek in 1998 and x.com was launched in 1999 by Elon Musk and that ultimately merged with Confinity to launch Paypal in same year. Paypal is world largest payment processor to send and receive money and that enable individuals and Businesses to transfer money electronically. It operates in 202 countries and has 237 million customers. PayPal is not a Bank because it does not give loan but money can be withdrawn without bank account.
    118. Payment Gateway - How it Work
Paypal is like Digital Wallet with different payment options. Credit Cards, Debit Card, Master Card and Bank accounts are linked under one account. 100 of thousands website accept payment through Paypal because of many reasons. Some are as below:
    • Payment is Easy
    • Payment is secure as Payment details are not shared with vendors
    • Buyer can perform shopping without sharing sensitive data with Online Shop
    • No need to add shipping address and payment details each time you shop online
    • Payment details are PayPal purchase protection - Secure
Here are the steps of typical transaction on E-Commerce:
    1. User add the product to the product cart
    2. User finishes the shopping and chooses to checkout
    3. User chooses to checkout with PayPal and logs into the account
    4. User selects preferred payment option linked to his PayPal account
    5. User sees a confirmation page to verify that the purchase is complete
    6. PayPal notifies the seller when the funds have been received
    7. User receives an email from seller confirming that the order is in progress
    121. Payment Gateway - Limitation of PayPal in Pakistan
PayPal has presence in over 190 + countries but unfortunately PayPal gateway does not support payment processing from Pakistan. The business case of payment gateway does not seem to be fit for PayPal in Pakistan due to many reasons which include internet scams hacking and State Bank of Pakistan regulation related to electronic payments. In short, PayPal services are not available in Pakistan.
    122. Payment Gateway in Pakistan - What is EasyPaisa?
EasyPaisa is product of collaboration between Telenor and Tameer Microfinance Bank to transfer money securely, instantly between persons. The money is transferred through 4000 plus outlets across country. EasyPaisa is first End-to-End Online payment solution for Online seller and their customers and it EasyPaisa is first End-to-End Online payment solution for Online seller and their customers. Plugins are available for EasyPaisa to be integrated with Shopping Carts like Magento and Prestashop.
    123. Payment Gateway in Pakistan – EasyPaisa --- How it Work
Easypaisa Solution is an electronic payment solution that enables internet users to make financial transaction online. It can easily and seamlessly be integrated with any online website and/or shopping cart and enable users to pay online through Easypay.
Merchants should register at the EasyPaisa Website to get unique store id which would be used to integrate EasyPaisa plugin into the shopping cart. Customers will choose EasyPay as the payment option.
    125. Payment Gateway in Pakistan – Sandbox Environment
Payment gateways need to be tested before launching because stakes of small mistakes could be very high as it includes financial transaction. Sandbox environment are clone of production i.e. Testing Environment but simulate live gateway environment. Service Provider usually provides Sandbox credentials with dummy data to verify functionality of Gateway. In other words, the services are completely and thoroughly tested with dummy data with the intention of finding any flaws so that they can be eradicated before the services becomes available to end users.
