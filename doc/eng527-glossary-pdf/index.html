       Genres
Conventional text types distinguished by typical content and internal organization. Genres may
constrain verbal form or style in specific ways, such as the use of agentless passives in scientific
articles.
       Speech Events
Speech Events Instances of communication that are realized through genres. These events are
characterized by their specific contexts and purposes.
       Communicative Conventions
Rules and norms that regulate how conversation is conducted, including turn-taking, appropriate
timing, and strategies for getting the floor.
       Conversational Styles
Distinctive ways of engaging in conversation that reflect cultural, gender, or individual
differences. These styles can be cooperative, supportive, competitive, or hierarchical.
       Authentic Self
The concept that an individual's actions should align with their inner desires and truths, rather
than external cultural norms.
       Agency
The capacity of individuals to act intentionally and shape their desires and norms through
practice.
       Cultural Norms
Shared expectations and rules that guide behavior within a society.
       Performative Identity
The concept that identity is created through repeated actions rather than pre-existing the actions.
       Standpoint Theory
A feminist theory that emphasizes the importance of women's perspectives and experiences in
scholarship.
       Radical Feminism
A feminist movement that argues for the essential superiority of women's nature and challenges
traditional gender roles.
       Asymmetry
Asymmetry is a lack of equality or balance between two or more elements, leading to an uneven
distribution or disparity.

       Tomboy
A tomboy is a girl who exhibits behaviors and interests traditionally associated with boys, such
as preferring sports or rough play.
       Taboo
Taboo is a social or cultural prohibition or restriction against certain behaviors, practices, or
discussions considered inappropriate or unacceptable.
       Gender Ideology
Gender ideology is a system of beliefs and attitudes about the roles, behaviors, and expectations
associated with different genders.
       Gender Essentialism
Gender essentialism is the belief that men and women possess inherent characteristics and
behaviors that define their gender, often based on biological or natural differences.
       Habitus
Habitus is a set of habits, dispositions, and ways of thinking shaped by one's social environment
and experiences.
       Hegemony
Hegemony is the dominance of one group or state over others, often maintained through cultural,
ideological, or political influence
       Sarcasm
Sarcasm is a form of verbal irony where someone says the opposite of what they truly mean,
typically to mock, criticize, or convey contempt.
       Gender Stereotype
Gender stereotype is a generalized belief about the characteristics, roles, and behaviors typically
associated with men and women.
       Archetype
Archetype is the original pattern or model of which all things of the same type are representations
or copies.
       Power dynamics
Power dynamics refer to the ways in which power is distributed and exercised within
relationships, groups, or organizations.

Grammatical gender is a linguistic category that classifies nouns, pronouns, and adjectives into
classes based on their association with masculine, feminine, or neuter forms. It influences
agreement and usage within a language.
       Rapport Talk (Women)
Rapport talk is a communication style typically associated with women that focuses on building
relationships, sharing personal experiences, and fostering connection. It emphasizes empathy
and emotional support.
       Report Talk (Men)
Report talk is a communication style often linked to men that aims to convey information, assert
knowledge, and achieve specific outcomes. It is more focused on sharing facts and solving
problems.
       Patriarchy
Patriarchy is a social system where men hold primary power and authority in political, economic,
and familial structures, often leading to the dominance of men over women and reinforcing
traditional gender roles.
       Critical Discourse Analysis
Critical Discourse Analysis is an approach to studying language that examines how discourse
(spoken or written communication) reflects, reinforces, or challenges power structures and social
inequalities in society.
       Sex Stereotypes
Sex stereotypes are preconceived and often rigid beliefs about the characteristics, roles, and
behaviors appropriate for individuals based on their sex. These stereotypes can perpetuate
traditional gender roles and inequalities.
       Socio-Cultural Anthropology
Socio-Cultural Anthropology is the study of human societies and cultures, focusing on
understanding social practices, norms, and cultural beliefs through participant observation and
ethnographic research.
       Community of Practice
A Community of Practice is a group of people who share a common interest or profession and
engage in collective learning through regular interaction and collaboration. This community
supports the development of shared knowledge and skills.
