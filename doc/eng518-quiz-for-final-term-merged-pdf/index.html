                                   Quiz for Final-Term

   1. The main benefit of doing a ___________________ is to provide the consumer with a
      mosaic of what is happening concerning a given topic (Literature review)
   2. While constructing a literature review, you might realize that the differences in the samples
      used in the studies produced the differing results. (true)
   3. If you do not find that the same results are replicated over a variety of studies, you can have
      more confidence that you are on the right track. (False)
   4. Doing a ________________ is important if you plan to do a study yourself. (Research
      review)
   5. The first place to begin is searching for studies using _____________ sources.
      (Preliminary)
   6. In preparation for constructing your review of the research literature, you need to formulate
      a __________ procedure for cataloging and storing your information for each study.
      (Systematic)
   7. When you set up your database of studies for your review, enter the following use. First,
      record very accurately the last names and all initials for every author of each study. (true)
   8. If you have read a published literature review, you will have noticed that the author
      basically looks for eight things when summarizing the ____________ of the study. (Main
      body)
   9. The variables in the study can be of _______ types. (Four)
   10.The focus of the study means that the area or the issue must be ignored. (False)
   11.The headings that are used in reporting most primary research studies including
      Introduction, ___________, Results and Discussion. (Method)
   12._________ section of literature review gives the details regarding the makeup of the
      review. (Method)
   13.Description and __________ are in fact criteria for deciding which studies to review.
      (Rationale)
   14.__________ section of literature review is an overview of what studies will be discussed
      and their relation to one another and the review as a whole. (Result)
   15.______________ section of literature review compares and contrast the results between
      studies. (Discussion)
   16.The Qualitative–Quantitative continuum has received a lot of attention over the past
      __________ years, usually accompanied with much controversy. (20)
   17.Larsen-Freeman and Long (1991), who described the two terms, quantitative and
      qualitative as two ends of a __________. (Continuum)
   18.Quantitative research has come mainly from the field of ________. (Psychology)
   19.______________ research frequently uses sample strategies for generalizing findings to
      larger populations. (Quantitative)
   20._________ research works to uncover information from information – rich samples.
      (Qualitative)
