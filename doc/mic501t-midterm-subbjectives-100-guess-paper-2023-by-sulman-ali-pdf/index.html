Branches of mycology:
Mycology is the branch of biology concerned with the study of fungi, including their genetic and
biochemical properties, their taxonomy and their use to humans as a source for tinder, traditional
medicine, food, and entheogens, as well as their dangers, such as toxicity or infection. Branches:
1= Medical mycology, 2= Agricultural mycology, 3= Ecological mycology – Decomposers of
woods, leaves, feces and other organic matters.

Define Dry heat method of sterilization:
Dry heat sterilization methods include hot-air ovens, flaming (using a Bunsen burner for items
like platinum loops), and incineration (burning to ashes). These techniques eliminate
microorganisms by exposing them to elevated temperatures without moisture, ensuring the
destruction of pathogens through oxidative processes or direct burning.

Define Five Kingdom Classification of Organisms.
The Five Kingdom Classification classifies organisms into five groups: Monera (prokaryotic
bacteria), Protista (single-celled eukaryotes), Fungi (multicellular, absorptive heterotrophs),
Plantae (multicellular, photosynthetic autotrophs), and Animalia (multicellular, ingestive
heterotrophs). This system, proposed by R.H. Whittaker, categorizes living organisms based on
their cellular structure, nutrition, and reproduction methods.

Define Spontaneous Generation:
(Aristotle) Spontaneous generation was a once-held belief that living organisms could arise
spontaneously from non-living matter. This concept, prevalent until the 19th century, proposed
that life could emerge without pre-existing life. However, advancements in microbiology,
particularly Pasteur's experiments, disproved this notion, establishing the principle of biogenesis.

Who name the small pox and when?
Lady Mary Wortley Montagu named the smallpox in 1717, while Edward Jenner developed the
smallpox vaccine in 1796.

Give Five Types of Microscopy:

1= Darkfield Microscopy: Enhances contrast by illuminating specimens against a dark
background., 2= Phase Contrast Microscopy: Accentuates phase shifts in light passing through
transparent specimens., 3= Fluorescent Microscopy: Uses fluorescent dyes to visualize specific
cellular structures., 4= Transmission Electron Microscopy: Achieves high resolution by
transmitting electrons through thin specimens., 5= Scanning Electron Microscopy: Provides
detailed surface images using electrons.

Enlist steps of Gram Staining:
   1.   Primary Stain (Crystal Violet): Colors all cells.
   2.   Mordant (Iodine): Enhances crystal violet retention.
   3.   Decolorization (Alcohol or Acetone): Removes stain from some cells.
   4.   Counterstain (Safranin): Colors decolorized cells.
   5.   Wash and Dry: Final steps to observe Gram-positive (purple) and Gram-negative (pink)
        bacteria.

Define Metachromatic granules:
Metachromatic granules, also known as volutin, are large inclusions within bacterial cells. They
stain red with methylene blue due to their unique property of displaying color changes during
staining. Composed of inorganic phosphates, these granules are characteristic features observed
in bacteria, such as Corynebacterium diphtheriae.

Which factors can influence the Bacterial death rate?
Bacterial death rate can be influenced by factors such as temperature, pH levels, exposure to
antimicrobial agents, the concentration of nutrients, and the presence of reactive oxygen species.
These conditions affect bacterial metabolism and susceptibility to environmental stressors.

Enlist chemicals which disrupts the plasma membrane.
1= Hexachlorophene, 2= triclosan, 3= Chlorhexidine, 4= Alexidine.

Define Gene, chromosome, genotype, phenotype.
1= Gene: The basic unit of heredity passed from parent to child. 2= Chromosome: threadlike
structures made up of protein and DNA that carry genomic information from cell to cell. 3=
Genotype: Genotype is the genetic makeup of an individual cell or organism that determines or
contributes to its phenotype, 4= Phenotype: all observable characters that influenced by
genotypes and environment.

Define any Three shapes of bacteria?
1= Coccus These are circular or spherical in shape. 2= Bacillus These rod-shaped bacteria. 3=
Spiral These are curved shaped bacteria. They are further divided into 3 more subgroups. Vibrio:
curved rods Spirillum: Helical but rigid Spirochete: Helical but flexible Spirochetes move by
axial filaments which are enclosed by the outer membrane.

Write the name of five layers of endospore.
1) Exosporium: A thin delicate outermost covering of the spore 2) Spore coat: 2nd layer
underneath the Exosporium. It is thick and composed of several protein layers 3) Cortex: It is the
3rd layer from outside in. It has peptidoglycan in it. 4) Spore cell wall or core wall: Surrounds
the protoplast or spore core 5) Spore core: Contains nucleoid and ribosomes.

Difference between death phase and stationary phase of bacterial growth.
1= The Stationary Phase: This is also called a period of equilibrium as microbial deaths equal
production of new cells. In other words, organisms start dying during this phase, however, the
number of dead organisms is replaced by new organisms because there is still replication of cells
going on. So, overall number of organisms does not change. This is the reason; it is called a
stationary phase.

2= The Death Phase: The number of deaths exceeds the number of new cells formed during this
phase. In other words, overall number decreases. It is also called logarithmic decline phase. Why
there is a decline phase or death phase. The reason is simple: Nutrients are depleted and waste
products which are toxic to the cells accumulate suppressing the growth and killing the cells.

Define Pasteurization:
Pasteurization is a heat treatment process that aims to reduce microbial contamination in food
