10.Properties and uses of the Inter-Quartile Range

      •   The IQR is generally used in conjunction with the median. Together, they are
          useful for characterizing the central location and spread of any frequency
          distribution, but particularly those that are skewed.
      •   For a more complete characterization of a frequency distribution, the 1st and 3rd
          quartiles are sometimes used with the minimum value, the median, and the
          maximum value to produce a five number summary of the distribution.

       •   Together these five numbers provide a good description of the center, spread and shape
           of the distribution.
