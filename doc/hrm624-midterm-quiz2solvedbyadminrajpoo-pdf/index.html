A disputant who is motivated to seek vengeance is likely to sacrifice the advantages of cooperation to
punish the other side. This phenomenon often occurs after a conflict has been in a competitive cycle and
has escalated and spread. Disputants have many reasons to seek revenge. e.g.

• Revenge to rectify injustice
• Revenge to prove self worth
• For preventing other disputant from further havoc
• Perception of hostility and hatred
• Vengeance as a difficult impediment to deal with conflict
