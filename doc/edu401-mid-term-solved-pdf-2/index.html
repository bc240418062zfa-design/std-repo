Q. Level of literacy
   Baseline literacy: The bare minimum to be considered literate is baseline
   literacy. The ability to read very simple and ability to write very simple, is
   included in it. Further includes, the ability to do simple Quantitative (arithmetic)
   analysis. It may also include basic computer skills.
   Functional literacy: The functional literacy means that you become functional
   within the society. In other words, a person is not dependent on reading the letter
   or writing something. It is an advanced level.
   Multiple literacy: Further advanced level of literacy is multiple literacy. When
   one can become able to distinguish between different words of similar meanings.
   As there is a very minute difference in the synonyms of the words, if a person
   can distinguish those words then it is multiple literacy.
Q. Equity issues in education
Equity in education means ensuring that all students have equal access to quality
education, regardless of their background, abilities, or socio-economic status.
Challenges include disparities in educational resources, teacher quality, and
opportunities. Addressing equity involves targeted interventions, inclusive policies,
and removing barriers.

Q. Gender as an equity issue
Gender equality is a fundamental human right. It means equal opportunities and
decision-making power for women and men. Despite progress, challenges persist.
Women face economic disparities, limited access to education, health risks, and
underrepresentation in leadership roles. To achieve gender equality, we must
address issues like lack of women in leadership, poverty, and unequal access to
resources.
Q. Strategies for developing accessible curricula
      Providing a flexible timeframe for students studying particular subjects
      Giving greater freedom to teachers in choosing their working methods
      Allowing teachers the opportunity of giving special support in practical
      subjects (e.g. orientation, mobility) over and above the periods allotted for
      more traditional school subjects.
      Allotting time for additional assistance for classroom based work
      Emphasizing aspects of pre-vocational training.

Pakistan, madrasas have faced scrutiny due to concerns about radicalization and
lack of modern education. The madrasa system was introduced in Pakistan during
British colonial rule and has evolved over time.
