What Are Helicoplacoids?
Helicoplacus is the earliest well-studied fossil echinoderm. Fossil plates are known from several
regions. Complete specimens were found in Lower Cambrian strata of the White Mountains of
California. The animal was a cigar-shaped creature up to 7 centimetres long that stood upright on
one end.

Define Cambrian explosion:
The Cambrian explosion generated a range of new body plans during a relatively short time
interval. It is refers to a period of rapid diversification and evolution of complex life forms that
occurred approximately 541 million years ago during the Cambrian Period. It marked the
appearance of diverse animal phyla and the development of complex ecosystems.

What was the brain capacity of Homo habilis?
The first species, H. habilis, lived in Africa from 2.4 to 1.5 Ma, and had a brain capacity of 630–
700 cm3 in a body only 1.3 m tall. H. habilis may have used tools.

Note on biodiversification of GOBE:
The Great Ordovician Biodiversification Event (GOBE) occurred during the Ordovician Period,
approximately 485 to 443 million years ago. It was a period of significant biodiversification,
marked by the emergence of numerous new species and the development of unique adaptations
and ecological strategies. Changes in ocean chemistry, fluctuations in sea levels, and the
formation of new habitats likely contributed to this diversification. The GOBE had long-lasting
impacts on Earth's ecosystems, shaping complex food webs and influencing the subsequent
evolutionary trajectory of life on our planet.

Define Latitudinal gradient:
The latitudinal gradient of biodiversity results in uneven distribution, with the tropics having
more diverse species due to high evapotranspiration and productivity, influenced by equatorial
climate conditions.

What is the gondwana:
In the middle of the Palaeozoic, 400 mya, a large landmass that we call Euramerica, lay across
the equator. To its south lay a great continent known as Gondwana. comprising present-day
continents like South America, Africa, Antarctica, Australia, and the Indian subcontinent.

Observation 3 Late phases of diversification cycles are associated with declining rates of

Oldest clear evidence for bipedalism:
The oldest clear evidence for bipedalism: human tracks in volcanic ash from Tanzania, 3.75 Ma.
The oldest substantial skeletons, of Praeanthropus afarensis, 3.2 Ma.

The work of Albert Gunther, and Arnold Ortmann in Biogeography:
1= In 1880, the British zoologist Albert Gunther published a book on fishes in which he
recognized 10 different regions in the distribution of shore fishes. 2= The German Arnold
Ortmann published a similar work based on the distribution of crustaceans such as crabs and
lobsters.

Origin of sheep:
Sheep (Ovis aries) are domesticated descendants of wild mouflon. Originating from ancient wild
sheep in Eurasia, they were selectively bred for various traits by humans for wool, meat, and
companionship.

Defines trace fossils:
Trace fossils, also known as ichnofossils, are indirect evidence of past organismal activity
preserved in sedimentary rocks. They provide valuable information about the behavior,
movement, and interactions of ancient organisms. Trace fossils are distinct from body fossils,
which are the preserved remains of the actual organisms.

Signor-Lipps Effect:
The Signor–Lipps effect is a paleontological principle proposed by Philip W. Signor and Jere H.
Lipps which states that, since the fossil record of organisms is never complete, neither the first
nor the last organism in a given taxon will be recorded as a fossil.

Work of Philip W. Signor and Jere H. Lipps.
Philip W. Signor and Jere H. Lipps studied fossil diversity patterns. They proposed the "Pull of
the Recent" idea, suggesting that recent species influence diversity more than older fossils in
analyses.

How plant cope with environmental stress.
Plants adapt to stress by changing growth patterns, conserving water, and producing protective
compounds like antioxidants, flavonoids, and secondary metabolites. They adjust their
physiology to survive in harsh conditions, ensuring better chances of survival.

Define Klepto-parasitism:
Klepto-parasitism is when one animal steals food that another animal worked to find or catch. It's
like food theft in the animal world. For example: The coots are messy eaters, and it is not
difficult for gadwall to move in and collect some of the loot. This behavior is called
kleptoparasitism and it is an effective way of widening the niche of the gadwall.

Causes of Glaciation:
Glaciation results from long-term climate cooling, altering Earth's energy balance. Factors
include variations in Earth's orbit, solar output, atmospheric composition, and ocean circulation,
inducing ice sheet growth.

Metabolic Theory:

In an environment where energy is abundant and temperature is consistently high, such as the
tropics, metabolic rates in organisms tend to be faster. Fecundity is greater, and generation times
may be shorter.
