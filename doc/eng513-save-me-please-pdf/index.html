Defining Language Learning
   o The process of acquiring a new language
   o Involves cognitive, social, and cultural factors
Some Theoretical Concepts: Language as a System
   Language as a system: A set of rules and structures
   Focus on grammar, vocabulary, and phonology
   Influenced by structuralism and linguistics
Some Theoretical Concepts: Language as Discourse
  Language as discourse: Language in use and communication
  Focus on meaning, context, and social interaction
  Influenced by sociolinguistics and pragmatics
Some Theoretical Concepts: Language as an Ideology
 Language as an ideology: Language and power relations
 Focus on social and cultural contexts, identity, and inequality
 Influenced by critical discourse analysis and sociolinguistics
Language Teaching and Theoretical Concepts about Language
 - Language teaching methods influenced by theoretical concepts
 - Understanding language as a system, discourse, and ideology informs teaching
    practices
Significance of a ‘Language Teaching Methods’ Course
               Provides a foundation in language teaching methodologies
               Helps teachers understand the theoretical basis of methods
               Enables teachers to critically evaluate and adapt methods
Language Teaching Methods in Ancient Times
 - Early methods: Grammar-Translation, Classical Method
 - Focus on reading, writing, and translation
Language Teaching Methods in Europe in Early Modern Times
      Renaissance and Enlightenment: Humanist and Rationalist approaches
      Focus on classical languages and literature
Language Teaching Methods in the 19th and Early to Mid-20th Century
               Reform Movement: Focus on spoken language and communication

                 Methods: Direct Method, Audio-Lingual Method
Language Teaching Methods from the Mid- to Late-20th Century
    Communicative Language Teaching (CLT): Focus on communication and
        authenticity
    Methods: Task-Based Learning, Content and Language Integrated Learning (CLIL)
What is a Theory? A framework for understanding and explaining phenomena
What is an Approach? A general philosophy or perspective on language teaching
What is a Method/ Methodology?
                 A method: A specific set of techniques and procedures
                 Methodology: The study of methods and their underlying theories
Types of Methods: Grammar-Translation, Direct, Audio-Lingual, Communicative, Task-
Based, CLIL
Some other terms: System, Procedure, Technique
                 System: A set of rules and structures
                 Procedure: A step-by-step process
                 Technique: A specific strategy or activity

What is Grammar Translation Method?
   o A traditional method focusing on grammar rules and translation
   o Strengths: Develops reading and writing skills, useful for language analysis
   o Weaknesses: Neglects speaking and listening skills, too focused on grammar
   o Opportunities: Useful for beginners, develops language awareness
   o Threats: Overemphasis on grammar, neglects communication skills
   o In Pakistan: Widely used, but needs to be supplemented with modern methods
What is the Direct Method?
      o A method focusing on spoken language and communication
      o Strengths: Develops speaking and listening skills, promotes authentic language
          use
      o Weaknesses: Difficult to implement in large classes, requires trained teachers
      o Opportunities: Encourages active learning, develops communication skills
      o Threats: Challenging to implement, needs resources and support
      o In Pakistani Context: Useful for developing communication skills, but needs
          adaptation to local context
      o Sample Activities: Role-plays, group discussions, audio-visual aids
Audio-lingual Method
   o A method focusing on listening and speaking skills through audio-visual aids
   o Background: Developed in the 1950s and 60s, influenced by behavioral
      psychology
   o Strengths: Develops listening and speaking skills, uses authentic materials
   o Weaknesses: Overemphasis on listening and speaking, neglects other skills
   o Opportunities: Useful for developing communication skills, uses modern
      technology
   o Threats: Needs resources and support, challenging to implement in large classes
