Diagnostic assessment is the type of the assessment that determines the causes intellectual,
emotional, and environmental of persistent learning difficulties.

There are six level’s of the cognitive domain: 1. Knowledge 2. Comprehension 3. Application 4.
Analysis 5. Synthesis 6. Evaluation

     10. what are subjective type questions? Tell names

Subjective test items (essay questions) are constructed response type questions that can be the
best way to measure the students' higher order thinking skills, such as applying, organizing,
synthesizing, integrating, evaluating, or projecting while at the same time providing a measure
of writing skills. Essay items can vary from very lengthy (5-10 pages), open-ended to limited or
restricted response (one page or less). Therefore, essay type questions are divided into two
types

   i. Restricted Response Items
  ii. Extended Response Items
     11. High stake testing matters

High-stakes testing as the use of a summative test or an assessment (i.e., a test designed to
measure student achievement after a period of instruction has been completed) to make
decisions that are of prominent educational, financial, or social impact.

   i. Types of High stake test
  ii. Criterian Referenced
 iii.     Test Norm-Referenced High-Stake Tests
      12. Advantage if essay type questions

Extended response questions have following advantages over other types of question formats:
1. Effective for assessing higher order abilities: analyses synthesize and evaluate. 2. It is
comparatively less time consuming to develop such items. 3. Emphasizes essential
communicational skills. 4. Guessing is eliminated.

     13. Benefits of rubric in evaluation process

A rubric is an explicit set of criteria used for assessing a particular type of work or performance
and provides more details than a single grade or mark. It is a set of scoring guidelines for
evaluating student work.

   14. Item analysis

The item analysis is an important phase in the development of a test. In this phase statistical
methods are used to identify any test items that are not working well. If an item is too easy, too
difficult, failing to show a difference between skilled and unskilled examinees, or even scored
incorrectly, an item analysis will reveal it.

   15. Extended response type questions with example

It allows the students to determine the length and complexity of the response. This type of
questions is most suitable for measuring higher level mental processes skills like synthesis and
evaluation. Teachers prefer these questions when they want to have information about
student’s ability to organize, integrate, express and evaluate information.

Example: What are the teachings of Islam about the respect of elders? How do these teachings
help us to make a good society? Marks will be given for correct information, your own point of
view, good judgment and good presentation.

   16. What is report? Name of reports?

Reporting is the process of communicating the knowledge and understanding gained from
assessing a student’s work to improve learning There are two forms of reports 1. Formal
reports 2. Informal reports

   17. Explain formal and informal report

Formal reports

Formal reports communicate about important aspects of the students’ progress in the areas of
academic, social, human and career development to parents and students.

Informal reports

Informal report tells parents about what their child is able to do. It provides information about
the areas of learning that need further attention or development and the ways the teacher is
supporting the student’s learning needs.
