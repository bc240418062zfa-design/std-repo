Secondary Structures of RNA
A simple nucleotide sequences of RNA is called as Primary structure and
denoted by 1’ while when these nucleotides fold together and form a
complex structure that is called secondary structure and denoted by 2’.
The preferred structure of RNA is 2’ which has many structural patterns
like Helices, Loops, Bulges and Junctions

Secondary Structures of Proteins -II
2’ structures or secondary protein structures are formed
as a result of H-Bond formation between N and C terminus
in a protein backbone. Types of 2’ structures include Alpha
helices and Beta sheets.

Need For Chou Fasman Algorithms?
Chou Fasman Algorithm helps to predict Alpha Helices, Beta Sheets and
Turns. The algorithm is based on statistical occurrence of Amino Acids in
known structures. The relative frequencies of each amino acid based on
known protein structures solved with X-ray crystallography. From these
frequencies a set of probability parameter were derived.

Difference B/T Uni_Prot & Swiss_Prot
Both UniProt and SwissProt are the online database for proteins.

Protein sequences from various species and organisms can be found in
