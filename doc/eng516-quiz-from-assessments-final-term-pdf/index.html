                          Quiz from Assessments (Final-Term)

1. Which of the following is a special meeting in which parties need each other’s agreement in
   order to achieve an effective result? (Negotiation)
2. Is it correct that ideally a role-play activity should give background information? (yes)
3. Is the following statement correct or incorrect? Negotiators are neither wholly distributive nor
   integrative. (correct)
4. Choose the correct option. Preparation of negotiation meeting includes ___________.
   (Assessing power)
5. Choose the correct option. Getting to know the other party in negotiation meeting is known as
   ___________. (Relationship building)
6. Is the following statement true or false? Identifying the key language for use in negotiations
   can be quite difficult? (true)
7. Is it true that the genre approach is an alternative approach to the teaching of writing? (yes)
8. Which of the following is the first step in the process approach to teach writing? (Prewriting)
9. Which of the following is about how sentences connect with each other to form a sense of
   unity? (Cohesion)
10.Is it true that the formatting allows the reader ease of access to the text? (yes)
11.In which of the following approaches learners are given the chance to study model texts and
   to base their own writing on what they have noticed in the texts? (Genre)
12.Is the following statement true or false? Determining the communication channel is an
   important feature of writing. (true)
13.Which of the following approaches to writing is a dynamic process? (Process)
14.Is the following statement correct or incorrect? Your choice of vocabulary, style and tone does
   not depend on who is going to read the report. (Incorrect)
15.Is the following statement correct or incorrect? Writing happens in a context; therefore, it has
   to relate to the setting. (Incorrect)
16.Is the following statement correct or incorrect? A Paired Crossword Approach combines the
   different threads of the course design. (correct)
17.Is the following statement correct or incorrect? Activities and practice of writing through
   controlled activities aim to develop competence in techniques, formats and language forms.
   (correct)
18.Letters are basically used for internal communication. (false)
19.One of the reasons for writing a letter is that Letters allow a better “paper trail” than emails
   and for a better documentation record. (true)
20.A letter should be ______ to have the desired effect on the recipient? (grammatically correct,
   All of the given, to the point and relevant, in the proper format)
21.At which place is a salutation used in a letter? (Opening)
22.Writing a letter provides more significance for correspondence. (correct)
23.The length of a letter can range from one page of text to more than three pages. (true)
24.A complimentary copy notation(cc) is used if a copy is being sent to other readers. (correct)
