 EVALUATIONS OF UNIVERSAL GRAMMAR
       AND COGNITIVE APPROACHES
Topic No: 069-074 - Evaluations of Universal Grammar and Cognitive
Approaches

Evaluation of Universal Grammar (UG):

What is the Universal Grammar (UG) view of language?

UG views language as a mental framework underlying all human languages.

What is the focus of UG in language study?

UG is concerned with the sentence and its internal structure rather than larger units
of language.

How does UG approach language clinically?

UG studies languages as a mental object, in a vacuum, without considering social
or psychological aspects.

What criticism does UG face regarding methodology?

 COMPILED BY SIR ZAHID                                                              3

UG's methodologies are criticized for not being representative of reality and lacking
reliability in SL judgments.

What is the primary focus of UG theorists?

UG theorists focus on linguistic competence, not concerned with naturalistic
performance.

How has recent work in UG addressed its criticism?

Recent work in UG has used a range of elicitation techniques to enhance reliability.

What does UG approach exclusively concern itself with?

UG exclusively concerns itself with documenting and explaining the nature of SL
linguistic systems, ignoring social and psychological variables.

In what ways has UG been useful for linguistic analysis?

UG has facilitated well-defined and focused hypotheses and has been useful in
explaining facts about SLA.

Cognitive Approaches to Second Language Learning (SLL):

What does the Cognitive Approach to SLL emphasize?

Cognitive approaches emphasize the learning component of SLL and focus on
understanding how the human brain processes and learns new information.

How do Cognitive Approaches differ from UG in terms of focus?

Cognitive Approaches focus more on the learner as an individual and how they
access linguistic knowledge in real-time.

What were UG theorists primarily interested in?

UG theorists were primarily interested in competence and not concerned with real-
time linguistic knowledge access or learner strategies.

Two Approaches of Cognitive Theorists of SLA:

What is the primary focus of Processing Theorists?

 COMPILED BY SIR ZAHID                                                            4

Processing Theorists investigate how second language learners process linguistic
information and how this ability develops over time.

How do Emergentist/Constructionist           theorists   view   knowledge     and
processing?

Emergentist/Constructionist theorists believe in explaining the nature of knowledge
and its processing through general cognitive principles.

What do Constructivists claim about language development?

Constructivists claim that language complexity emerges from associative learning
processes exposed to a massive and complex environment.

How do many Constructivists believe language develops?

Many Constructivists believe that language develops as learners move from
learning exemplars committed to memory to making more abstract generalizations.
