Question #1: Keep in view the language of all strings ending with ‘a’ defined over Σ = {a,
b, c, d}. For which input letter will we take a loop on the final state of its transition
diagram?

Options:
a. a
b. b
c. c
d. d

Correct Answer: a. a

Question #2: If S = {a, b}, then which of the following Regular Expressions (RE) will
generate all possible strings?

Options:
a. (a + b)*
b. (ab)*
c. (ab + ba)*
d. a* + b*

Correct Answer: a. (a + b)*

Question #3: The formal language is also known as _________.

Options:
a. Syntactic language
b. Semantic language
c. Informal language
d. Normal language

Correct Answer: a. Syntactic language

Question #4: Which one of the following is a correct word produced by the Regular
Expression ab?

Options:
a. bbbb
b. aaaa
c. Null
d. abab

Correct Answer: b. aaaa

Question #5: Consider the Regular Expression (RE) a (a + b)b*. Which one of the
following is NOT accepted by the provided RE?

Options:
a. aba
b. aa
c. abb
d. aab

Correct Answer: c. abb

Question #6: The recursive method for defining a language has ______________ steps.

Options:
a. three
b. four
c. two
d. one

Correct Answer: a. three

Question #7: Which of the following does not contribute while finding out the length of
strings?

Options:
a. a
b. a+
c. b
d. b^

Correct Answer: d. b^

Question #8: The language of all strings defined over the alphabet set {x, y} having triple
x’s or triple y’s will have the minimum strings with a length of:

Options:
a. 1
b. 2
c. 4
d. 3

Correct Answer: a. 1

Question #9: Which one of the following words is not accepted by the regular
expression (a+b)(aa)(a+b)?

Options:
a. bab
b. aaaa
c. aa
d. baaa

Correct Answer: a. bab

Question #10: The Generalized Transition Graph (GTG) for the expression (aa+aba)* may
have a minimum number of states:

Options:
a. 1
b. 3
c. 4
d. 2

Correct Answer: c. 4

Question #1: In the context of creating an NFA for the concatenation of FA1 and FA2
(where FA2 accepts the null string), which of the following options is correct?

A) FA2 having a final state only

B) FA2 having an initial state only

C) Initial states in both FAs

D) Final states in both FAs

Answer: C) Initial states in both FAs

Question #2: In order to make an NFA for the union of FA1 and FA2, the new initial state
should be linked to:

A) Final and initial states of FA1 and FA2 respectively

B) Initial states of both FAs

C) Initial state of FA1 only

D) Initial and final states of FA1 and FA2 respectively

Answer: B) Initial states of both FAs

Question #2: In order to make an NFA for the union of FA1 and FA2, the new initial state
should be linked to:

A) Final and initial states of FA1 and FA2 respectively

B) Initial states of both FAs

C) Initial state of FA1 only

D) Initial and final states of FA1 and FA2 respectively

Answer: B) Initial states of both FAs

Question #3: Let FA3 be an FA corresponding to FA1FA2, then the final state of FA3
must correspond to the final state of:

A) FA1 only

B) FA1 and FA2

C) FA1 or FA2

D) FA2 only

Answer: B) FA1 and FA2

Question #4: FA and _______ are the same except that _______ has a unique symbol for
each transition.

A) NFA, FA

B) FA, TG

C) TG, NFA

D) NFA, TG

Answer: D) NFA, TG

Question #5: Kleene Theorem III states that if the language can be expressed by RE then
there exist -------- accepting the language.

A) FA

B) Both TG and GT

C) GT

D) TG

Answer: A) FA

Question #6: Consider we have languages L7 and L6. Which of the following represents
their concatenation?

A) L7 + L6

B) L7

C) L6

D) L7/L6

E) L6*L7

Answer: A) L7 + L6

Question #7: Considering FA1 and FA2 having 2 states each. Now FA1+FA2 can have a
maximum ______________ number of states.

   A) More than 3
   B) 2

B) None of the given options

C) 3

Answer: C) 3

Question #8: In proving Kleene Theorem II, circuits are reduced into:

A) Both difference and asterisk

B) Difference

C) Asterisk

D) Sum

Answer: D) Sum

Question #9: How many new states are introduced while developing NFA for the closure
of an FA?

A) One

B) Three

C) Four

D) Two

Answer: A) One

Question #10: NFA with the null string has ---------- initial state(s).

A) One

B) Many

C) Two

D) Three

Answer: A) One
