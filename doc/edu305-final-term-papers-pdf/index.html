Q: 1 explains intimidation techniques of psychological coercion in your own words.

Answer:

There are different intimidation techniques such as

   1.   Threats
   2.   Sarcasm
   3.   Ridicule
   4.   Psychological coercion
   5.   Physical coercion
   6.   Disapproval

Psychological coercion:

This is an oppressive strategy teacher used to control behavioral problems. They put a
psychological pressure on students by using emotional outbursts and mentally distress students to
comply with authority.

Q: 2 How does development of self-efficacy and empathy enhance student success in
classroom?

Answer:

Empathy: - having concern about student’s personal goals and interests.

Teacher having an empathetic attitude with student are more successful in conveying their
instructions. It builds a harmony among student and teachers. Teacher having empathetic
behavior try to understand and solve students problem. A mind works best in a supportive
environment.

Solved by Sahar

Self-eficacy:-

It is facilitated by one’s beliefs and expectations of himself /herself to complete certain task
successfully or not.

Self-efficacy certainly effect learning i.e. a student having low expectation of himself will not
perform well.

 Student’s negative experiences and other negative feedbacks let him feel low of himself and he
lose his confidence. Student expect very low of himself and remained unable to achieve better
results.

While on the other hand there are certain factors that can increase self –efficacy and helps a
student to perform better.

Performance Accomplishments, vicarious learning, verbal persuasion , physical / affective status.

Q: 6 identify some of the behaviors of students that need counseling services in schools?

Answer:

Students who insult, tease, harass, mock, abuse, lie, steal, aggressive, lack confidence and
victims of bully.

Q: 7 explain overlapping as a classroom management strategy.

Answer:

 Overlapping means responding to multiple students at the same time. It gives a view to students
that you can do multi-tasking at the same time i.e. responding to one student’s question while
checking others home work.

What strategies should be implemented in schools to control violence among students?

Answer:

   1. Prepare for a crisis
   2. Train staff to respond against student aggression
   3. Build rapport and trust
   4. Dress properly
   5. Keep items of value out of reach
   6. Keep calm and in control
   7. Tell students about the expected behavior and consequence for compliance and
      noncompliance.
   8. Adopt a therapeutic attitude.
