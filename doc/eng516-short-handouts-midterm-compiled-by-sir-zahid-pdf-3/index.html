Lesson 01
           ESSENTIALS OF BUSINESS ENGLISH AND
                     BUSINESS COMMUNICATION
Topic - 001: What is Business Communication?
What is the definition of Business Communication?
Business Communication is the communication that occurs in the workplace,
corporate offices, or educational settings and is primarily conducted in the
English language.
Why is effective business communication important in the modern workplace?

 COMPILED BY SIR ZAHID                                                         1

Effective business communication is crucial for success in the modern
workplace, as it involves both internal and external communication, often on a
global scale, for purposes such as selling, persuading, negotiating, informing,
and presenting.
How has the teaching of business English evolved in recent decades?
The teaching of business English has gained significance over the last few
decades, with a growing number of undergraduates from various fields needing
to take courses in business English communication.
What distinguishes business English from general English, and why is some
knowledge of general English necessary for learning business English?
Business English builds upon general English and focuses on business-specific
situations, emphasizing clarity, logic, and presentation styles. A basic knowledge
of general English is considered a prerequisite for learning business English.
Topic - 002: Origin of Business Communication
What are the key factors that contributed to the growth of business English
teaching?
The growth of business English teaching was influenced by the importance of
commerce and technology, the widespread use of English as an international
language, and the oil crisis of the 1970s, which led to a need for cost-effective
English language courses.
How did changes in linguistics impact the development of ESP and business
English?
Linguistics shifted from describing the formal aspects of language to focusing on
how language is used in real-life communication. This change emphasized the
differences between written and spoken language and the language needs of
specific groups.
How did developments in educational psychology influence the rise of ESP and
business English?

 COMPILED BY SIR ZAHID                                                               2

Educational psychology emphasized the importance of considering learners'
needs, interests, and motivation in language learning. This approach led to the
development of courses that catered to learners' specific requirements.
Topic - 003: Scope of Business Communication
What are the main components of a Business English course?
A Business English course typically consists of language knowledge (grammar,
vocabulary, and sound system) and communication skills (oral and written
communication in business contexts).
How does business English differ from general English in terms of context and
content?
Business English builds upon general English, focusing on business situations
that require clarity, logic, and specific presentation styles. It also includes
professional content, management skills, and cultural awareness.
What is the legitimate scope of business English teachers?
Business English teachers are responsible for designing and delivering courses
that aim to enhance language knowledge, communication skills, management
skills, cultural awareness, and the understanding of professional contexts.
Topic - 004: Development of Business Communication
What are the five main phases in the development of English for Specific
Purposes (ESP)?
The five main phases in the development of ESP are Register Analysis, Rhetorical
or Discourse Analysis, Target Situation Analysis/Needs Analysis, Skills and
Strategies, and Learner-centered Approaches.
How did these phases contribute to the development of ESP, including
business communication?
These phases contributed to ESP by shifting the focus from formal language
aspects to discourse, identifying organizational patterns in texts, analyzing
learner needs, exploring thinking processes underlying language use, and
emphasizing learner-centered approaches.

Topic - 005: Important Terms in Business Communication and ESP
What is the ESP tree, and how does it categorize various branches of English
language teaching?
The ESP tree categorizes various branches of English language teaching, such as
English for Science and Technology, English for Business and Economics, and
English for Social Sciences, under the umbrella of English for Specific Purposes
(ESP).
What are the distinctions between English for Academic Purposes (EAP) and
English for Vocational Purposes (EVP)?
EAP focuses on academic language needs, while EVP centers on language
requirements for vocational purposes, which include work-related and
professional contexts.
What are some specific terms related to business communication, such as
ESBP and EGBP?
ESBP stands for English for Specific Business Purpose, while EGBP stands for
English for General Business Purposes, targeting learners with limited working
experience.
Topic - 006: Approach not Product
What is Business Communication teaching considered, and why?
Business Communication teaching is considered an approach rather than a
product, as it emphasizes the learner's specific needs and is not a distinct form
of language.
What distinguishes Business Communication teaching from other forms of
language teaching?
Business Communication teaching is not different in kind from other language
teaching; it uses common language learning processes and methodologies. It is
distinguished by its focus on the learner's specific reasons for learning a foreign
language.
