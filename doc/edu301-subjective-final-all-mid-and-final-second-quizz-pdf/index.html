Describe attention time for small children

Childhood development experts generally say that a reasonable attention

span to expect of a child is two to three minutes per year of their age.

That's the period of time for which a typical child can maintain focus on

a given task. Average attention spans work out like this: 2 years old:

four to six minutes. Small children write words in joining writing very

slowly, because there is no complex response. To take a child to

complex response, we need to provide them with practicing. If we do not

provide them with practice they can never reach the complex response.

As a result, children may keep growing in age but they are unable to

write quickly.
