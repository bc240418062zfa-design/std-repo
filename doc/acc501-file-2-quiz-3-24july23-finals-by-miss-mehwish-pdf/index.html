1. What will be the weighted average cost of capital if a firm's capital structure has 55% debt
and 45% common equity; while, after-tax cost for debt is 8% and the cost of common equity is
15%?
 a) 11.15%
 b) 9.91%
 c) 10.54%
 d) 10.40%

 Correct Option: a) 11.15%

2. Business risk depends on which of the following risk of the firm’s assets?
 a) Diversifiable Risk
 b) None of the given options

 c) Unsystematic Risk
 d) Systematic Risk
 Correct Option: d) Systematic Risk

3. Unsystematic Risk is also known as:

 a) Non-diversifiable Risk
 b) None of the given options
 c) Market Risk
 d) Diversifiable Risk

 Correct Option: a) Non-diversifiable Risk

4. Which of the following is referred to as the ratio of the standard deviation of a distribution to
the mean of that distribution?
 a) The expected return
 b) Coefficient of variation

 c) Probability distribution
 d) The standard deviation
 Correct Option: b) Coefficient of variation

5. Which of the following is the time period between the acquisition of inventory and the
collection of cash from receivables?
 a) Current Cycle

 b) Cash Cycle
 c) None of the given options
 d) Operating Cycle
 Correct Option: b) Cash Cycle

6. Which of the following is the difference between the current assets and the current
liabilities?
 a) Net difference
 b) Current ratio
 c) Net working capital
 d) Net available capital

 Correct Option: c) Net working capital

7. Which one of the following statements is INCORRECT while changing the firm's capital
structure?
 a) It causes changes in the company's debt
 b) It always changes the firm's total value

 c) It may not change the firm's total value
 d) It causes important changes in the company's equity
 Correct Option: b) It always changes the firm's total value

8. Which of the following is NOT an example of systematic risk?

 a) Inflation
 b) Interest Rate
 c) Gross Domestic Product
 d) Strike call in a company
 Correct Option: d) Strike call in a company

9. What would be the standard deviation of returns for an investment that has a variance of
0.0075?
 a) 0.0910
 b) 0.0948
 c) 0.1052
 d) 0.0866

 Correct Option: d) 0.0866

10. According to the 2nd M&M proposition, the cost of equity does NOT depend upon which of
the following?
  a) The required return of the firm's assets
  b) The firm's cost of debt
  c) The firm's stockholders

  d) The firm's debt-equity ratio
  Correct Option: b) The firm's cost of debt
