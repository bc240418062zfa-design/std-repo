Performance Characteristics of D/A
          Converters
 Resolution
    Number of bits that are converted

    Reciprocal of the number of discrete steps in the

     output expressed in percentage
    4-bit D/A has a resolution of 6.67% (1/15 x 100)

 Accuracy

    Actual o/p of a D/A w.r.t. expected o/p

    Expressed as a percentage of full-scale o/p

    O/p = 10 v accuracy is 0.1% then max error is 10mV

    Should not be +/-1/2 least sign. bit

          Converters
 Linearity
    Deviation form the straight line output of a D/A

 Monotonicity

    DAC does not take any reverse steps when D/A

     sequenced over its entire range of input steps
 Settling time

    The time it takes the D/A to settle within +/- ½

     LSB of its final value when a change occurs in its
     input
