Difference between pBR322 and pBR325:
pBR322 and pBR325 are plasmids used in genetic engineering. pBR322 is an in vitro
constructed cloning vehicle with ApR and TcR genes from RSF2124 and pSC101, and a
replication element from pMB1. It has 40 restriction enzyme target sites but is less suitable for
long DNA segments. In contrast, pBR325, a derivative of pBR322, also encodes
chloramphenicol resistance along with ampicillin and tetracycline, offering expanded
applications and improved vectors like pUC19 and pAT153. These plasmids have been crucial in
DNA cloning and genetic engineering experiments, contributing to advancements in molecular
biology research.

What is the cosmid vector:
A cosmid vector is a type of DNA carrier that includes a specific segment from a bacteriophage
called lambda (ʎ). Including “Cos” site. These vectors, called cosmids, are useful for cloning
large sections of foreign DNA. They work well with an in vitro packaging system to insert DNA
fragments. Cosmids are especially useful for creating libraries of genetic material from
eukaryotic organisms.

Write a note on RT-PCR:
RT-PCR, or Reverse Transcription Polymerase Chain Reaction, is a technique used to amplify
and detect RNA molecules. It involves converting RNA into DNA using reverse transcriptase
and then amplifying the DNA using PCR. RT-PCR is widely used in research, diagnostics, and
forensic analysis for studying gene expression, detecting viruses, and genetic testing.

Gauecher disease:
Gaucher disease is a rare genetic disorder where glucocerebroside accumulates in cells due to a
deficiency of the enzyme glucocerebrosidase. This buildup leads to organ and tissue damage.
Symptoms include enlarged liver and spleen, anemia, and bone abnormalities. Enzyme
replacement therapy is a common treatment.

Chemical composition of DNA:/ Note on DNA

DNA, or deoxyribonucleic acid, serves as the genetic blueprint for living organisms. Its primary
function is to store, transmit, and express genetic information. DNA encodes the instructions for
building and maintaining an organism's structure, controlling its growth, development, and
functioning. DNA, or deoxyribonucleic acid, is composed of nucleotide units. Each nucleotide
consists of a phosphate group, a deoxyribose sugar molecule, and one of four nitrogenous bases:
adenine (A), thymine (T), cytosine (C), or guanine (G). The sequence of these bases forms the
genetic code. Adenine pairs with thymine, and cytosine pairs with guanine through hydrogen
bonds, creating the double-stranded helical structure of DNA. This unique composition allows
DNA to store and transmit genetic information, playing a fundamental role in heredity and
cellular processes.

Whats happen during Chromosome jumping:
During chromosome jumping, the targeted DNA is first identified, cut into fragments using
restriction enzymes, and then circularized. This process facilitates the proximity of DNA
sequences that were originally distant in the genome. The resulting circularized DNA fragments,
originating from the closure sites, constitute a jumping library. Chromosome jumping is applied
in molecular biology techniques like the cloning of genes, exemplified by the cloning of the
human cystic fibrosis gene, utilizing the ability to bring together disparate DNA sequences for
comprehensive genomic analysis.

How can PCR help in improving quality of environment?
   1.   PCR detects environmental pollutants like heavy metals and pathogens.
   2.   It monitors microbial diversity in various habitats.
   3.   PCR studies genes involved in bioremediation for effective pollutant removal.
   4.   Environmental DNA (eDNA) analysis identifies species presence and biodiversity.
   5.   PCR tracks changes in gene expression due to environmental stressors.
   6.   Overall, PCR aids in environmental monitoring, assessment, and management, ensuring
        ecosystem preservation and human health protection

Genome mapping and its type?
Assigning of a specific gene to particular region of a chromosome and determining the location
of and relative distances between genes on the chromosome. There are two type here:1= Genetic
Linkage Mapping: Illustrates the order and relative distances between genes or genetic markers
on chromosomes. 2= Physical Mapping: Provides actual distances between landmarks on a
chromosome, typically measured in base pairs.

Significant of PCR in medical field / Role of PCR in Medical:
PCR has major impact on medicine especially in the field of clinical microbiology or diagnosis.
Molecular tools have also allowed to perform prenatal genetic diagnosis. PCR enables the
detection of genetic disorders such as Down syndrome, cystic fibrosis, and sickle cell anemia
during pregnancy.

What is biopaninng?
The primary screening method involves enriching phage clones with binding affinity for the
target through a process called biopanning. Biopanning is a process used to find specific
molecules, like antibodies, by enriching them from a mixture.

DNA sequence databases:
Large amounts of sequence data accumulated since the development of current DNA sequencing
technology are maintained in three databases: i) National Center for Biotechnology Information
ii) DNA Databank of Japan iii) European Bioinformatics Institute-UK
