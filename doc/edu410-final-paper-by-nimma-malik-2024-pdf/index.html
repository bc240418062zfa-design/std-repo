01:
        Explain principles of listening.
Pay Attention:
                Focus on the speaker. Look at him or her because facial expressions and body language
can communicate as much as language.
For example, there will usually be more engagement with the listener in making eye contact and use of hand
gestures.

Practice Active Listening:
                           Ask the speaker to slow down or repeat when you don’t understand or just want
to be certain about what you heard.
Repeat back what you think you heard him or her say, as there can often be a gap between what we thought
we heard and what the speaker intended.

Pay Attention to Structure:
                                In a formal lecture or speech, the speaker will usually let you know ahead of
time the organization of the discourse: “Today we will discuss the two types of sentences, Type One and
Type Two,’’
and then what will follow is a description of Types One and Two. These devices, called “discourse markers,”
actually help the listener in organizing and understanding the lecture.

03:
What is Listening Strategies?
 Listening strategies are techniques or activities that contribute directly to the comprehension and recall of
listening input. Listening strategies can be classified by how the listener processes the input.
       Top-down strategies are listener based.

04:
Define Tense.
Tense is a form of the verb that indicates time. A verb tense not only indicates past, present, and future
action but also indicates whether the action is ongoing or complete.
In English the tense also may provide emphasis and may determine whether or not an action or condition
was continuous or recitative. There are three basic tenses: past, present, and future.

05:
What is Cooperative Learning?
 Cooperative Learning is an approach to teaching that makes maximum use of cooperative activities involving pairs
and small groups of learners in the classroom.
Cooperative Language Learning (CLL) is also known as Collaborative Learning (CL).

06:
How can you say that audio lingual Methods similar to the direct
Methods?
Audio Lingual Method/ALM:
                                  The Audio-Lingual method of teaching a foreign language is also known as
the army method. It originated from the behaviorist theory of learning languages and was introduced to teach
language to troops during World War 2.

Similar to the direct method:
➤ They both are oral-based approaches - pronunciation and ability of speaking in the target language is
superior to other skills

➤ Grammar is learnt inductively

■ Different from the direct method:
➤ Not only vocabulary but also grammatical sentence patterns

➤ Stronger theoretical base in linguistics (structural linguistics) and psychology (behavioral psychology)

07:
What Is Comprehensive listening?
Listening to understand the message the speaker is communicating called Comprehensive Listening.
It involves not only hearing the words being spoken but also comprehending their meaning, context, and
nuances.

How can spelling can be taught at higher stage?
Teaching spellings at higher stage:
 Teaching spellings to beginners in early classes demands some easy strategies, but to elder students like
7th and 8th graders can be conducted with higher level activities.
When you are teaching something it is very important to involve student’s cognition for a long term learning.
There are certain activities which can be used for higher learners.
       Using missing spelling chart
       Shuffling the spellings
       Remove extra letter

10:
How can you teach principles of speaking to your student?

Principles for Teaching Speaking Skills:
1. Focus on both fluency and accuracy
2. Provide intrinsically motivating techniques
3. Encourage the use of authentic language
4. Provide appropriate feedback and correction
5. Capitalize on the natural link between speaking and listening
6. Give students opportunities to initiate oral communication
7. Encourage the development of speaking strategies.

What is present perfect tense?
Present perfect tense:
                          present perfect tense means that some activity has been started in past and
completed in present.
Example:
He has written a story.

12:
What is GTM?
Grammar Translation Method/GTM:
                                        The grammar translation method is a method of teaching foreign
languages derived from the classical (sometimes called traditional) method of teaching Greek and Latin.
We can use GTM for translations, for word lists, grammar etc.

13:
What are Idioms and collocation?
Idiom:
       a group of words established by usage as having a meaning not deducible from those of the individual
words (e.g. hang one’s head; see the light).
Examples:

            Cats and Dogs
            A piece of cake
Collocation:
            a word or phrase that is often used with another word or phrase, in a way that sounds correct
to people who use it (day and night, come to the point)
Examples:
            Heavy rain
"There was heavy rain all night, causing flooding in many areas."
            Fast food
"Let's grab some fast food for lunch."

What are levels of language? And Explain it.
There are 5 levels of language:
        1)     Phonology
        2)     Morphology
        3)     Syntax
        4)     Semantics
        5)     Pragmatics
1: Phonology:
                 Phonology is the study of how sounds are organized and used in natural languages. It is a
branch of linguistics concerned with the systematic organization of sounds in languages.
