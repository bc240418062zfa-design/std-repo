Question No: 37 ( Marks: 5 )
Is it possible to have a simple graph with four vertices of degree 1, 1, 3, and 3.If no then give reason?(Justify your
answer)
Solution:

Yes, It is possible to make a graph with four vertices of degree 1, 1, 3, 3
Because 1+1+3+3=8
And According to handshaking theorem, the sum of the degrees of all the vertices of G equals twice the number
of edges of G.
2*4=8
So it is possible.
