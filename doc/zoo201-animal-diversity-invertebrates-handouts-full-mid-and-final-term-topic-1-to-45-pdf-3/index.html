Topic- 001: Patterns of organization:

Asymmetry: Absence of a central point or axis around which body parts are equally distributed e.g
Protists and many sponges.

Symmetry: It describes how parts of an animal are arranged around a point or an axis.

Two types 1. Radial symmetry      2. Bilateral symmetry

Other patterns of Organization:

    1. Unicellular organization

    2. Diploblastic organization (a. two germ layered gastrula)
