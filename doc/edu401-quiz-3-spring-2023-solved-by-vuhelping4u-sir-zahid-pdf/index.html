1.What is given to the population education at the present stage due to the
difference in goals an objectives?
1.unity
2.entity
3.identity
4.unit
The correct answer is:
3. identity
At the present stage, population education is given a separate identity due to the
differences in goals and objectives from other educational activities like sex
education, family life education, environmental education, and development
education.

2.Population education is bound to reinforce which of the following for
innovation and renovations as the system is already using formal and learner
oriented approaches?
1.
problems
2.issues
3.
trends
4.

solutions
The correct answer is:
3. trends
Population education is bound to reinforce trends for innovation and renovations,
as the system is already using formal and learner-oriented approaches.

3.Privatization is also thought of as a ______________
1.secularism
2.pragmatism
3.Liberalization
4.idealism
The correct answer is:
3. Liberalization
Privatization is also thought of as liberalization, where agents are freed from
government regulations or restrictions.

4.If it is decided that a prescriptive approach is necessary the efficacy of
prescription is, in certain circumstances, _______________.
1.questionable
2.answerable
3.easy
4.difficult
The correct answer is:
Questionable
If it is decided that a prescriptive approach is necessary, the efficacy of
prescription is, in certain circumstances, questionable.

5.There are ___________ forms of Privatization
1.3
2.5
3.4
4.6
The correct answer is:
3
There are three forms of privatization, as mentioned in the provided paragraphs.

different ways of adding population education to the school curriculum?
1.Effectiveness
2.Subjectivity
3.Efficiency
4.Objectivity
The correct answer is:
Effectiveness
Methodological issues are concerned with the effectiveness of different ways of
adding population education to the school curriculum.

and national issues
1.channels
2.sports

3.curricula
4.news
The correct answer is:
3. curricula
Many of the earlier curricula for school programs emphasized world and national
issues.

2.education
3.Curricula
4.political system
The correct answer is:
3. Curricula
Educational institutions in all countries should be encouraged to expand their
curricula to include a study of population dynamics and policies.

problems

2. program developmenT

barriers for further progress in conceptual and methodological development of
population education?

practice
2.development
3.theory
4.research
The correct answer is:
4. research
The continued absence of research findings in certain areas is considered one of
the major barriers to further progress in the conceptual and methodological
development of population education.

11.In the “World Population Plan of Action”, ____________ rights are
recognized.
1.men’s
2.peoples
3.human

4.women’s
The correct answer is:
4. women's
In the "World Population Plan of Action," women's rights are recognized.

1.
re-structure
2.reconciliation
3.
recovery
4.renovation
The correct answer is:
4. renovation
An important goal often suggested for population education is to contribute to

1.
mortality
2.
funeral

The correct answer is:
3. family-planning
Most of the motivational activities aim to support family-planning activities,
encouraging individuals and families to plan and control the size of their families.

growth

Enhancement

Population education involves relations to sex education, family life education,

Before passing on to the question of integration, it seems necessary to indicate

3. population
