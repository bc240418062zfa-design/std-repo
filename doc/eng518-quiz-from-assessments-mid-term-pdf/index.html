1. Research improves learning, teaching and decision making in human life. (True)
2. Systematic inquiry means to follow a proper scientific method. (True)
3. Refinement and action of knowledge are the functions of research (True)
4. _______________ is irrelevant to research. (Imagination)
5. Conclusion is the direct _________ of the hypothesis. (Verification)
6. Anything related to language and society, learning, teaching, management, decision making
   and researchers can be a topic of concern in ELT research. (True)
7. Anything related to language, practical issues, problems related to second or foreign language,
   language policy are not involved in the paradigm of ELT research (False)
8. ___________ study the use of many different sets of materials to teach distinctively.
   (Researchers)
9. Research __________ can be identified through having an experience or the use of secondary
   sources. (Questions)
10.A true research must provide clear understanding of __________. (Meaning)
11.There are _________ levels of research. (two)
12.Types of Research based on data collection procedures contains: (Quantitative, Qualitative)
13.In educational research, the issues of influencing ___________ theories and practices are
   emphasized. (Educational)
14.Objectivity in ________ is one of the characteristics of an investigator. (Thinking)
15.__________ ways may solve your issues, by following a systematic process. (Scientific)
16.A problem is selected through __________ thinking towards a target issue; a careful ordered
   thinking is required. (Reflective)
17.To identify a problem, a researcher needs to know _________. (Exact nature and dimensions
   of the problem)
18.Before selecting specific research problem, it is crucial to follow a criterion. (True)
19.A researcher must not take any precautionary measures in defining research. (False)
20.A _______________ is the description of an issue currently existing which needs to be
   addressed. It provides the context for the research study and generates the questions which the
   research aims to answer. (Problem statement)
21.After the development of research problem, a tentative solution of a problem is devised which
   is known as: (Hypothesis)
22.The scientific research method is based on certain basic ___________. (Postulates)
23.Hypotheses vary in form and __________. (Function)
24.A question may or may not qualify as a _______. (hypothesis)
25.Temporary solution is one of the functions of a ____________ in research. (Hypothesis)
26.In agreement with the observed facts, a good hypothesis should contain clear roles for
   variables. (True)
27.A _________ is anything that has a quantity or quality that varies. (Variable)
