Question:
Globalization has created the new challenges as well as opportunities for human civilization
in the current era. Every social institution go through the transformation stage due to the
globalization. However, the most important aspect for sociologists to explore is the “Family
institution” which is greatly affected by globalization. Nowadays, traditional extended
families are breaking down into nuclear ones in Pakistan. In addition, family relations and
value structure is being deteriorated day by day. By analyzing the situation of family
institution in Pakistan, enlist any five negative impacts of globalization on Pakistani family
structure.

SOLUTION:
Globalization has had serious effects on Pakistan's economy, politics, society, law and religion.
Like many other developing countries, Pakistan is also experiencing both positive and negative
effects of globalization. Every society has its own culture and way of life. It is established by a
group of people who live together and follow certain rules in their society.
Cultural globalization is affecting men's and women's clothing.
Wearing t-shirts, glasses, tall shoes and jackets is not a part of Pakistani culture.
People are becoming more conscious about their dressing style and fashion.

Women have become more brand conscious.
There is an increasing trend among women to wear western clothes like jeans and shirts.

Five negative impact of family structure in Pakistan
   •   Unequal economic development
   •   Lack of local business
   •   Increases potential global recession
   •   Exploits cheap labor markets
   •   Causes job displacement
   •   Economic specialization
   •   Loss of biodiversity
