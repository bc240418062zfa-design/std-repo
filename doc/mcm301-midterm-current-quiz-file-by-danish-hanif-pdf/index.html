                          MCM301 Quiz File
Question #1: Each morning Amna decides how to dress, what to eat, where to go, etc.
This refers to which of the following levels of communication?

A) Interpersonal Communication

B) Intrapersonal Communication

C) Mediated Communication

D) Person-to-Group Communication

Correct Answer: B) Intrapersonal Communication

Question #2: Which of the following involves grasping what the speaker means by
seeing the ideas and information from his/her point of view?

A) Hearing

B) Listening

C) Speaking

D) Filtering

Correct Answer: B) Listening

Question #3: What do the clothes we wear communicate?

A) Affection

B) Rejection

C) Boredom

D) Status

Correct Answer: D) Status

Question #4: The word 'communication' is derived from a Latin word ‘communico’,
which means:

A) To tell

B) To give

C) To respond

D) To share

Correct Answer: D) To share

Question #5: All of the following are the levels of listening except:

A) Protective Listening

B) Critical Listening

C) Active Listening

D) Partial Listening

Correct Answer: A) Protective Listening

Question #6: The degree to which you and others share common meanings for words
will depend on:

A) The extent to which you share similar backgrounds and experiences

B) None of the given options

C) The diversity of your backgrounds

D) The extent of your biasness towards each other

Correct Answer: A) The extent to which you share similar backgrounds and
experiences

Question #7: Which of the following is not considered as a Public Relation function?

A) Corporate communications

B) Consumer service

C) Product publicity

D) Creative support

Correct Answer: B) Consumer service

Question #8: Probing is:

A) Making a judgment about the worth of the sender's statement.

B) Attempting to gain additional information, continue the discussion, or clarify a point.
C) Attempting to explain what the sender's statement means.

D) Attempting to evaluate the credibility of the sender.

Correct Answer: B) Attempting to gain additional information, continue the
discussion, or clarify a point.

Question #9: Which of the following is/are example(s) of interpersonal exchange?

A) Dialogues

B) All of the given options

C) Conversations

D) Small group discussions

Correct Answer: B) All of the given options

Question #10: All of the following are categories of feedback, except:

A) Evaluation

B) Probing

C) Interpretation

D) Noise

Correct Answer: D) Noise

Question 1: The quality of tone is a combination of which of the following?

A) Pitch, strength, and volume
B) Pitch, strength, and character
C) Volume, rate, and fillers
D) Fillers, character, and volume

Answer: A) Pitch, strength, and volume

Question 2: Which phrase shows the key rule to make a good speech?

A) To keep in mind the idea that is presented in the speech.
B) To avoid the audience’s choice and focus most on things that directly affect them.
C) Centering on the self
D) To keep in mind that audiences care most about things that directly affect them.

Answer: D) To keep in mind that audiences care most about things that directly
affect them.

Question 3: All of the following you may want to carry while doing your research
except one. Which one is?

A) Bibliography
B) Video games
C) Information-recording
D) Note-cards

Answer: B) Video games

Question 4: Business associates can be a helpful source for:

A) None of the given options
B) Can reveal questions and concerns
C) Identifying probable questions and objections
D) It is helpful to recall the questions

Answer: C) Identifying probable questions and objections
Anonymous

Question 5: The term pitch refers to which of the following?

A) The degree of lowness of sound
B) The degree of highness or lowness of a sound
C) The degree of highness of sound
D) All of the given options

Answer: B) The degree of highness or lowness of a sound

Question 6: Which of the following is not the type of electronic periodical indexes?

A) World Wide Web
B) Journals
C) Online systems
D) CD-ROM

Answer: B) Journals

Question 7: Which of the following is usually related to environmental factors that
affect communication?

A) Listener barrier
B) Resistance
C) Sender barrier
D) Physical barrier

Answer: D) Physical barrier

Question 8: Which of the following barriers are most often the hardest to identify
and reduce or eliminate?

A) None of the above
B) Sender barriers
C) Listener barriers
D) Physical barriers

Answer: A) None of the above
