 Topic-106: Borrowing
 Define borrowing in linguistics.
 Borrowing is the process by which a word from one language is adapted for
 use in another, resulting in loanwords.
 Give examples of borrowed words and their source languages.
 Croissant (French), Dope (Dutch), Lilac (Persian), Piano (Italian), Pretzel
 (German), etc.
 What is loan translation, and provide an example.
 Loan translation involves translating elements of a word into the borrowing
 language; e.g., "moment of truth" from Spanish "el momento de la verdad."
 Topic-107: Compounding / Blending
 Explain compounding as a word formation process.
 Compounding involves joining two separate words to create a single form, e.g.,
 bookcase, fingerprint.
 Provide examples of blending.
 Smog (smoke + fog), brunch (breakfast + lunch), motel (hotel + motor).
 What is clipping, and give examples.
 Clipping involves reducing a word, e.g., fax from facsimile, gas (gasoline), ad
 (advertisement).

 Topic-109: Acronyms
 Define an acronym.
 An acronym is a word or name formed from the initial components of a phrase
 or word, often individual letters.
 Provide examples of acronyms.
 CD (compact disk), VCR (video cassette recorder), NATO, NASA.
 How are initials pronounced in acronyms?
 Initials in acronyms are pronounced as new single words.
 Topic-110: Derivation (Prefixes, Suffixes, Infixes)
 What is a derivational affix, and how does it differ from an inflectional
 affix?
 A derivational affix forms a word from another, differs from inflectional affixes
 by not being obligatory, occurring closer to the root, and having a more
 meaningful impact.
 Give examples of prefixes and suffixes in English words.
 Prefixes: un-, mis-. Suffixes: -less, -ish.
 What is an infix, and provide examples in English.
 Infix is an affix not commonly used in English; examples include

                              GRAMMAR I
 Topic 111: Grammar
 What does the word 'grammar' mean and its origin?
 Answer: The word 'grammar' comes from the Greek word 'gramma,' meaning
 'art of letters.'
 Define grammar and its components.
 Answer: Grammar is the set of structural rules governing the composition of
 clauses, phrases, and words in a natural language. It includes phonology,
 morphology, and syntax, complemented by phonetics, semantics, and
 pragmatics.
 How do speakers acquire grammar in their native language?
 Answer: Speakers acquire grammar in their native language primarily
 through observation, not conscious study or instruction, often during early
 childhood.
 Name some early systematic grammars and their origins.
 Answer: Yaska, Pāṇini, Pingala, Katyayana, and Patanjali were early
 systematic grammarians of Sanskrit, originating in Iron Age India.
 What are the strict rules for combining words into phrases in English?
 Answer: English has strict rules for combining words into phrases, such as
 Noun Phrase, Verb Phrase, Adjective Phrase, Adverb Phrase, and
 Prepositional Phrase.
 Topic 112: Traditional Grammar
 Define traditional grammar.
 Answer: Traditional grammar is the collection of prescriptive rules and
 concepts about language structure, often based on Latin grammar principles
 and focusing on a historical model of proper language use.

 Name some characteristics of traditional grammar.
 Answer: Mechanics (Proper Punctuation), Style (Sentence Structure), Usage
 (Diction and Parts of Speech), and Style (Spelling).
 Topic 113: The Parts of Speech
 What is a part of speech in traditional grammar?
 Answer: A part of speech is a category of words with similar grammatical
 properties, defined by early grammarians like Yāska and Dionysius Thrax.
 Topic 114: Agreement
 What are the categories involved in agreement in traditional grammar?
 Answer: Number, person, tense, voice, and gender are categories involved in
 agreement in traditional grammar.
 Explain how gender works in grammatical terms.
 Answer: Grammatical gender is different from natural gender; it's based on
 noun type (masculine, feminine). Some languages, like Spanish and German,
 have different forms for articles and adjectives based on noun gender.
