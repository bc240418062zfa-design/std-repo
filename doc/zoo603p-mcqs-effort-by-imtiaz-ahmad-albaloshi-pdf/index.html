Zoo603P: Fish Biology(MCQS)

Whatsapp:03196386297

1. What is fish ranching?

Answer: Fish ranching involves restocking of the wild stock for harvesting.

2. During summers, the temperature of the freshwater ecosystem generally ranges from_
fahrenheit.

Answer: 30-71 degrees

3. In which aquatic habitat are cichlids and poeciliids commonly found?

Answer: Primary freshwater

4. The freshwater ecosystem is mainly divided into_ types based on its region.

Answer: three

5. The authors' examinates for global fisheries employment are_ times higher than the
foods and Agriculture organization estimates.

Answer: 1.75

6. Which of the following is not a recommended measurement tool for fish taxonomy?

Answer: Wooden scale

7. Fences are used to guide fish towards traps or pockets in_ ?

Answer: Coastal areas

8. Which type of fishing line uses natural or artificial baits with hooks to capture fish?

Answer: Set line

9. The study of fishes is called?

Answer: Ichthyology

10. The jawed fishes are called?

Answer: Gnathans

11. The jawless fishes are called?

Answer: Agnathans

12. Which of the following is a poisonous fish?

Answer: Pufferfish

13. This is the simple form of fishing where no gear gets involved.

Answer: Fishing without gears

14. In this line the one end of lines gets fastened with floats, buoys and markers whereas
the other end is tied with boats.

Answer: Drift line

15. In which line, only predatory fishes get caught?

Answer: Troll lines

16. It is in a powder form and can be used to make formalin solution.

Answer: Paraformaldehyde

17. _ are commonly used to fix and preserve fish specimens?

Answer: Ethanol and Isopropanol

18. Labeo is a _ fish?

Answer: herbivorous fish

19. _ are absent in Labeo?

Answer: Intestinal villi and scroll valve

20. In fact over 2500 species of fish comprise about_ of the vertebrate population.

Answer: 50%

21. Labeo rohita is a carp that is mostly found in?

Answer: rivers and ponds

22. The_ has four nares(nostrils) one on each side of and above the mouth?

Answer: Perch

23. These are fine-supporting structures?

Answer: Fin-rays

24. _ rays are segmented, often branched and biserial?

Answer: Soft rays

25. Orbital and temporal bones are?

Answer: Flat

26. Jaw bones are supported by?

Answer: Dentary bone

27. Triangular bone is _ which limits the movement of lower jaw.

Answer: quadrate

28. _rays are present in one row and it reduces the friction?

Answer: Fin-rays

29. Articulation of fin is performed by using?

Answer: radials

30. _ is composed of two pelvic bones?

Answer: Pelvic girdle

31. BOD stands for?

Answer: Biochemical oxygen demand

32. COD stands for?

Answer: Chemical oxygen demand

33. Water quality is influenced by _and natural factors?

Answer: Anthropogenic activities

34. _refers to the cloudiness of water and is a measure of the ability of light to pass
through it.

Answer: Turbidity

35. _reflects the concentration of vegetation and inorganic matter in water?

Answer: Color
