Topic 8 Level of organization

Four levels of biological organization—
    a) organismal, organisms interactions with their environments
b) Population, how populations changes over time
c) Community, different populations interactions in a community
d) Ecosystem, examine the living species (the biotic components) of the ecosystem and the
nonliving portions (abiotic components), such as air, water, and soil, of the environment.
