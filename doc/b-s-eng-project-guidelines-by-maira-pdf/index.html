   “Project Guidelines by Maira Shahzadi”

     What is a Research Project or Thesis?

 Thesis or project is a research work in which you’ll be
provided with a topic by your instructor and you’ve to do
                  research on that topic.
Your work should be plagiarism free in order to pass that
specific project course because it’s mandatory for degree
                        completion.

              “Project Guidelines by Maira Shahzadi”

     Components of the Project/Thesis

  Thesis/Project comprised of mainly five chapters:

1. Introduction          2. Literature Review
3. Research Methodology
4. Results and Discussion
5. Findings/conclusion/Recommendations

          “Project Guidelines by Maira Shahzadi”

                   1. Introduction
In the introduction chapter, you’ll write a brief
introduction of the topic assigned to you by the
instructor. You can get idea from the previous research
articles but you can’t take material from those articles
because that will make your work plagiarized and you’ll
get 0 marks in that assignment.

           “Project Guidelines by Maira Shahzadi”

Your first assignment will be about the introduction chapter and will include the
                                 following headings:
                                  1.1 Introduction
                write an introduction of the topic (2 pages almost)
                            1.2: Objectives of the study
     you’ll write the objectives of your study by using the words like “ to
                  investigate…, to explore…, to highlight..etc.
              you can add 1-2 objectives about your research study.
Example: To investigate the role of First language on the English speaking skills
                                      of students.
                              1.3: Research Questions
Add 1-2 research questions by using the question words e.g. what, how, why, etc.
 Example: What is the Role of L1 on the English speaking skills of the students.
             (Note: It’s just an example. Your topic will be different.)

                    “Project Guidelines by Maira Shahzadi”

                 1.4: Statement of the problem
 Under this sub-heading, you’ll write a short paragraph of about
200 words about the problem that you’re going to discuss in your
                         research study.
                 1.5: Significance of the study
  In this sub-heading, you’ve to write a paragraph of 200-300
  words about the significance/importance of the topic for the
          audience (teachers, students, local people, etc)

                “Project Guidelines by Maira Shahzadi”

          Chapter 2: Literature Review
This is the second chapter of your thesis and you’ve to write
this in your 2nd assignment after the approval of your 1st
assignment which was about the introduction.
Literature Review generally means “ the review of the previous
studies that has been done on that specific topic you’re
assigned with.
You’ve to read about 4-5 articles after downloading from the
Google, Google scholar. I’ll prefer Google Scholar because you
can easily get references as well from the Google scholar.
             “Project Guidelines by Maira Shahzadi”

              Read and open the notepad with your PDF.
   Read the Abstract, methodology and conclusion section of the
   articles you’re reading and write these three sections in a short
              paragraph about 100 words for one article.
(Note: Write the literature review in your own words without using
 the same words as used in the article otherwise your work will be
                              plagiarized.)
Also write the name of the researcher before starting the review of
            the articles e.g. Moyer (2014) investigated ....
 Read 4-5 articles in the same way and write their reviews in your
                               own words.
This is the work you’ve to done in the literature review chapter and
              this chapter should be less than 800 words.
                “Project Guidelines by Maira Shahzadi”

            Chapter 3: Research Methodology

    This chapter is mostly included in the 2nd assignment with
literature review for Eng528 project but mostly for eng 525,526,
 this chapter is written as a separate one. Whatever it’s place is,
                 the method and formatting is same.
              It includes the following sub-headings:
         3.1: Research Design 3.2: Research Method
        3.3: Population of the study       3.4: Sample size
      3.5: Sampling Technique 3.6: Research instruments
             3.7: Reliability and validity of the tools
                “Project Guidelines by Maira Shahzadi”

First of all just design survey questionnaires which should include a
maximum of 20 questions and decide what type of research design
you’ll choose in order to collect data e.g. your research method will
  be qualitative or quantitative? You will collect data from online
  survey design or by physically visiting the schools or colleges?
                     After deciding the above factors,
                  select the population and sample size.
E.g. Students of Punjab Group of Colleges will be your population
           and the number of students (100,200) will be the
                         sample size of the study.
         In the Methodology chapter, you just have to design
     questionnaires about your topic, select the research design,
                    population, size and instruments.
                 “Project Guidelines by Maira Shahzadi”

            Chapter 4: Results and Discussion
In this assignment, you’ve to distribute the questionnaires to the
students whether through online survey forms or by taking prints
 of the questionnaire and hand over to the students by physically
                visiting them in school or college.
  After you got your data, analyze it and write it in the form of
                         tables and charts.
