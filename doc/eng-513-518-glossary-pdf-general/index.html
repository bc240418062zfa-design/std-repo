                                 ENG(513-518) Glossary

    Accredited :Accredited is (Of a person, organization, or course of study) officially recognized or
     authorized.
    Allocation :The action or process of allocating or sharing out something is called allocation.
    Alternative 'Concurrent' Model :A student simultaneously studies both one or more academic
     subjects, and the ways of teaching that subject, leading to a combined bachelor's degree and
     teaching credential to qualify as a teacher of that subject.
    Anomaly :Something that deviates from what is standard, normal, or expected
    Approach :It is a way of looking at teaching and learning. Underlying any language teaching
     approach is a theoretical view of what language is, and of how it can be learnt. An approach
     gives rise to methods, the way of teaching something, which use classroom activities or
     techniques to help learners learn.
    Assess :Evaluate or judge
    Audio Lingual Method :It is a teaching method and based on behaviorist theory, which
     postulates that certain traits of living things, and in this case humans, could be trained through a
     system of reinforcement. The correct use of a trait would receive positive feedback while
     incorrect use of that trait would receive negative feedback.
    Autonomous :(Of a country or region) having the freedom to govern itself or control its own
     affairs.
    Caution :Vigilance, carefulness
    Collocation :The habitual juxtaposition of a particular word with another word or words with a
     frequency greater than chance is called collocation.
    Communicative Language Teaching Approach :It is an approach to language teaching that
     emphasizes interaction as both the means and the ultimate goal of study.
    Community Language Learning :It is a language-teaching approach in which students work
     together to develop what aspects of a language they would like to learn. It is based on the
     Counselling-approach in which the teacher acts as a counselor and a paraphraser, while the
     learner is seen as a client and collaborator.
    Concocted :Assemble
    Concordance :Agreement or consistency
    Consecutive' Model :A student simultaneously studies both one or more academic subjects, and
     the ways of teaching that subject, leading to a combined bachelor's degree and teaching
     credential to qualify as a teacher of that subject
    Content Based Instruction :The focus of a CBI lesson is on the topic or subject matter. During
     the lesson students are focused on learning about something. This could be anything that
     interests them from a serious science subject to their favourite pop star or even a topical news
     story or film.
    Contextualize :Place or study in context.

    Continuous Professional Development :CPD is the process by which teachers (like other
     professionals) reflect upon their competencies, keep them up to date, and develop them
     further.
    Continuum :Range, variety
    Cooperative Language Learning :It is group learning activity organized so that learning is
     dependent on the socially structured exchange of information between learners in groups and
     in which each learner is held accountable for his or her own learning and is motivated to
     increase the learning of others.
    Crucible :A situation of severe trial, or in which different elements interact, leading to the
     creation of something new
    Curricula :It is the plural of curriculum which means syllabus
    Deontology :The study of the nature of duty and obligation
    Dimensions :Aspects, features
    Discourse :In linguistics, discourse refers to a unit of language longer than a single sentence.
     More broadly, discourse is the use of spoken or written language in a social context.
    ESP (English for Specific Purposes) :It stands for English for Specific Purposes. It is defined to
     meet specific needs of the learners. It makes use of underlying methodology and activities of the
     discipline it serves. It is centered on the language appropriate to these activities in terms of
     grammar, lexis, register, study skills, discourse and genre.
    Feedback :Feedback is information given to the learner about his/her performance with the
     objective of improving performance.
    Grammar :Grammar is the set of rules and conventions of language shared by a group of native
     speakers (whereby we decide whether a particular language form is right or wrong).
    Grammar Translation Method :Sometimes also known as the Classical Method, this is a
     traditional teaching technique that was used to teach Latin and Greek and was particularly in
     vogue during the 16th Century. The focus at this time was on the translation of texts, grammar,
     and rote learning of vocabulary. There was no emphasis on speaking and listening
     comprehension because Latin and Greek were taught more as academic subjects rather than a
     means of oral communication.
    In-service :(Of training) intended for those actively engaged in the profession or activity
     concerned.
    Innateness :Existing in, belonging to, or determined by factors present in an individual from
     birth : native
    Interpersonal :The interpersonal function refers to the grammatical choices that enable
     speakers to enact their complex and diverse interpersonal relations. This tenet of systemic
     functional linguistics is based on the claim that a speaker not only talks about something, but is
     always talking to and with others.
    Intrapersonal :It is a communicator's internal use of language or thought. It can be useful to
     envision intrapersonal communication occurring in the mind of the individual in a model which
     contains a sender, receiver, and feedback loop.
