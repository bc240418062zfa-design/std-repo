Q#02: What is In-text citation?

An in-text citation is a way to credit the original source of information within the body of your
writing. It briefly identifies the source by including the author's last name and year of
publication (APA) or the author's name and page number (MLA), depending on the citation
style used.

Why Use In-Text Citations?

    To avoid plagiarism
    To give credit to the original author
    To support arguments with credible sources

Q#06: What is literature review for reference?

A literature review for reference refers to a section in a research paper, thesis, or academic
work that surveys existing studies, books, articles, and other scholarly sources relevant to a
specific topic. It provides an overview, synthesis, and critical evaluation of prior research to
establish the foundation for a new study.

Components of a Literature Review:

   ➢ Introduction
   ➢ Method
   ➢ Results
   ➢ Discussion.

Q#11: Describe the importance of a literature review.

A literature review is an important part of research because it helps you understand what has
already been studied about a topic. It provides background information, identifies gaps in
knowledge, and ensures that your work is not just repeating what others have done. By reviewing
previous studies, you can build a strong foundation for your research, choose the best methods,
and support your ideas with existing knowledge. It also helps combine different viewpoints,
showing patterns and differences in past studies. Most importantly, a literature review justifies
why your research is needed and makes your work more credible.
