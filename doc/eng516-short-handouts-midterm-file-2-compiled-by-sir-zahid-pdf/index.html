Lesson 06
                         NEEDS ANALYSIS-II
Topic - 031: Communication Needs
What is the purpose of a needs analysis in teaching a course?
The needs analysis is done to determine communication needs, business needs, and
pedagogical needs of learners and teachers before teaching a course.
How can different communication situations affect communication needs?
Different communication situations give rise to different communication needs,
and these needs can be traditional (like letters and reports) or modern (like internet,
intranet, fax, instant messages).
How is needs analysis conducted for learners' communication needs?
Needs analysis for learners' communication needs involves finding out what
learners need to do in English, such as using English with clients or writing
assignments.
What tools can be used for needs analysis in communication?
Tools for needs analysis include questionnaires, interviews, observation, and
commercially available tests.

What is the purpose of a placement test in assessing communicative
competence?
A placement test assesses the current communicative competence of learners and
helps place them with others having similar needs and ability.
What are the characteristics of a reliable placement test?
A reliable placement test should be valid, have face validity, authenticity, and be
consistent in its results.
How do task-based assessments reflect real-life situations?
Task-based assessments use knowledge gained from the target needs analysis to
design tasks that reflect real-life situations, such as giving presentations for
business English learners.
Can you provide an explanation of how task-based assessments reflect real-
life situations and what steps are involved in designing such assessments?
Task-based assessments are designed to reflect real-life situations that learners will
encounter. For example, business English learners may be asked to give
presentations if they are expected to do so in their job roles. Designing these
assessments involves considering the target needs analysis and creating tasks that
mirror the actual workplace scenarios.
Topic - 033: Pedagogical Needs
What are pedagogical needs based on?
Pedagogical needs are based on the teacher's style, experience, and needs, the
learning environment, and the learning styles of learners.
What are some aspects of a teacher's role in addressing pedagogical needs?
A teacher's role can include being a trainer, coach, and consultant, adapting their
teaching style, and being a reflective practitioner.
Topic - 034: Language Styles
How can learning styles be influenced by culture?
Learning styles can be influenced by the culture of learners, where expectations of
the teacher's role may differ.
What is NLP, and how can it be useful to business English teachers?
NLP, or Neuro-Linguistic Programming, provides insights into communication
skills, including non-verbal communication, active listening, and perception, which
can be useful for business English teachers.
What is Neuro-Linguistic Programming (NLP), and how can it be applied in
teaching business English and enhancing communication skills?
Neuro-Linguistic Programming (NLP) is a collection of techniques and theories
that offer insights into how the mind works and how people communicate. NLP
can be applied to teaching business English by improving communication skills,
such as building rapport, non-verbal communication, and active listening, both for

Topic - 035: Business Needs
Why is understanding business needs crucial for a BE teacher?
Understanding business needs is crucial because it helps the teacher align the
course with the sponsor's, company's, or department's expectations, ensuring that
the training is worthwhile.
What are some key aspects a teacher should consider about the company
when researching business needs?
The teacher should consider the company's product, market, size, and various
business functions, and understand the hierarchy of the company.
Topic - 036: Main Priorities
How does a practical needs analysis help in course design?
A practical needs analysis helps in course design by considering factors like target
situation analysis, communication needs, discourse, and means analysis, which
includes facilities and learner placement.
What role does the sponsor play in determining course design?
The sponsor often has a clear idea of the learners' needs and might influence course
design, as seen in formal reports and course design decisions.
Is needs analysis entirely objective?
Needs analysis is not entirely objective, as it involves decisions based on teachers'
interests, values, and beliefs about teaching, learning, and language. It is both
preparatory and ongoing.

                     DESIGNING A COURSE-I
Topic 037: Aims
What is the role of a needs analysis in course design?
A needs analysis gathers information to inform course design decisions.
How does a needs analysis influence course design?
It influences the development of syllabus, materials, methodology, and evaluation.
What are the general aims of a Business English course?
A Business English course aims to achieve specific communication and
pedagogical goals.
What is the connection between course aims and the needs analysis?
Course aims are derived from the information gathered in the needs analysis.
