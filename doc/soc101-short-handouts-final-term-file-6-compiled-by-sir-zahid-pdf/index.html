Topic-142: Trends in Families: Divorce

What is the trend in divorce rates in societies like Pakistan?

Divorce rates are increasing, especially in urban areas and among better-educated
and wealthier families.

What factors influence the rise in divorce rates in Pakistan?

Shifting cultural norms, women's increased economic independence, and easier
access to legal assistance and support.

How is the divorce procedure governed in Pakistan?

The divorce procedure is governed by complex Islamic law.

Topic-143: Single Parent Families

How have single-parent families evolved over the past few decades?

Single-parent families have advanced the most, with divorce or separation being the
primary cause.

What are the effects of single-parent households on children, according to
Thompson and Hickey (2016)?

Children from single-parent households are more likely to have behavioral issues,
drop out of school, face legal problems, and experience health issues.

Topic-144: Blended Family

Define a blended family.

A blended family is formed when two divorced individuals remarry, bringing their
children from previous marriages into the new household.

What challenges do blended families face?

They need to be resourceful and adaptable, dealing with the complexities of roles
between biological parents and stepparents.

Contrary to common assumption, what is the reality about blended families
losing their distinct identities?

Most blended families do not lose their distinct identities, sense of self, and
emotional links to their birth families.

Topic-148: Reproduction and Union Formation

How do social norms and legal restrictions control reproduction and union
formation?

Answer: Social norms and legal restrictions influence how people reproduce and
establish unions, with different customs around courtship, dating, and marriage.

What factors are strongly connected with reproduction and union formation in
many countries?

Answer: Social status, race, and religion are often strongly connected with
reproduction and union formation.

How can economic factors impact the creation and growth of unions?

Answer: Economic factors such as employment and educational possibilities can
have an impact on the creation and growth of unions.

Topic-149: Mate Selection

What is the process of choosing love relationships called?

Answer: The process is called mate selection.

What factors can influence the mate selection process?

Answer: Socioeconomic status, personality traits, physical attractiveness, family and
social expectations, caste, religion, and family history can influence mate selection.

Topic-150: Arranged Marriages

Who actively participates in selecting spouses in arranged marriages?

Answer: Parents or other family members actively participate in selecting spouses
in arranged marriages.

Why are arranged marriages still common in many nations, including
Pakistan?

Answer: Arranged marriages are seen as a way to ensure compatibility and

What role do economic factors play in arranged marriages?

Answer: Economic factors may be involved, as families may exchange gifts or
dowries as part of arranged marriages.
