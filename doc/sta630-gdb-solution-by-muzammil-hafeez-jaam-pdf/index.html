                                            Scenario:
In the coming decades, artificial intelligence will almost certainly be deployed in every
workplace. It will automate routine tasks while advancing the human skills and capabilities. A
substantial possibility exists that it may result in the elimination or restructuring of conventional
jobs. On the other hand, it will also create new employment opportunities, particularly in the
fields of data analytics, human-AI collaboration, and the development of artificial intelligence
software and apps. However, it's also necessary to comprehend the risks associated with the use
of AI since it will reduce the use of the human intellect. Hence, it may have both positive and
negative impacts at the same time. The rapid advancement of Artificial Intelligence (AI) has
challenged researchers to test the underlying theories to investigate the relationship between AI
and human efficiency.

                                        Requirement:
After carefully analyzing the above scenario, you are required to identify which type of research
(descriptive, exploratory, or explanatory) is most appropriate to be used for data analysis.
Provide sound justifications to support your choice.

                                            Solution:
The scenario outlined discusses the integration of artificial intelligence into workplaces and its
potential impacts, both positive and negative. Given the diverse facets of this integration and its
effects on jobs, efficiency, and human-AI collaboration, the most appropriate type of research for
data analysis would likely be a mix of exploratory and explanatory research.
1. Exploratory Research:
                              This type of research is suitable because it allows for an initial
exploration and understanding of the subject. With the rapid advancement of AI and its various
potential impacts on jobs and human efficiency, an exploratory approach would enable
researchers to gather data, generate hypotheses, and understand the different dimensions of AI
integration in the workplace. It allows for the identification of emerging patterns and issues that
might not be apparent initially.
2. Explanatory Research:
                              This type of research helps in explaining the relationship between
different variables. In this scenario, explanatory research would be essential to delve deeper into

understanding the cause-and-effect relationships between the deployment of AI, job restructuring,
changes in human skills, and the resultant impacts on efficiency. It would help in exploring why
certain changes occur and how they affect the dynamics of the workplace.
Given the complexity and the need to understand both the broader landscape (exploratory) and
the specific cause-and-effect dynamics (explanatory) of AI integration in the workplace, a blend
of exploratory and explanatory research methodologies would be most appropriate for
comprehensive data analysis. This combination would allow for a more nuanced understanding
of the multifaceted impacts of AI on jobs, human capabilities, and efficiency.
