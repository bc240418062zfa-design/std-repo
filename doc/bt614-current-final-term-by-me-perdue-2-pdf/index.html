Question:
Which is the content present in matrix of mitochondria?

Answer: The matrix of mitochondria contains most of the enzymes of the tricarboxylic acid (TCA)
cycle, ribosomes, and 75kbp circular DNA molecules that code for some 10% of mitochondrial
proteins.

What are the yield of coefficient of saccharomyces carvisiae in anaerobic and aerobic
conditions?

Answer: Saccharomyces cerevisiae grown on glucose has biomass yield coefficients of 0.56 and
0.12 g/g under aerobic and anaerobic conditions, respectively.

Or

Yield coefficient of Saccharomyces cerevisiae in anaerobic and aerobic conditions:

Aerobic conditions: 0.56 g biomass/g glucose

Anaerobic conditions: 0.12 g biomass/g glucose.

3) How b- lactam act as a antibiotic?/Mechanism of β-lactam antibiotics.

Answer: β-lactam antibiotics inhibit peptidoglycan synthesis by binding to transpeptidase
enzymes. This prevents cross-linking of peptidoglycan, weakening the bacterial cell wall and
leading to cell lysis.

Write two types of culture collection?

Answer: The American type culture collection (ATCC)

European collection of animal cell cultures (ECACC)

Why we use antifoam?/ Use of antifoam

Answer: Antifoams are used in fermentation to reduce foam formation, which can block air
filters and cause contamination. They work by reducing surface tension. Natural antifoams
include plant oils and fish oils, while synthetic ones include silicon oils and poly alcohols.

How many ways to harvested the cell?

Answer: Harvesting of biomass in fermentation includes:

Filtration

Centrifugation

Flocculation

Sedimentation.

Anoxygenic photosynthetic bacteria.

Answer: Anoxygenic photosynthetic bacteria perform photosynthesis without producing
oxygen.

They use three main components:

1. Light-harvesting complex – captures light using bacteriochlorophyll and pigments.

2. Reaction center – excites electrons using bacteriochlorophyll.

3. transport systemElectron (ETS) – moves electrons through molecules like
bacteriopheophytins and cytochromes.

Instead of water, they use sulfur compounds, hydrogen, or organic molecules as electron
donors. Light excites bacteriochlorophyll, releasing an electron that moves through ETS to
generate energy.

8. Difference between prion and aquaprion.

Answer: 1. Prion

A misfolded protein that can cause fatal brain diseases.

Linked to diseases like mad cow disease and Creutzfeldt-Jakob disease in humans.

Does not contain DNA or RNA but can spread by forcing normal proteins to misfold.

Well-studied and confirmed in science.

2. Aquaprion

A proposed (not yet confirmed) protein that may help transport water in cells.

Thought to work like aquaporins, which allow water to move through cell membranes.

No known link to diseases.

Still under research and not yet fully understood.

Key Difference: Prions are disease-causing proteins, while aquaprions (if real) may play a role in
water transport in cells.

How foams are produced?Disadvantages of foam formation.

Answer: Foaming occurs when media proteins attach to the air–broth interface and denature,
forming a stable foam.

Disadvantages:

Blocks air filters, leading to contamination

Reduces fermentation efficiency

Requires additional space ("freeboard") in fermenters.

How cells are harvested?

Answer: Filtration

Centrifugation (tubular, disc stack, multichamber bowl centrifuges)

Flocculation and sedimentation

Membrane separation (ultrafiltration, reverse osmosis).

Why transposons are called jumping genes?

Answer: Transposons are DNA sequences that can move (or "jump") from one part of the
genome to another, either within the same DNA molecule or between different molecules (e.g.,
from plasmids to chromosomes).

5. Obligate use CO2. How carbon dioxide is produced in it?

Answer: Some organisms, such as autotrophic microbes, require CO₂ for biosynthesis.

CO₂ is produced in metabolic pathways such as the citric acid cycle and pyruvate
decarboxylation.

1. what is beer? Objective of mashing system.

Answer: Beer:The term beer is given to non-distilled alcoholic beverages made from

partially germinated cereal grains, referred to as malt.

Objective of mashing system: Converts starches in malt into fermentable sugars using enzymes,
optimizing sugar extraction for fermentation. Or The objective in mashing is to convert as much
of the malt and adjunct starch as possible to fermentable sugars.

What are photoautrophs? write their types.

Answer: Photoautotrophs are organisms that absorb light energy and convert it into chemical
energy (ATP and reduced coenzymes) via the light reactions of photosynthesis.

Types:

1. Bacteriochlorophyll-based (Purple sulfur bacteria, green bacteria, purple non-sulfur bacteria)

2. Chlorophyll-based (Cyanobacteria, algae, higher plants)

3. Bacteriorhodopsin-based (Archaea such as Halobacteria).
