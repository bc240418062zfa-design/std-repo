                               ENG516_Glossary

1. Accuracy :Accuracy in technical communication refers to grammatical and
   contextual correctness.
2. Achievable objectives :Achievable objectives are those objectives which are
   achievable and attainable in terms of abilities, realistic facilities and logistics.
3. Assessment and Evaluation :Assessment is concerned with individual student’s
   learning. Evaluation refers to course evaluation as a whole.
4. Authentic Materials :The term ‘authentic’ denotes that the texts were written
   for purposes other than language teaching and learning. Authentic materials
   could be real commercial documents, newspaper articles, recordings of meetings
   and conversations used in the workplace, newspaper clippings, excerpts from
   case studies, reports.
5. Aviation English :Aviation English is the de facto language of civil aviation
   which is called Radiotelephony; it is a specialized communication between pilots
   and air traffic controllers, and other personnel associated with the aviation
   industry.
6. Backwash Effect :When the course is designed with the test in mind, its effect
   is called wash back. Wash-back, according to Brown and Hudson, is “the effect
   of testing and assessment on the language teaching curriculum that is related to
   it”.
7. Business Communication :The communication which takes place at the
   workplace, in the educational setting, corporate office, or in a government job, is
   called business communication and is basically carried out in the English
   language.

8. Business English for Academic Purposes :English for academic purposes or
   EAP comes under the umbrella of ESP, and is required for those who use
   language to perform academic tasks or for employment.
9. Case Studies :A case study is a description of a problem or decision faced by a
   company business. It describes a specific business problem and creates a jumping
   off point for analysis and discussion.
10. Code of conduct :Code of conduct is the rules which are observed in different
   fields of life including education and pedagogy.
11. Communication Needs :Communication needs are the communication
   channels - traditional like letters and reports and the telephone as well as modern
   like the internet, intranet, fax, and instant messages-which we use to
   communicate.
12. Communicative Language Teaching :Communicative language teaching
   (CLT), aims to develop learners’ ability to use language to communicate. CLT is
   not a precise term and can mean different things to different people.
13. Controlling :Setting standards, checking actual performance against
   standards, taking corrective action as needed
14. Course Books :Course books are the major source of framework material
   which can be used extensively.
15. Dealing with Professional Content :A contentious issue in teaching of
   Business Communication is the amount of professional content in the course
   design.
16. Designing a Course :Designing a business English course is a matter of
   making a series of decisions based on information gathered during a needs
   analysis about learners’ communication, business and pedagogical needs.

   language in use, although it is important to note that the word discourse can be
   used in a variety of ways by language professionals.

19. EBE :English for Business and Economics
20. EGAP :English for General Academic Purposes
21. EGBP :English for General Business Purposes: targets those learners who
   have lack of working experience (pre-experienced or low-experience)
22. EGP :English for General Purposes
23. EMP :English for Medical Purposes
24. EOP :English for Occupational Purposes
25. ERPP :English for Research Publication Purposes
26. ESAP :English for Visual Arts Study
27. ESBP :English for Specific Business Purpose
28. ESS :English for Social Sciences
29. EST :English for Science and Technology
30. Ethnocentrism :Ethnocentrism is the view that one’s culture is the center of
   everything, and other cultural groups are judged with reference to it and are
   compared to one’s cultural group.
31. EVP :English for Vocational Purposes
32. Feedback :Feedback is information given to the learner about his/her
   performance with the objective of improving performance.
33. Framework Materials :Frameworks are explicitly structured contexts
   designed to help teachers and learners.

34. Grammar :Grammar is the set of rules and conventions of language shared
   by a group of native speakers (whereby we decide whether a particular language
   form is right or wrong).
35. Grid :A grid or framework sets out the aims and objectives, followed by all
   the elements that we believe are necessary for someone to be able to meet the
   objectives.
36. Intercultural Competence :One’s ability to know and deal with attitudes,
   behaviors, beliefs, and values of other cultures other than one’s own culture.
37. Leading :Getting others to get the job done, motivating
38. Learning Strategies :Learning strategies are “…characteristics we want to
   stimulate in students to enable them to become more proficient language
   learners” (Oxford, 1990).
39. Learning Styles :Dunn and Dunn (1979) define learning styles as “A term
   that describes the variations among learners in using one or more senses to
   understand, organize, and retain experience”.
40. Linguistic Competence :Linguistic competence is shown in the use of the
   basic elements that go together to form a language, such as vocabulary, grammar,
   phonology.
41. Listening Skills :Listening is a conscious selective process, unlike hearing
   which is involuntary, depends on non-verbal signals.
