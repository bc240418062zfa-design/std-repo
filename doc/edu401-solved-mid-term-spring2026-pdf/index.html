Short Questions
1. Impact of Poverty on Learning

Poverty negatively affects learning by causing malnutrition, poor health, stress, lack of
educational resources, and low attendance. Students from poor families often achieve lower
academic performance and have higher dropout rates.

2. Properties of TKI Conflict Instrument

The Thomas-Kilmann Conflict Mode Instrument (TKI) identifies five conflict-handling styles:

      Competing
      Collaborating
      Compromising
      Avoiding
      Accommodating

It measures assertiveness and cooperativeness and helps individuals understand their conflict-
management behavior.

Long Questions
1. Build Your Argument on Madrassa and Radicalization in
Pakistan
Madrassas have historically played an important role in providing religious education in
Pakistan. They offer free education, accommodation, and food to many poor students. However,
concerns have been raised that some madrassas may promote narrow interpretations of religion
and contribute to extremism. Critics argue that lack of curriculum diversity and limited exposure
to modern subjects can isolate students from mainstream society. On the other hand, many
madrassas operate peacefully and contribute positively to education. Therefore, instead of
blaming all madrassas, reforms should focus on curriculum modernization, teacher training,
registration, transparency, and inclusion of science, mathematics, technology, and civic
education. Such reforms can help madrassas contribute more effectively to national
development.

2. Pakistan Constitutional Provisions for Education
The Constitution of Pakistan recognizes education as a fundamental right.

Article 25-A

The state shall provide free and compulsory education to all children aged 5–16 years.

Article 37-B

The state shall remove illiteracy and provide free and compulsory secondary education.

To implement these provisions, Pakistan needs:

      More schools and infrastructure
      Additional legislation
      Increased educational funding
      Public awareness
      Better educational facilities

3. Free and Accessible Education
Free and accessible education means providing equal educational opportunities to all learners

non-discrimination, participation, and achievement of all students. Key principles include:

4. Madaris Demands on Reform
Major demands regarding madrassa reforms include:

   1.   Recognition of madrassa certificates.
   2.   Financial support from the government.
   3.   Preservation of religious autonomy.
   4.   Inclusion of modern subjects without compromising religious education.
   5.   Teacher training and professional development.
   6.   Transparent registration system.
   7.   Participation of madrassa representatives in policymaking.

The objective is to integrate madrassas into the national education system while maintaining
their religious identity.
