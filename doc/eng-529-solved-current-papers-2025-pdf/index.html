Q: writing Pal?
Writing Pal (W-Pal) is a smart tutoring system created by the Science of Learning
and Educational Technology (SoLET) Lab at Arizona State University. It is mainly
designed for high school students who speak English as their first language.
However, some studies have also included English learners in high school and
college freshmen in the U.S. Unlike other automated writing tools, W-Pal focuses
on teaching writing strategies.
It follows four key principles:

1. Strategy Instruction
2. Modularity
3. Extended Practice
4. Formative Feedback
Q: E-marking?
E-marking is the process of giving marks and feedback by staff to students online.
This can include:

Online comments (e.g., using Blackboard inline grading)

Annotated files (e.g., adding comments in Microsoft Word)

Audio or video feedback

Online forms for grading

It makes grading easier and more accessible for both teachers and students.

Q: Multimedia, individual difference and advance difference principle?
1. Multimedia Principle – Students learn a language better when text is combined
with pictures instead of just text alone.

2. Individual Differences Principle – Students learn better when they can choose
between visual or verbal explanations based on their preference.

3. Advance Organizer Principle – Using advanced organizers (like summaries or
previews) helps students understand reading and listening tasks better, especially
when both visuals and words are used instead of just words alone.
Q: Functions of Google Book and Google Ngrams Addon?
Google Books NGram Viewer: This tool shows how often a word has been used
in books over time. It helps track how language changes.

Culturomics - Google Ngrams Addon: This is an extra feature for the Google
NGram Viewer, allowing you to download more data to study word trends.

Q: Reading theories for both L1 and L2 have attempted to explain reading
processes from three perspectives? structural
Structural perspectives believe readers just take in the meaning from the text.
cognitive
The cognitive view says reading is a process where readers use their background
knowledge to actively understand the text.
metacognitive
The metacognitive view sees reading as an active process where readers use
strategies to understand the text and monitor their comprehension.

Q: Benefit of ecomma ?
eComma is an open-source web tool that turns reading into a group activity, where
learners work together to understand a text. Developed by the Center for Open
Education Resources and Language Learning, it allows learners to help each other
understand a text by annotating it together.
Q: The interactionist hypothesis?

The Interactionist Hypothesis says that language learners first realize what they
don’t know and then learn by interacting with others. When communication
problems happen, learners work together to understand new words or grammar.
The Focus on Form approach helps learners focus on these language gaps during
activities.
Q: Benefits of E-assessment?
E-assessment offers several benefits, including:

Immediate feedback to help students improve.

Access from anywhere and at any time for students.

Detailed reports to identify areas needing more focus or improvement.

The ability for students to take multiple online tests to reassess their knowledge.

Q: Thing remember the teachers when designing curriculum with the help of
technology?
1. Clear Learning Goals: Set what students need to learn and how tech helps.

2. Integration of Technology: Use tools like e-learning to improve learning.

3. Alignment Across Stages: Make sure the curriculum connects different learning
stages.

4. Student Engagement: Use tech to make lessons more interactive.

5. Adaptability: Adjust the curriculum to fit different learning styles using tech.
Q:What is meant by ‘affordance’ in digital tools?
Affordance: The potential uses or benefits of a technology perceived by its users.
For example:
1. Subtitles in videos can aid comprehension.
2. Interactive features like pausing and annotating videos can enhance learning.
Q: How does technology develop learners’ thinking, and what are three
benefits?
Development of Thinking: Technology promotes critical, analytical, and problem-
solving skills.
Three Benefits:
1. Encourages higher-order thinking (analysis, synthesis).
2. Supports critical thinking through real-world scenarios.
3. Enhances problem-solving with interactive tools.
Q: Sociocultural theory?
Sociocultural Theory Says that language is learned through social interaction.
Working with others, like native or non-native speakers, helps learners improve
their language skills. This idea is used in modern language teaching, especially in
CALL, where collaboration and interaction are important for learning.
Q: characteristics of blended learning?

1. Mix of Online & Face-to-Face Learning – Combines classroom and online
teaching.

2. Use of Different Media – Includes print, audio, video, and interactive content.

3. Flexible Learning – Students and teachers can connect anytime, anywhere.

4. Student-Centered Approach – Focuses on active and engaging learning.

5. Technology Integration – Brings all learning tools together online.

6. Better Learning Experience – Makes learning more effective and enjoyable.
