    Drones are stout male bees with large eyes and no

Q. Ari lac and Phunki lac
Lac can be scraped from the twigs before or after the
emergence of larvae. If it is used for manufacturing before the
emergence of larvae, the type of lac produced is called as “Ari
lac”and
if it is used for manufacturing purpose after swarming of larvae
has occurred, the lac is said to be “Phunki lac”.
