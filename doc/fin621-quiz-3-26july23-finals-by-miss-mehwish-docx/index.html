1. Question: Which group of ratios relates gain on sales and investment?
   Options:
   a) Profitability ratios
   b) Liquidity ratios
   c) Debt ratios
   d) Coverage ratios
   Correct Option: a) Profitability ratios

2. Question: The percentage change is computed by dividing the amount of the dollar change between years and:
   Options:
   a) The average of past ten years
   b) The average of past five years
   c) The current year
   Correct Option: c) The current year

3. Question: In the end month of the accounting period, the company ABC obtains a long-term bank loan in order to pay off short-term obligations so that the working capital and current ratio may improve. The above is an example of:
   Options:
   a) Window Dressing
   b) Financial budgeting
   c) Financial forecasting
   d) Creativity of accounting
   Correct Option: a) Window Dressing

4. Question: Ratio analysis may be undertaken to study liquidity, turnover, profitability, and other indicators. To which does the current ratio most relate?
   Options:
   a) Liquidity
   b) Stability
   c) Solvency
   d) Profitability
   Correct Option: a) Liquidity

5. Question: Which group of ratios relates the financial charges of a firm to its ability to service them?
   Options:
   a) Coverage ratios
   b) Profitability ratios
   c) Debt ratios
   d) Liquidity ratios
   Correct Option: a) Coverage ratios

6. Question: A company has total current liabilities of Rs. 110,000 and total current assets of Rs. 150,000. Calculate the current ratio of the company.
   Options:
   a) 0.73
   b) 1.39
   c) 1.36
   d) 1.41
   Correct Option: c) 1.36

7. Question: The gross profit margin is unchanged, but the net profit margin declined over the same period. When could this have happened?
   Options:
   a) Cost of goods sold increased relative to sales
   b) The tax rate has increased
   c) Sales increased relative to expenses
   d) Dividends were decreased
   Correct Option: a) Cost of goods sold increased relative to sales

8. Question: Which of the following represents how quickly a company converts its inventory into cash?
   Options:
   a) Operating income
   b) Operating cycle
   c) Inventory turnover rate
   d) Days to sell inventory
   Correct Option: c) Inventory turnover rate

9. Question: Why do we calculate the Inventory turnover ratio?
   Options:
   a) To measure how quickly the company converts its inventory into cash
   b) To measure how quickly the company prepares its inventory
   c) To measure how quickly the company purchases its inventory
   d) To measure how quickly the company sells its inventory
   Correct Option: d) To measure how quickly the company sells its inventory

10. Question: Which one of the following is NOT one of the broad areas of a business?
    Options:
    a) Reliability
    b) Solvency
    c) Profitability
    d) Stability
    Correct Option: a) Reliability
