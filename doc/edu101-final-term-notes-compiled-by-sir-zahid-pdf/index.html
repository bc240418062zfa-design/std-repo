Q1: Who is the Swiss psychologist mentioned in the paragraph?
ANS: Pestalozzi.

Q2: In which decades did America take notice of Pestalozzi's
theories?
ANS: The 50's and 60's.

Q3: How should Pestalozzi's theories be interpreted?
ANS: They should be taken as "suggestive" rather than facts.

Q4: Which educators were influenced by Pestalozzi's theories?
ANS: Tyler, Taba, Bruner, Kohlberg, and many more.

Q5: What were some of the criticisms or observations made about
Tyler's approach?
ANS: Tyler employed various assessments.

Q6: What was Taba's criticism of education?
ANS: Taba believed there were too many facts and not enough
connections.

Q7: How did Bruner's stages differ from Piaget's stages?
ANS: Bruner's stages, like Piaget's, involved development in complexity,
but they were revisited.

Q8: Which psychologist is associated with moral stages?

COMPILED BY SIR ZAHID                                                      7

ANS: Kohlberg.

Q9: What is the first stage of Jean Piaget's cognitive developmental
stages and how long does it last?
ANS: The first stage is the sensorimotor stage, which lasts from birth to 2
years.

Q10: What are some characteristics of a child in the sensorimotor
stage?
ANS: A child in the sensorimotor stage uses their senses and motor skills
to explore the environment, begins using language to imitate and represent
the environment, and is egocentric.
