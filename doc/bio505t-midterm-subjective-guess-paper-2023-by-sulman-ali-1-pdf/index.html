Note on oligonucleotide in mapping of disease gene.
Oligonucleotide is a short DNA sequence that can be used in mapping disease genes. These
sequences can be used as probes to detect the presence of disease genes in a sample.
Oligonucleotide probes can be synthesized to match the sequence of a specific gene, and then
used to detect the presence of that gene in a sample. This technique is useful in identifying the
genetic basis of diseases.

Genetic and environmental causes of variation.
Genetic causes of variation include mutations and genetic recombination, difference in DNA
sequence that can affect traits such as eye color, hair color, and susceptibility to diseases.
Environmental variation: Differences in traits that are caused by environmental factors such as
nutrition, exposure to toxins, and access to healthcare. while environmental causes of variation
include exposure to different environmental factors such as temperature, light, and diet.

Spermatogesis and oogenesis.
Spermatogenesis is the process of producing sperm, while oogenesis is the process of producing
eggs. Spermatogenesis occurs in the testes of males, while oogenesis occurs in the ovaries of
females.

Difference between prokaryotes and eukaryotes.
Prokaryotes are unicellular organisms that do not have a nucleus or membrane-bound
organelles, while eukaryotes are multicellular organisms that have a nucleus and membrane-
bound organelles.

Common Epigenetics regulated phenomena:
Common epigenetic phenomena include DNA methylation, histone modification, and non-
coding RNA regulation.

CEN DNA.
CEN DNA is a sequence of DNA found in eukaryotic chromosomes that is responsible for
attaching the chromosomes to spindle fibers during cell division.

Explain the conformation of DNA.
The conformation of DNA is a double helix structure composed of nucleotides. The nucleotides
consist of a sugar molecule, a phosphate group, and a nitrogenous base. The nitrogenous bases
include adenine, thymine, cytosine, and guanine. Adenine pairs with thymine and cytosine pairs
with guanine through hydrogen bonds. The double helix structure of DNA is stabilized by
hydrogen bonds between the base pairs and the hydrophobic interactions between the stacked
bases.

Note on staining procedures.
Staining procedures are used in microscopy to enhance the contrast and visibility of biological
specimens. Different types of stains can be used to highlight different structures within a sample.
For example, hematoxylin and eosin stains are commonly used to differentiate between different
types of cells and tissues. Gram staining is used to differentiate between different types of
bacteria. Staining procedures can also be used to detect the presence of specific molecules within
a sample, such as antibodies or enzyme.

Difference btw continuous and discontinuous variation.
Discontinuous variation entirely genetically controlled. They cannot be altered by external
condition. blood group, color blind and dwarfism are genetically controlled.
Continuous variation describe the situation in which there are many intermediate between the
extremes. Example shade of hair color between the black and blond.

Define Division of meiosis:
Meiosis is a cellular division process that produces haploid cells with half the chromosome
number of the parent cell. It involves two rounds of division, resulting in four genetically diverse
daughter cells.

What is meaning of exception of mendelian inheritance?
The exceptions to Mendelian inheritance are patterns of inheritance that do not follow the rules
of dominance, segregation, and independent assortment described by Gregor Mendel. Some
examples of exceptions to Mendelian inheritance include: 1= Incomplete dominance: 2=
Codominance: 3= Multiple alleles: 4= Polygenic inheritance: 5= Epistasis:

Define leading strand, Lagging strand, okazaki fragments. Gyrase and sister
chromatids
In DNA replication, the leading strand is synthesized continuously in the 5' to 3' direction,
following the replication fork, while the lagging strand is synthesized discontinuously in short
fragments called Okazaki fragments. Okazaki fragments are later joined by DNA ligase to form
a continuous strand. Gyrase is an enzyme that helps relieve the tension caused by DNA
unwinding during replication. Sister chromatids are two identical copies of a chromosome

joined together at the centromere, formed during DNA replication, and are separated during cell
division.

Write note on genome imprinting:
Genomic imprinting is an epigenetic mechanism where certain genes are expressed differently
based on their parental origin. Imprinted genes are marked with epigenetic tags, such as DNA
methylation, during gamete formation. This leads to parent-specific gene expression patterns that
can influence embryonic development, growth, and disease susceptibility. Imprinting disorders
can result from abnormal gene expression patterns and have significant implications for human
health.

What happens in anaphase:
In anaphase, the sister chromatids of each chromosome separate and move towards opposite
poles of the cell. Microtubules attached to the centromeres shorten, pulling the chromatids apart
and ensuring equal distribution of genetic material.
