1. The meaning-focused output strand involves learning through speaking and
   writing—using language productively.
2. Speech is processed primarily in a sequential fashion.
3. Baton signals are hand and head movements, which are typically associated with
   emphasis and prosodic cadence
4. Discourse analysis, as a branch of pragmatics, is concerned with the ways
   listeners make use of linguistic information and background knowledge as they
   listen in a social context.
5. Formulating a grammatical system can be achieved only through the processes
   of extraction.
6. The listening skill is a semi-recursive activity.
7. Brown defines cognitive difficulty as the factors that make the four central
   listening processes easier or more difficult to perform.
8. TLU stands for Target Language Use.
9. What is to be assessed can refer to both a criterion that represents a correlation
   with some standard of success.
10. Psycholinguistic orientation examines the encoding processes in memory.
11. Metacognitive instruction refers to pedagogical procedures that enable
   learners to increase awareness of the listening process by developing richer
   metacognitive knowledge about themselves as listeners.
12. According to Willis (1996, 2005), a task is an activity in which learners use
   language for a communicative purpose in order to achieve an outcome.
13. Interactive listening requires learners to engage in face-to-face interactions
   where they often alternate between the roles of listener and speaker.
14. Vocabulary acquisition is an important goal of listening instruction.
15. What is the first goal of listening? Sociolinguistics is concerned with the
   relationship between language use and social factors.
16. In classroom language learning situations, the primary opportunity for
   collaborative conversations is learner–learner interaction.
17. Interview activities provide small-scale question and answer interaction.
18. It has been claimed that over 50 percent of the time that students spend
   functioning in a foreign language will be devoted to listening.
