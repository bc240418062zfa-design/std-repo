Define the term of efficiency and write its formula. Does it have a unit or
dimension? Briefly describe your answer with examples?            (3+1+1)

Definition
Useful energy output to the input is called efficiency. or
Fraction of energy of a system, performing useful work, is the efficiency of that
system.

Unit and Dimension:

Efficiency is a unit less quantity. It is the ratio of the energy
output to the energy input; hence it has no units and Dimension.
Question-2                                                                   (7 Marks)
A hair dryer releases 1 KJ of energy in half a minute from the effect of 0.1 kW power given
to the system. What is the maximum efficiency of a hair dryer? Is it possible that the
efficiency of hair dryer is 1006? Explain your answer.                            (5+1+1)
Answer:

Yes, it is possible that the efficiency of hair dryer is 100%. It depends upon what
portion of the electricity use is for the heating element, and what portion is used for
the fan. The Heating element is a resistive load, and is 100% efficient. The fan is
an inductive load, and is not 100% efficient. I imagine that in most hairdryers they
are not putting super high-efficiency motors in to drive the fans, but I also suspect
that the vast majority of the energy used will be by the heating coils, so your
overall efficiency will likely still be very high.

heat engine

In a heat engine, heat is transferred from a higher temperature level called source
to a lower temperature level called sink. Work is obtained during this process.

Carnot engine

It is an ideal heat engine whose efficiency is maximum. In Carnot engine, Carnot
cycle is used. In Carnot engine, ideal gas is the working substance and
all the dissipative forces are absent.

Steam engines and turbines operate on the Rankin cycle which has a
maximum Carnot efficiency of 63% for practical engines, with steam turbine
power plants able to achieve efficiency in the mid 40% range.
The efficiency of steam engines is primarily related to the steam temperature and
pressure and the number of stages or expansions. Steam engine efficiency
improved as the operating principles were discovered, which led to the
development
of the science of thermodynamics

In a steam engine, hot steam, usually supplied by a boiler, expands under pressure,
and part of the heat energy is converted into work. The remainder of the heat may
be
