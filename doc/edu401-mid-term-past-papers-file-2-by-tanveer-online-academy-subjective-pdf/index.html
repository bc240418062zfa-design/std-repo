Question: MDG brief note

Answer: MDGs (Millennium Development Goals) were a set of 8 global goals established by

the UN in 2000 to address issues like poverty, education, gender equality, and health by

2015.

Question: Note on universal literacy

Answer: Universal literacy aims to ensure that every individual has the ability to read and

write, fostering societal development and reducing inequalities.

Question: Three components of conflict

Answer: The three components of conflict are opposing interests, interdependence between

the parties, and interaction that results in disagreement.

Question: Three points on easy and accessible education

Answer:

   1. Free primary education

   2. Equal access regardless of gender or socioeconomic background

   3. Availability of educational resources and infrastructure

Question: Tips to manage conflict

Answer:

   1. Communicate openly

   2. Focus on the problem, not the person

   3. Find common ground

Question: Problem in literacy

Answer: Problems include lack of access to quality education, gender disparity, poverty, and

insufficient teaching resources.

Question: Difference between empathy and sympathy

Answer: Empathy involves understanding and sharing someone’s feelings, while sympathy

means feeling pity or sorrow for someone’s situation.

Question: Income role in equity issues in education

Answer: Income disparities affect access to quality education, leading to inequities where

lower-income students face more barriers in accessing resources and opportunities.

Question: Note on problem in literacy

Answer: Literacy challenges include poverty, insufficient funding, gender inequality,

inadequate infrastructure, and lack of trained teachers.

Question: Your opinion over discrimination religious issues in school textbooks

Answer: Discrimination in textbooks can foster intolerance and division. It's important for

education to promote inclusivity and respect for all religions.

Question: Write some tips for conflict management

Answer:

   1. Listen actively

   2. Stay calm and composed

   3. Collaborate to find solutions

Question: Medium of instruction

Answer: The medium of instruction is the language used to teach students in educational

settings, which can affect comprehension and learning outcomes.

Question: Implementation of free and accessible education

Answer: This requires government policy reforms, sufficient funding, and inclusive

educational strategies to remove barriers for marginalized groups.

Question: Govt ambiguity on madrassa reform

Answer: The government has faced challenges in madrassa reform due to political resistance,

unclear policies, and balancing religious and modern education.

Question: Components of conflict

Answer: Conflict typically consists of opposing views, emotional responses, and the actions

taken to address the disagreement.

Question: Managing tips of conflict

Answer:

   1. Stay neutral and objective

   2. Focus on resolution, not blame

   3. Seek compromise

Question: Uses of literacy

Answer: Literacy empowers individuals to engage in society, pursue education, improve

economic prospects, and participate in political and social life.

Question: Points for managing conflicts
