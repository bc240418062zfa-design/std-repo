What is Computer Science?
Computer science (CS) is the study of computers and algorithmic processes, including their
principles, their hardware and software designs, their applications, and their impact on
society. Computer Science is the discipline that seeks to build a scientific foundation for such
topics as:

      Hardware
      Software
      Programming
      Networks
      Graphics
      Robots
