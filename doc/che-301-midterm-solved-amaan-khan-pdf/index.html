Classification of gravimetric analysis

1. Precipitation gravimetry

2.      Volatilization gravimetry

3.      Electrogravimetry Precipitation Gravimetry

• The analyte is converted to a sparingly soluble precipitate which is then filtered, washed (free of
impurities), converted to a product of known composition by suitable heat treatment, and weighed.

• For example, a precipitation method for determining calcium in natural waters. The reactions are: 2NH3 +
H2C2O4 2NH4+ + C2O4-2 Ca+2(aq) + C2O4-2 (aq) CaC2O4 (s)

Precipitation Gravimetry (Continued)

• The precipitates are filtered using a weighed filtering crucible, then dried and ignited to converts the
precipitate to calcium oxide. The reaction is: CaC2O4 (s) CaO (s) + CO (g) + CO2

• After cooling, the crucible and precipitate are weighed, and the mass of calcium oxide is determined by
subtracting the known mass of the crucible. The calcium content of the sample is then computed.
