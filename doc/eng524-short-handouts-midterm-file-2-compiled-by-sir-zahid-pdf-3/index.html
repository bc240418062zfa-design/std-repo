Lecture-06

                             CDA and Context
Topic-01: Definition of Context

How does Blommaert define context?

Blommaert defines context as "the totality of conditions under which discourse is
being produced, circulated, and interpreted."

According to Van Dijk, what are the elements that make up context?

Van Dijk defines context as "the cognitive, social, political, cultural, and historical
environments of discourse."

What metaphors were used in early approaches to depict context?

Early approaches used metaphors such as "figure as text" and "ground as
context."

What are the components of context according to Halliday & Hasan (1985)?

Halliday & Hasan distinguish text, co-text, context of situation, and context of
culture.

How is the relationship between text and context described?

Text and context are described as being in a "mutually reflexive" relationship,
where each influences the other.

What is contextualization, and how is it related to meaning?

Contextualization is the process by which text and context come together to
create meaning.

According to van Dijk, what regulates contextualization?

Contextualization is regulated by the 'K-device,' which is the mutual knowledge
shared by the speaker and hearer.

How is the dynamic nature of context described?

Context is described as dynamic and changing as the discourse progresses.

What is intertextuality, and why is it important in CDS?

Intertextuality is the process whereby textual features of one text reappear in
another, and it is important in CDS because it links different texts together.

Why is identity considered an important contextual factor?

Identity is important because the image one has of oneself or others affects the
interpretation of actions and motivations.

Topic-02: Early Models of Context

How did Malinowski emphasize the role of context in language?

Malinowski stated that a word without linguistic context is meaningless, and
context is essential for interpretation.

 COMPILED BY SIR ZAHID                                                             2

What distinction did Firth and Halliday make regarding context?

Firth and Halliday distinguished between the context of situation and the broader
context of culture.

What is the Sapir-Whorf hypothesis, and how does it relate to context?

The Sapir-Whorf hypothesis suggests that the culture underlying a language
represents the context within which that language is interpreted.

What is Hymes's speaking mnemonic, and what are its components?

Hymes's speaking mnemonic includes components like setting, participants, ends,
act sequence, key, instrumentalities, norms, genre, and pragmatics.

How does Grice's cooperative principle relate to context in communication?

Grice's cooperative principle emphasizes that the interpretation of utterances is
an inferential process based on rational thought and context.

Topic-03: Recent Models of CDA

How does Conversation Analysis (CA) view context?

CA views context as constructed moment by moment through conversational
moves.

What are the two views of CA regarding context?

There is a strong view (Schegloff) that emphasizes the evidence of context within
the sequence of speaker turns and a weaker view (Drew and Heritage) that looks
at the institutional context.

What are the three dimensions of context in Systemic Functional Linguistics
(SFL)?

The three dimensions are field of discourse, tenor of discourse, and mode of
discourse.

How does SFL enable critical discourse analysts to relate language form to
context?

SFL allows analysts to identify form-function links and relate language form to
context.

What is the central aspect of communication in ethnographic approaches?

Social context is the central aspect of communication in ethnographic
approaches.

How does Cognitive Linguistics view context?

Cognitive Linguistics views context as mental models constructed in the cognitive
systems of interacting group members.

What does Corpus Linguistics study in the text/context interface?

Corpus Linguistics studies the use of language in a specific context compared to a
reference corpus to show variations in language use.

What is Multi-Modal Discourse Analysis (MMDA) concerned with?

MMDA is concerned with analyzing all semiotic systems, not just language, and
how they are deployed for ideological purposes.

Who pioneered MMDA, and what does it focus on?

MMDA was pioneered by Kress and van Leeuwen and focuses on the re-
contextualization of social practices for ideological purposes.

                           CDA and Genre (I)
Topic-01: Introduction of Genre

 COMPILED BY SIR ZAHID                                                            4

What are genres in language and discourse?

Genres are ways in which people 'get things done' through their use of spoken
and written discourse. They involve using language in specific ways based on
content, purpose, and audience.

How does the use of language in a genre relate to social and cultural context?

Language use in a genre is influenced by the social and cultural context in which it
is used. Different genres may emerge in response to changes in technology and
communication methods.

Who defines genre according to Martin (1984)?

Martin defines genre as 'a staged, goal-oriented, purposeful activity in which
speakers engage as members of our culture.'

What is the key concept in Miller's (1984) view of genre?

In Miller's view, a genre is defined by the action it is used to accomplish, rather
than by the form or content of discourse.
